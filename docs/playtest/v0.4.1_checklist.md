# v0.4.1 - your playtest checklist

For your own session with `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.1-windows.zip` (unzip, run `TheWaywardPath.exe`). Press **F8** whenever something
feels off - the report folder opens; `node tools/feedback_row.js` logs every report into `docs/playtest/feedback_log.md` afterwards.
Roughly in play order; the ones marked **(open question)** feed an inbox item.

## The first ten minutes (a new game)
- [ ] New Game -> the forest: 4 still captions and ~14 lines before the Path choice. Too much before you get to move?
- [ ] The Path choice (279 words over four tiles). Do you read them, or pick by the name? **(open question: inbox Daytime 2)**
      (The title still says "Choose your element" in this build; main says "Choose your Path".)
- [ ] The Forgotten Cave on the Path you picked: Necrocrat and Gourmand should feel fair now (sim: ~90%); Refusemancer is the hardest (82%).
- [ ] Crosspath: Maren's welcome, then "Make It Yours" - open the Deck Station, press **Save Deck** without changing anything, pick
      slot 1. It should complete (it used to stay greyed out). Is the slot picker clear?
- [ ] First opens right after arriving: C (character screen), Sable's cards, Tilly Tonic's items, the Deck Station - no hitch?
- [ ] The shop tips show on your first visit to Sable and Tilly Tonic (a fix that went into this build).

## Boss rules (new icons)
- [ ] A gatekeeper (Brick, Prudence, Chef, Moss): the battle HUD shows its rule icons where it has rules; hover reads well.
- [ ] Shiro Swindle (the giant-chest con man): after his lines, a "Before the fight" card lists The Oldest Trick and Smoke and Mirrors;
      the fight should be a real boss now (sim 48%; his rules were missing before).
- [ ] The Arena, Grand Bellows: Crowd Favourite + Heckling Crowd (1 damage to you at the start of each of his turns). Fair or cheap?

## The Gainlands as a Necrocrat (decision applied this morning)
