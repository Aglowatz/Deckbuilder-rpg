Emotion is averaged over 49 main-story items; 11 mechanical or comic lines are n/a. Humour is averaged only over lines with a joke (main: 27 for each version; side: A 32, B 33).

Overall:
- **Main story:** slight edge to B. B is clearly stronger on sincere beats (items 3, 9, 37, 47, 52, 57). A is stronger on comic timing and on Maren's and Primm's wit (items 14, 18, 24, 30).
- **Side content:** clear edge to A. B often splits A's jokes into clipped fragments (items 76, 78, 82, 99) or explains them (items 86, 95).
- **Pattern:** neither version is consistently better. B gains when it merges A's clipped lines into one breath, and loses when it chops A's flowing lines apart. The best final script would cherry-pick from both.
