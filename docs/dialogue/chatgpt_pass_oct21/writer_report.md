## Corrections and review notes

- The two Capital references dating the escape ten years ago now agree with the recent rescue; the captivity still lasts ten years.
- Story-line place names and titles use tokens, including signs and the ending. Barks and loading tips use no tokens.
- Overlong dungeon descriptions are shortened while retaining entry HP, healing locations and victory goals. The Old Well confirmation is one line.
- Loading tips now state one/two/all-Path progression, describe Tilly's skin rather than hair, and avoid promising a full heal at every dungeon entrance.
- Source `speaker`, `chars`, IDs and flags remain untouched, including legacy/misassigned speaker metadata. No external game validator or playable build was supplied, so this verifies the exchange files, not in-game rendering or routing.
- Fig Sly's two bark rows remain blank, despite appearing in the editable batch, because the brief locks black-market vendors. Asset credits remain intact.
