## Critique

### The Capital: gate, signs and narration
- This is the strongest sign writing in the game, and it already does what the brief asks for. The slogan undercuts itself on the last
  clause: "4. Visitors must have already been approved." (3025), "Having a height is, on balance, a violation." (3030), "Please queue for
  fun." (3041), "Trams must arrive on time. None did. All trams are suspended. Punctuality has improved considerably." (3087), "(It is not
  garbage. It is Behind.)" (3065), "Seventeen checkpoints in one street. The last checks whether you enjoyed the first sixteen." (3116),
  and the abandoned Form 1-A with "The authorization is valid. Nobody is here to enforce it." (3120). I left nearly all of them alone.
- The complaint box results (2958-2963) and the defacing pop-ups (2956-2957, 3360) are good small rewards. "He looks, for the first time,
  like a person." is the right size.
- The weak spots are consistency, not comedy:
  - 2944: says "nobody has run the wheels" in a district whose whole joke is that the wheels never stop. Suggested a fix.
  - 3137: the rift warning is an official sign, yet it admits "His hold is failing". Suggested moving the fact into a Wrinkle's scrawl under
    the official line. That keeps the fact and adds the Capital's core joke.
  - 3081: the Grave Row shaft sign contradicts itself. Suggested a fix.
  - 3036: spells "Showcase Quarter" literally in a story file. The token would change the token count, so the suggestion drops the name.
- Wheel narration (2975-2981) is good, but 2977 has "old enough to vote" (odd in a monarchy) and 2979 tells the player what to feel
  ("Children!"). Suggested quieter versions.
- Mr. Pell's burial (2986-2988) is right in tone. 2987's "A quiet tune accompanies the coffin" is the one vague image in an otherwise
  specific beat. I avoided "hum" in the suggestion because the castle's humming (2934, 3114) and the lullaby are clues.

### The Capital: the facade (citizens, doors, Plume, posters)
- The nine citizens are excellent and follow the bible exactly: approved, then off, then a sincere slip. "I have a permit for four more
  words. I wish it existed." (3299) is a perfect joke: the bakery sign says the bread is closed permanently, so the four words are both the
  joke and true. All the slips stay sincere, and I made no suggestion on any slip. One approved2 line got a tag (3322, greeting card).
- Doors (3329-3337) are good. Door 3 (3334-3335, the young woman painting the window "who has meant to write home for years") is a lovely
  quiet set-up for Isla. Keep it.
- Plume's broadcasts are on voice ("he loves you, and he is watching. These are the same statement." 3343; "Please report anyone who
  exists." 3350). Gaps: none of them carry the "escaped Royal Asset" hunt or a Primm reform, and 3345-3346 (exits for entering) repeat the
  exit guard's joke (3225). Suggested using those boxes for the hunt (3339) and for "tidy records" curdling into erased history (3345-3346),
  which sets up Wren the archivist.
- The posters (3351-3360) are perfect as written, including the "Face: yours" reveal. Keep them.

### The Capital: guards and Captain Spotless
- Spotless is the best-voiced antagonist in the chunk: HALT, "Stand at it", "retroactively, approved", "I have always supported this
  outcome. Please remember that exact wording." (3209). The tunnel version, "I cannot allow you to have found it." (3457), and the tired
  ending (3491-3492) give him the human under the armour. One fix: 3213 spends "eleven" on a gate tally ("eleven thousand visitors").
- "State your height. No: do not state it." now appears four times (3203-3204, bark 3422, S-CAP intro 3466). It's fine as a catchphrase,
  but the S-CAP intro (a boss intro, no tokens) could be fresher if the designer wants. I made no suggestion because players can reach the
  tunnels before the gate fight.
- The guards (3215-3229) are "Kafka by way of a doorman" exactly. One small payoff was suggested (3218).

### The Capital: the Crease (Wren, Kestrel, Hesper)
- Wren's intro and insights are clear, short and on-voice. "We have tea and grudges. The tea is better." works. Her return lines (3168-3169)
  are the thinnest. 3169 is the best place in the whole Capital for Primm's sincere early reforms, and right now the chunk never names
  one. Suggested a school-lunch line (High).
- Hesper is right as written ("Drink while it is still three things the Ministry dislikes."). Kestrel's chalk lines and "Two is rude"
  are good; no changes.

### The Capital: the four Path quests
- Widow Pell is gentle and specific. The 27-B/27-C loop at the permit office (2994-2999) is the right kind of funny, and her `.ready`
  beat is untouched. 3192 was a little generic ("I don't know what else to do"). Suggested her own self-deprecating patience line
  (from the bible), with no joke on the loss.
- Rollo's gasping rhythm works. His bark 3427 repeats the intro's "it was cardio" (3195). Suggested a Beefcake people-throwing line
  instead. 3260 (the reward) was unclear and now ends on "Remember ends?".
- Marlo is good; "Paste is food the way a form is a hug." stays. Fern is good; "Weeds. Moss. Children." stays.
- The insights are clear and land in the right order. Note that grief and spontaneity share the same Thursday 3:00-3:05 slot
  (3000, 3246). That's funny and consistent, so keep it.

### The Capital: lieutenant scenes (Quota, Clipwell, Plume ribbon, Old Hob)
- Quota is precise and funny ("Do you know what rest is? Unmeasured time."; "I will hate every minute. I will time every one."). Clipwell's
  "if nothing ever ends, then nobody ever has to cry again" is the best sincere line in the chunk.
- Fixes: Quota's "four hours and eleven minutes" (3376) spends the clue. Fern's "Peel from shell." (3392) reads as a typo.
- The Plume ribbon scene and Old Hob are canon and good; no suggestions.

### The Paste Works
- Small and solid: "Something in the third one is humming the national anthem." (3454), "a wheel of cheese with a ration number on it"
  (3486), Crumb's "I invented the paste because nobody could get it wrong. You can't burn paste." (3487) and the "...Yes, Chef." payoff.
- The one voice problem: Crumb opens with "HALT!" (3459), which is Spotless's catchphrase. Suggested "ATTENTION!".
- "This is not a drill, it is a soufflé!" (3474) is nonsense for a paste barrage, but it's harmless Crumb food-metaphor. Kept.

### The Old Service Tunnels
- Kestrel's quest text is clear. The grate pop-ups (3449-3450) and Spotless's tunnel scenes are good, and 3491-3492 ("All four Paths,
  shoulder to shoulder, and nobody measuring anybody") is the emotional point of the dungeon.
- The Four-Faction Junction blurb (3453) is about gym scrap and never says what the junction is. Suggested opening it with the four-Path
  build fact from story bible section 3.

## Flavour inventions

- Primm's first decree was a hot school lunch for every schoolchild (from the canon "school lunches" reform; only the "first decree"
  framing is new).
- Plume's broadcasts call the escaped prince "lost Ministry property" (consistent with the posters' "Royal Asset").
- A Plume broadcast says the public records "have never been tidier" and that people who remember things differently go to the Correction
  Ward. This is consistent with Wren keeping the true record.
- The wheel children wear regulation school caps.
- The dead in Grave Row "have asked" to use the Crease lifts (consistent with the Restless Dead).
- Fern sends Clipwell to sort "peel from shell" on Granny Gristle's compost rows. This is a reading of the existing line, not a new fact.

## Design notes

- **Speaker mislabel**: the Paste Works quest lines (3369-3374, `dungeon_content.json::quest.cap_paste_works.*`) are attributed to
  "Elder Maren" in the edit CSV. The giver is Chef Marlo (`zone_quest_definitions.gd`: `["final", "marlo"]`), and the lines are his
  (the onion in his apron, "'Some' is the best amount"). `tools/export_dialogue.js` defaults every `quest.*` key to Maren (lines 141 and
  229). Fix the export map, or reviewers may "correct" Marlo's voice toward Maren's.
- **Speaker mislabel 2**: `fx.wake_outside` (2940-2941) is a gate guard measuring you, but the CSV credits Nurse Hesper Dray.
- **Literal name in headings**: "THE SHOWCASE QUARTER" (3013 `prop.facade_arch`, 3035 `sign.facade_welcome::0`) is literal in a story
  file. The checker only catches mixed case. Changing it to a token would print "the Showcase Quarter" in lower case, so the designer
  should decide whether sign headings are exempt.
- **"His Perfection" in story_scenes.json** (3395, Plume's ribbon line) is literal but tested ("Must keep"). Leave it, but note it as
  the one tested exception.
- **"eleven" outside the clue**: besides the two suggestions (3213, 3376), the motif is used correctly by Citizen 4471. A grep of the other
  chunks for "eleven" used as a throwaway number could be worth doing.
- **Spelling**: "parlor" (3016 sign, 3237 Quest Log) vs "parlour" (Pell's dialogue and barks). Pick one; the rest of the game is British
  ("neighbourhood", "colour").
- **Spotless's height catchphrase** is used four times. If the S-CAP intro (3466) gets a fresh line later, it must work whether or not
  the gate fight happened first.
- The Capital names no Primm reform anywhere except the trams sign's joke (3087). Wren's return line is my one suggestion; Old Fern or
  Marlo could carry a second ("the paste started as a school lunch") if the designer wants it, but that may overlap other chunks.
- Not touched, on purpose: every citizen slip, Citizen 4471, Pell's `.ready`, Isla's barks, Primm's postgame barks, Old Hob, Fig Sly
  (locked), all Quest Log and battle UI rule text (clear and complete).
