# Critic notes, chunk 05: the Verdant Dump (Oct 31)

Suggestions: `docs/dialogue/critic_oct31/suggestions_05.csv` (20 rows, all pass `tools/dialogue_critic_check.js --check`).

## Critique

### Verdant Dump / gatekeeper (Moss Mulligan, rows 1-30)
- Strong. The corrupted-to-shy flip works: "Forever. Very nicely. Like mulch." (9), "Did I thorn at you?" (14, 16), the potted thorn that "isn't ready to meet the curtains" (2). The rule text and banners are clean.
- Missed story: nothing at the gate hints at who corrupted Moss (Clipwell, per canon). Row 17 is plain exposition ("The Rot took Archdruid Compostella first..."), so it is the cheapest place to plant "a very tidy lady with shears" before the mill reveal.
- Quest Log "Root Cause" (21-30) is clear and names every place. "Uproot Moss Mulligan" is a fine pun.

### Verdant Dump (rows 31-349)
- The signs are the best writing in the zone: "Every patch is fertilized by the same goat." (140), "The finest rust in the kingdom. Small batch. Very slow finish." (183), "It is thriving at us." (191), the raccoon's note about its retirement (205-206). Narration pop-ups are mostly sharp (41, 61-63, 74-75, 399).
- Repetition: "ambition" is the punchline of three junk descriptions (65, 153, 180); "rain and regret" appears twice in the Rotheart (397, 462); "Shop's open" opens two Gristle returns (222, 529), a greeting the voice bible retires.
- Owned puns used by the wrong owner: "cool head" (207, the Buffet golems) and "footing" (376, Coach Benchley).
- Brother Bramble is the zone's best-built NPC: worry, the compost outburst, then the eleven trees and the glowing young man (215-218) placed at the right temperature. His intro (211) is the one lecture: a textbook sentence about what Refusemancers do.
- The Gourmand rivalry ("garbage eaters") is never voiced in the zone. Only the debuff flavour (324) touches it. Two suggestions seed it (84 worker, 211 Bramble), from different angles.
- Clipwell's composting ban and her "tidy decrees" are never set up before the mill; the player meets the ban cold at row 379.
- Worker barks are good one-liners, but 83 and 84 are filler ("That's the Dump for you."). The freed pairs (492-500) are better than the corrupted ones.
- Billie Muckfoot is distinct and funny (224, 233-236, 531-533). Row 225's "They don't speak either" reads as a contradiction right after she says she speaks fluent goat.
- Thistlewick and Rhonda are distinct, on-voice and correctly leave quiz facts and sorting instructions alone.
- Old Hob (329-330, 546) is perfect: "I've taken up knitting." is a once-you-know joke.

### The Trash Panda Throne (rows 350-364)
- Good guilty-royalty comedy: "Or crouch. Crouching is also acceptable." (354), "audited it. Orally." (357), "Is it warm in here?" (361).
- Row 352 drops the pause from the canon catchphrase ("We sampled it." instead of "We... sampled it."), the first time it's heard.
- The kingdom excuse is used twice (361, 364). That's the running gag; it works because the second one adds "Do not point at the crumbs."

### The Rotheart (rows 365-491)
- Atmospheric and well paced: the breathing tunnel (366), "It is not entirely a wall." (415), "It does not recognize anything." (417). The Heart Roots and the seed tag (428-431) carry the clue cleanly. The sever cutscene (479-488) is the emotional peak of the zone and should not be touched.
- Clipwell's mill scene is excellent ("the right side is still growing in", "being honest"). But her mid-fight line (387) repeats her intro line (384) word for word in the same fight.
- Some map blurbs explain the mechanic flatly (405) or tell the player what to feel (370: "feels like it is watching you").
- The still captions (489, 491) use the "You see / You watch" camera narration that the Oct 21 review flagged.

### After freeing (rows 492-546)
- Warm and funny without stepping on the sincere beats: "She waters the weeds too. Says they had a rough decade." (497), "I'd forgotten that was a smell." (500), "Into a bucket. For the plants." (533), Gristle's "Found, not stolen. Twice." (545).
- Compostella's first freed line (534) misses her canon barn gag (she can't remember it; the barn remembers her) and reads as a plain apology.
- The twelfth tree (523-524) and "do not bury it in a jar" (538-539) are right as they are.

## Flavour inventions

- Gourmand food waste reaches the Dump ("half our garbage is their souffle", row 84) and the Buffet eats Refusemancer-grown carrots (row 211). This is consistent with the two Paths' services and their rivalry.
- The Dump's stairs grow extra steps overnight (row 83).
- A goat watches you eat at the harvest meal (row 39).
- Clipwell visited Moss in person ("a very tidy lady with shears came calling", row 17). Canon says Clipwell corrupted Moss; the visit is the only new detail.

## Design notes

- **Druid Sorrel** (rows 98-101, 168, 237): the voice bible's Billie entry says "no unseen Druid Sorrel". She is narrated as present at the centre bridge, so she is not strictly dangling. If she has no model or plate there, either add one or give the bridge lines to Billie or Bramble. I made no suggestion because that would rename her.
- **Clipwell's composting ban** is never set up before the mill. The story bible's "tidy decrees nailed to rotting trees" would make a good E-to-read sign in the Grange (for example "DECREE: COMPOSTING IS UNTIDY. BY ORDER OF THE ROYAL GROUNDSKEEPER." with a druid's scrawl underneath).
- **Direction**: the HUD (31) says the Rotheart is "to the north"; the Quest Log hint (30) says "at the bottom of the Dump". Both may be true on the terraced map, but one consistent landmark word would help.
- **Primm's grudging good reforms**: there is no Dump line for this yet. A worker could carry it once the wording is settled (for example the wells, using `{villain}`). It was left out here to keep the barks from all becoming lore.
- The Raccoon's catchphrase pause should match everywhere it appears (352 is fixed in the suggestions; 357 and the voice bible use the pause).
- Rows 84 and 211 both seed the Gourmand rivalry from different speakers. If the designer wants only one per zone, keep 84 (the bark) and take 211 without its last two sentences.
