# Build size policy

| Version | zip | pck | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| v0.3.0 | 1630 MB | 1500 MB | working art exported by mistake |
| v0.3.1 | 477 MB | 488 MB | import rules per folder (Oct 14) |
| v0.5.1 | 799 MB | ~883 MB | music, zone kits, stills and key art added since |
| **v0.5.2** | **600 MB** | **700 MB** | Size pass Nov 3 (`docs/perf/size_pass_nov3.md`) |

The budget warning is **750 MB** of pack (`tools/export_windows.sh` BUDGET_MB, `tools/build_audit.js`). Current numbers by
category: `docs/build_size.md` (regenerated by every export); per folder: `node tools/build_audit.js --folders 3`.

## How it works

- **Import rules per folder:** `tools/art_import_rules.json` is the single source of truth (first match wins).
  `node tools/apply_import_rules.js` rewrites the `.import` files; every `tools/import_*.sh` runs it after importing, and
  `tools/export_windows.sh` runs it again before exporting. New textures without a `.import` get the project default
  (lossy WebP, 0.85) until the rules run.
- **Right-sizing is import-time, not destructive:** `size_limit` and `quality` shrink the packed texture; the full-size source stays in
  the repo (and on Drive). To go back, raise the limit or quality in the rules file and re-import.
- **What is excluded from the export:** `export_presets.cfg` `exclude_filter` is regenerated by `tools/update_export_excludes.js`:
  tests, tools, docs, dev scenes, working folders, every `.glb/.gltf` nothing references (`tools/unused_assets.js`) and the files listed
  in `tools/export_unused.json` (checked by hand from `node tools/reference_graph.js --list`, which also models every loader that builds
  a path from an id).
- **Guard:** `bash tools/export_windows.sh` prints the payload by category, exports, checks the pack and the zip, and warns above 750 MB.

## Rules for new assets (Nov 3)

### Painted 2D art

- **Size:** about 1.0-1.3x the largest on-screen size at 1440p (`bash tools/texfit_run.sh` + `node tools/texfit_report.js` measure
  every screen). Full-screen paintings stay at their delivered 1536-1920 px: they are drawn at that size or larger.
- **Quality (lossy WebP):** 0.80 for full-screen paintings, stills, loading paintings, boards, maps, vendor stages and cards;
  0.85 for faces (portraits), world sprites and bestiary pictures; 0.88 for the title key art and the logo (drawn upscaled).
  Check a new kind of picture with `python tools/webp_quality_test.py <file>` (100 % crop sheet) before lowering it further.
- **Lossless:** text-bearing UI and icons up to 256 px.
- **BC7 for 2D only** for the UI screen backgrounds (shown everywhere). Stills and loading paintings are lossy: one is on screen at a
  time and the art cache releases it.
- **World sprites** keep their native canvas size (anchors and hat fits are in canvas pixels): quality only, never `size_limit`.

### World textures

- **Opaque tiling surfaces** (ground, path, wall, roof, trim, surf, TEX-, `*_albedo`): **BC1** (`"hq": false`) **with mipmaps**,
  1024 px. Check new surfaces for alpha first: a surface with real alpha keeps BC7 (add an explicit rule above the pattern ones).
- **Cut-outs** (trees, props, signs, decals, sheets, windows): BC7, 1024 px.
- **Normal maps** `_n`: BC7, 256 px. **Roughness** `_r`: BC1, 256 px. Painted-terrain `_nr`: BC7, 256 px.
- **Backdrop strips and skies:** lossy 0.9, 2048 px (drawn horizon-wide; smaller copies blur visibly).
- Basis Universal was tested and rejected (Godot 4.7.2 stored it uncompressed here, 3x BC7).

### Music and sound

- Music: **Vorbis q6 by design** (Gainlands rock Nov 3: the designer compared q3 with the old quality by ear and preferred q6, so the size
  pass's q3 re-encode was undone - every MUS-/STG- track re-rendered at q6 from its score). Do not lower it to save space; size work
  looks elsewhere first. 48 kHz stereo, -20 LUFS loops, -17 LUFS stings. `tools/music/lib/render.py` VORBIS_Q = 6 (WP_VORBIS_Q only for
  A/B tests); toolkit renders are imported verbatim (`deliver_part.sh` passes `--no-process`). A loop dropped in as a WAV goes through
  MusicDropIn at q6 too. `python tools/music/bitrate_check.py` compares levels.
- Stings and short effects stay WAV (QOA in the pack).

### Memory

- Big 2D art goes through `ArtCache` (`core/data/art_cache.gd`): load on demand, least-recently-used eviction under a budget per
  preset (Low-end 96, Low 160, Medium 256, High 512 MB decoded). A new picture loader should call `ArtCache.fetch(group, key, loader)`,
  never keep its own session-long dictionary. Per-area groups are forgotten in `AreaCaches.release`.
- Music streams: the registry keeps the last 8 (`AudioRegistry.STREAM_CACHE_SIZE`); OGG plays from its compressed packets.
- The Low-end preset never parks the world scene during a fight (`GraphicsQuality.parks_scene`).
