# Build size policy

v0.3.0 shipped at 1.63 GB (1.5 GB pck). v0.3.1 ships at **477 MB zip / 488 MB pck**. The budget warning is 800 MB.
Current numbers by category: `docs/build_size.md` (regenerated by every export). The v0.3.0 numbers: `docs/build_size_before_v0.3.0.md`.

| Category (packed MB) | v0.3.0 | v0.3.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| working art in `_art_inbox/` (was exported by mistake) | 582 | 0 |
| music and ambience (OGG, unchanged) | 218 | 218 |
| world textures (TEX/ENV/DEC, kit surfaces) | 91 | 115 |
| normal+roughness maps | 32 | 17 |
| card art | 267 | 29 |
| UI and icons | 53 | 26 |
| world test art (sprites, sky, backdrops) | 40 | 14 |
| portraits | 32 | 10 |
| key art / maps / battleboards / vendor screens | 110 | 23 |
| 3D models | 14 | 10 |
| everything else | 14 | 19 |
| **Total** | **1454** | **490** |

World textures grew on purpose: they are now BC7 VRAM textures (about 4x smaller in GPU memory than lossless, and faster to load).

## How it works

- **Import rules per folder:** `tools/art_import_rules.json` is the single source of truth (first match wins).
  `node tools/apply_import_rules.js` rewrites the `.import` files; every `tools/import_*.sh` runs it after importing, and
  `tools/export_windows.sh` runs it again before exporting. New textures without a `.import` get the project default
  (lossy WebP, 0.85) until the rules run.
- **Right-sizing is import-time, not destructive:** `size_limit` shrinks the packed texture; the full-size source stays in
  the repo. To re-export at higher quality raise the limit (or `quality`) in the rules file and re-import. `_art_source/`
  (gitignored, excluded from export) is still where card/portrait originals go per `docs/art/art_pipeline.md`.
- **What is excluded from the export:** `export_presets.cfg` `exclude_filter` is regenerated by
  `tools/update_export_excludes.js`: tests, tools, docs, marketing, gut, `scenes/dev`, `_art_inbox`, `_art_source`,
  `_asset_library`, screenshots, logs, builds and every `.glb/.gltf` that nothing references (`tools/unused_assets.js`).
- **Guard:** `bash tools/export_windows.sh` prints the estimated payload by category, exports, prints the real pck and
  zip size, and warns above 800 MB.

## Rules of thumb for new assets

- Painted 2D art (cards, portraits, backgrounds, maps, key art): lossy, sized to about 1.5x its largest on-screen size at 1080p.
- World/3D textures: VRAM compressed with mipmaps (surfaces) or without (kit pieces, to stay as crisp as before);
  normal+roughness maps (R,G normal, B roughness) at half the colour texture's resolution, never the Godot normal-map mode.
- Small UI pieces and icons (256 px and under): lossless.
- Music and ambience: OGG. WAV only for very short effects (stings are already QOA-compressed on import).
- World sprites keep their native canvas size: head anchors, hat fits and pivots are stored in canvas pixels
  (`world/sprites/sprite_anchors.gd`), so they are neither downscaled nor trimmed.

## Left on the table

- Music is 128 kbps stereo OGG (218 MB, 45% of the build). Re-encoding at 96 kbps would save about 55 MB; it was
  not done because re-encoding risks the loop seams and changes the sound.
- Ground textures at 1024 px as BC7 are 1.3 MB each (68 of them). Dropping the opaque ones to BC1 (4 bits per pixel)
  would halve that, at a small quality cost.
