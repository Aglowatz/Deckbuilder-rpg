# Card art audit (Nov 9)

**Summary.** I reviewed all 356 card images: 30 contact sheets, `cards_01` to `cards_30`, covering B, BAS, BR, C, G, GB, GBN, GN, GR, GRB, GRN, INF, JUNK, N, NB, NBR, NR, P4, R, RES and T. I took a closer look at about 40 cards: whole images at 520x780 for screening, and crops where a detail mattered. I flagged 7 cards. 3 are REDO (BAS-G, B-13, RES-IRON) and 4 are KEEP with a note (BAS-B, NBR-04, GR-04, N-06). None are FIX IN CODE. No card has a glaring problem. I found no wrong finger counts, extra limbs or melted faces that a casual player would notice. I checked the hands closely on B-02, B-16, P4-02, GB-01, B-09 and C-14, and all of them are fine. The cards with symbols on paper (C-12, C-25, G-21, N-06, N-11, N-15, N-24, N-32, P4-03) use simple shapes or icons, not fake lettering. They read as stylised, so I left them alone, except that N-06 gets a note. Flex appears on no card. B-21 "Flex on 'Em" is a pose, not the character, and the Beefcake lion on B-01 and B-23 is a generic gym-goer. T-24 (Elowen Vey) matches her portrait `assets/art/portraits/NPC-ELOWEN.webp`. A perceptual-hash check of all 356 files found no pixel-level duplicates. One pair repeats the same composition (B-13 and B-26, see below).

## Flagged cards

- BAS-G | Feastforge | Flat, vector-like cel shading with hard edges, unlike the painted style of the other cards. The whole image is a saturated cobalt blue (tiles, cabinets, walls), but Gourmand is the orange Path, so in a Gourmand deck it looks like it belongs to another Path. It also clashes with its siblings BAS-N and BAS-R. | noticeable | REDO | card_BAS-G_flatblue.jpg
- B-13 | Hulking Hauler | This is almost the same picture as B-26 "The Carpool": a shirtless strongman in a harness, running toward the viewer, pulling a wooden wagon full of cheering passengers on a dirt road. Both are Beefcake cards, so a player can have them in hand together. The card-list art note asks for exactly this scene, so the overlap comes from the brief. B-26 fits it better because of its name, so B-26 stays and B-13 gets new art. | noticeable | REDO | card_B-13_dupB-26.jpg (compare card_B-26_dupB-13.jpg)
- RES-IRON | Iron (resource) | A glossy photoreal 3D render of a barbell plate, with wet asphalt, orange fire and a factory haze. It has no painted brushwork, and it sits right next to the painted RES-GARBAGE and RES-INGREDIENT. Players see it often, as the Iron resource. | noticeable | REDO | card_RES-IRON_photoreal.jpg
- BAS-B | Powerhouse | The same flat vector-like style as BAS-G. The palette is right (Beefcake red and gold) and the content fits, so it stands out less than BAS-G. | minor | KEEP (redo together with BAS-G only if you want the four BAS cards to match) | card_BAS-B_flatstyle.jpg
- NBR-04 | Total Teardown | A flatter cel and comic look than its neighbours, with blocky buildings and a flat green wave. The content fits the brief (a demolition wave, a crew in hard hats made of a Beefcake, a skeleton and a raccoon). | minor | KEEP | card_NBR-04_flatstyle.jpg
- GR-04 | Food Fight | The two human chefs, centre-left and upper-right, have the same face, moustache and outfit, like a clone stamp. You only see it if you look for it. | minor | KEEP | card_GR-04_twinchefs.jpg
- N-06 | Notary Wraith | The scroll in the centre is covered in rows of pseudo-glyphs that look like lettering. It reads as arcane script for a wraith, so it is acceptable. Do not let a redo add real-looking fake text here. | minor | KEEP | card_N-06_scrolltext.jpg

## Duplicated art

- No pixel-level or near-pixel duplicates among the 356 card files. I checked with a 16x24 average hash; the closest pairs (NB-03, NR-05, T-04, RES-GARBAGE, GRB-01, R-32) are just similar sky and ground tones and are visibly different pictures.
- Same composition: **B-13 Hulking Hauler and B-26 The Carpool**. The fix is in the REDO above.
- Similar on purpose, fine: G-12 Meatloaf Mold and token T-05 (the meatloaf golem it creates). G-08, G-28 and tokens T-06 and T-07 (cake and pastry golems). B-05 Hamster Wheel Runner and INF-01 Hamster Wheel Power Plant (the same idea at two scales).
