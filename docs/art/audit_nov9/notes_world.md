# Art audit Nov 9 - world / UI art notes (zone kits, textures, bestiary, icons, cosmetics)

Reviewer pass over every contact sheet in `docs/art/audit_nov9/sheets/`: zone_kits_01..41 (603 images), textures_backdrops_01..11 (126), characters_bestiary_01..09 (125), icons_01..05 (195), cosmetics_01..03 (40) - **1,089 images**. Suspicious ones were cropped at full resolution (`crops/w_*.jpg`). For the props that ship in the game, the code was checked to see which sheet cells are actually placed, so a flaw on a cell nobody uses is marked as such.

**Summary.** The world art is in good shape. **There is no garbled pseudo-text anywhere in these 1,089 images**: every sign, banner, poster, form, notice board, label plate and door plaque is either pictorial or left blank. That covers the Buffet signs, the DNA plaques and forms, the Capital banners and signs, the Gainlands regime posters and the Dump signs. No melted objects, no impossible architecture, and no bad hands on any bestiary portrait or kit figure (spot-checked: Shiro, Ancient Spotter, Old Kingdom Automaton, Permits Zombie, the hamster-wheel runners, the fallen-statue child). Every icon reads at 32 px. Problems found: **0 glaring, 5 noticeable, 13 minor**. Verdicts: **1 REDO** (plus 1 optional cheap REDO), **5 FIX IN CODE**, and the rest KEEP with a note. By category:
- zone_kits: 4 noticeable (Capital pastel walls, DNA queue post, Dump window), 6 minor
- textures_backdrops: 1 noticeable (Capital pastel plaza tile seam), 1 minor
- characters_bestiary: 1 noticeable (Antechamber Sentry visor), 2 minor
- icons: 2 minor
- cosmetics: 1 minor (style note)

Style note (minor, KEEP): the kits do not share one rendering style. The Gainlands gym props are glossy mobile-game 3D renders. The DNA desk, stamp and rope are close to photoreal CG. The Buffet cloche and pot are mirror-polished. The Dump and Town kits are the most painterly. Each kit is consistent inside its own zone, so a casual player reads this as zone flavour, not as a mistake.
