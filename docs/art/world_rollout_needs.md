# World rollout: what each area still needs (for the art sheet)

What would make every area look better at the new low camera (pitch 20 degrees, the hero a painted sprite, tilt-shift, bloom, a real sky). The table below is also
written as tab-separated text in `docs/art/world_rollout_needs.tsv` (same rows, no markdown): open that file, select all, copy, and paste into your Google Sheet.

**How to read it**
- **ID** is a suggested ID in the same families as the World Look Test sheet (`SKY-`, `BD-`, `FOL-`, `TEX-`, `DEC-`, `PROP-`, `SPR-`); the game imports by ID, so keep them.
- **Image Description**: start with the named style lock from `data/source/world_look_test.csv` (Backdrop / Sky + Foliage / Texture / Sprite lock), then paste the text.
- **Priority**: 1 = looks unfinished without it, 2 = clearly better with it, 3 = polish.
- Today every area already works with the closest existing asset (the town's hills and treeline, tinted per zone, the six foliage sprites, the town water); the rows are what
  replaces those stand-ins.

## Areas, most needing first

| Rank | Area | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The D.N.A. | An indoor level with no inside: a flat dark gradient for a ceiling, the map's black skirt shows at the edge, nothing at the horizon but a dim fog. Every view is "a platform floating in the dark". Needs a ceiling, a far cubicle farm and a near wall ring. |
| 2 | The Capital, outside | The bleak wasteland is the most atmospheric area but its horizon is the town's lush treeline tinted grey, which is wrong for ash and rifts. Needs its own sky, a wall-and-wasteland backdrop and dead-land foliage. |
| 3 | The starting area | A dense 3D forest ring with a dusk sky that is mostly hidden by tree cones; the clearing reads, the world around it is generic. Needs a dusk sky card, a dark treeline layer, glow plants and the cave mouth as a hero piece. |
| 4 | The Verdant Dump | Golden-hour mood works but the distance is the town's hills tinted amber; no junk skyline, the water is the town's clean blue. |
| 5 | The Endless Buffet | Same: warm and readable, but the horizon should be cake towers and cutlery spires and the water gravy or syrup, not lake. |
| 6 | The Capital, inside | The clean idyll works as a colour mood but the sky and horizon are generic; the roofline and the castle on the hill are what should frame the plaza. |
| 7 | The Gainlands | Bright and sunny and nearly there; the floating islands are 3D close up and need distant sisters, the lake should be bluer. |
| 8 | Primm's castle grounds (the Castle Approach in the Capital) | The doors are a black void; the golden statues and sign stand on a plain plaza. Needs a painted facade, towers and a finished sky over it. |
| 9 | The Path-ology Lab | Only a dungeon diorama (and the hatch in the forest): not part of the overworld camera yet. Needs the hatch as a prop and floor / tank pieces if it ever becomes walkable. |
| 10 | The main town | The reference look: sky, three rings, landmarks, water, foam and foliage all painted. Only variety and the corner cases are left. |
