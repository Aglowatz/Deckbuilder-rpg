# The Path-ology Lab: no zone kit

The Path-ology Lab is not a walkable area (Art Bible G-07, `docs/design/subarea_removal.md`, SR-3): it is the postgame node-map dungeon opened by the hatch in the forest clearing. The kit list that was here (white tile and clinical surfaces, tank and locker cut-outs, the LAB-HERO-* concepts of the hatch yard, the decontamination arch, the specimen wall, the break room and the four tanks) dressed five walkable antechamber scenes that no longer exist, so it was removed. The one piece that stands in a main area, the hidden hatch in the forest clearing, is a forest asset (`starting_forest.md`).

Art the Lab still needs: the painted node map and battleboard (MAP-LAB / BB-LAB are placeholders today), the loading picture, and the node-map backdrop (`DungeonBackdrop` theme `lab`, `data/zone_styles/pathology_lab.json`), in the look of `docs/art/art_bible.md` (no sun, white tile cracked by roots, four Path-colour tank glows, one warm thing per room).
