# Primm's Castle: no zone kit

Primm's Castle is not a walkable area (Art Bible G-07, `docs/design/subarea_removal.md`, SR-3): it is the Capital's main dungeon, a node map opened by the castle door inside the wall. The kit list that was here (marble and gold surfaces, mirror and topiary cut-outs, the CAST-HERO-* concepts of the courtyard, colonnade, stair and crooked rose) dressed four walkable castle-grounds scenes that no longer exist, so it was removed; nothing in it is reusable in a main zone except the palace facade, which the Capital lists itself (CAPI-HERO-PALACE-FACADE, `capital_inside.md`).

Art the castle still needs: the painted node map (MAP-*), the battleboard (BB-*) and the loading picture, through the map pipeline (`bash tools/import_maps.sh`, `docs/design/dungeons`), in the look of `docs/art/art_bible.md` (cold crisp morning, marble and gold, mirrors, colonnades).
