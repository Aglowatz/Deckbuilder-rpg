- **Second tram shed** (`CapitalLayout.TRAM_SHED_TWIN`): decorative twin in the yards' north-west block, the mirror's span inside the route
  clearances (x 86-95.5, 4 m deep: the west boulevard runs past its south side). Its block held the big transit rift, which moved to the
  yards' east corner (114, 61.5; radius 3.2 -> 2.6, still a big rift) - the only open ground left in the yards (probed: the side plaza's statue
  pair fills the wheels' yard); it keeps 2.4 m from the power-cable spot and 0.5 m from every route.
- **Grave Row and the palace approach**: no striped lawns - checker paving (TILE floors; the painted lawn mask treats both as paving like the
  Reek) with formal parterres on it (`CapitalDressing._parterre`: a white planter of roses, a clipped cone in a gold urn, a white path border
  with its gold line). Grave Row: 5 (3 from the garden grid + its two hand spots); the approach: 2 compact ones on the band before the plaza
  (its house grid leaves no 4 m of open ground; the band behind is the forecourt route's clearance). Headstones, the permit office, the parlour,
  the family plot, the portraits, posters and loudspeakers stay where they were.
