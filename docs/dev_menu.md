# Dev Menu (F10)

A debug-only cheat panel for testing. **F10** opens and closes it anywhere: the title, the forest, Crosspath, zones, the Capital,
dungeon maps, menus and battles. Escape also closes it. The world pauses while it is open. Every action shows a toast ("[Dev] ...") and
goes through the game's real systems (Session, the quest log, flags, the scene manager), so what it does is what play would do.

- **Debug builds only.** `SceneManager` installs it with `DevMenu.install(get_tree())`, which checks `DevTools.menu_enabled()` (the
  same gate as the Dev Shrine: `OS.is_debug_build()`, or the project setting `deckbuilder/dev_shrine_enabled`). A release export never
  creates it. Tested in `tests/test_dev_menu.gd` and checked by `tools/run_release_smoke_exported.sh`.
- Code: `app/dev_actions.gd` (`DevActions`: every action, usable from tests and smokes), `ui/dev/dev_menu.gd` (the panel),
  `ui/dev/dev_free_camera.gd`, `ui/dev/dev_walk_overlay.gd`. Hooks: `BattleScreen.dev_end/dev_refresh`, `BattleContext.dev_battle`,
  `Session.deck_capacity` (flag `dev_all_deck_slots`).
- Live check: `bash tools/run_dev_menu_smoke.sh` (opens every tab, travels, wins a menu fight, jumps to a boss node, round-trips a snapshot).

## Tabs

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Travel** | Crosspath, the starting forest, the Undercroft arena (opens the arena screen); each Path zone (a fresh visit from town, gatekeeper locks ignored); the Capital outside the gate, inside the gate (the Crease; sets the gate-open, inside and hub-known flags) and Primm's castle (D-PC); the Iron Cave (S-IRON); **any dungeon** from the dungeon list (main, side, the Forgotten Cave, the Lab) at its first node, at any node, or at the boss. "Go to node" enters through the dungeon's real entry and clears the route up to that node, so the party stands next to it. |
| **Player** | Set level 1-30, either through the real level-ups (rewards, the reward track, the level-up box; up only) or **Silent** (XP and the level's stats only; can go down); +100 / +1000 / +10000 gold; packs (one of every pack, or any pack x N); **add any card** by searching its name or id (copy caps apply unless **Ignore caps** is ticked: then the copy is kept past the cap with no essence conversion); any equipment piece; any item; unlock all ten deck slots; full heal; set HP (in a battle: the hero's battle HP; in a zone or dungeon: the run's HP; the town is always full). |
| **Progress** | Per Path zone: gatekeeper beaten, zone freed (= its main dungeon's boss beaten; on uses the real `complete_zone`, off corrupts it again); Primm defeated (on = the real `unlock_postgame`). **Any quest's step**: pick a quest (every quest, including the early-game chain and the Old Well), pick a step, Set: earlier steps' conditions are met (flags set, counters raised, required quests filed), later ones are un-met; a step reward set by hand is marked paid, never paid out; "Every step done" completes a quest with no turn-in through the real quest system. **This area**: reveal the map, open all hidden chests (real rewards), reset the area (its hidden chests close, its map fog returns, its enemies come back; Crosspath's outskirts roamers wake up; the scene reloads; flags are left alone). |
| **Battle** | **Auto-win all encounters** (Fixes Oct 25): every battle that starts - zone, dungeon, boss, arena, scripted - is won at once through the real result (rewards, XP, quest progress, story triggers, post-battle dialogue); an AUTO-WIN badge shows at the top of the screen while it is on (`DevActions.auto_win_enabled`, never in a release build); **Ctrl+Shift+W** wins the current battle. Start any encounter of the balance sim's catalog (`EncounterCatalog`: tutorial, roamers, elites, gatekeepers, every dungeon node and boss, the arena; search by name or group such as `boss` or `zone:beefcake`) with **your deck or any Path's starting deck**; it pays nothing and returns to Crosspath. In a battle: Win now, Lose now (the real result panel follows), draw 1 / 3, +1 / +3 energy (floating energy, gone at the end of the turn), +1 of each resource (Iron, Red Tape, Contract, Ingredient, Garbage), show the enemy hand. |
| **View** | FPS / frame-time overlay (bottom left); time scale 0.5x / 1x / 2x / 4x (re-applied after the final blow's slow motion); hide the HUD for screenshots (every CanvasLayer of the scene); **free camera** (the world pauses; WASD moves, Q/E down/up, Shift faster, hold the right mouse button to look; untick to hand the view back); **walkable overlay** (green where the hero may stand, red where not, 14 m round the hero, from the area's `WalkableArea`). These keep running after the menu closes. |
| **Saves** | Named snapshots ("before Rotheart") saved to `user://dev_saves/<name>.json`, apart from the five save slots; Load resumes where the snapshot was taken (the same path as loading a slot); Delete. |
