# Capital inner city, critic pass c3 (Nov 1)

Target: `docs/art/paintovers/PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY.png`. Shots: `docs/art/capital_rebuild_nov1/c3/*.jpg` (13 shots). Scored strictly: 8 = matches the target, 10 = as good or better.

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Grandeur | 5 | `ref` and `boulevard` read as a real capital: 5-6 storey cream-and-gold townhouse rows, a broad axis, gilded giants on plinths. But the castle at the end (`palace_approach`, `ref_play`) is a flat cream wall with one arch and a staircase, barely taller than the townhouses; `overview` and `gate_plaza` show a flat-topped city with nothing rising above the parapets. |
| 2. Symmetry / perfection | 6 | Plaza and boulevard are properly mirrored (`fountain`, `ref_play`, `palace_approach`: paired statues, paired topiary, paired lamps, identical citizens in white). Broken by the right side of `ref` and `gate_plaza`: a lawn with benches dropped at odd angles, a street with pink-roofed low houses, a manhole cover in the checker, face-signboards on easels at the boulevard sides (`palace_approach`); inner districts are cottages/pavilions/mausolea with no mirroring at all. |
| 3. Building height + detail | 6 | `palace_approach` townhouses: five storeys, tall arched blue windows with gold surrounds, balconies with flower boxes, string courses; `boulevard` rows are consistent. Missing everything above the cornice: every house ends in a flat cream parapet, no pastel slate roofs, no pinnacles, no pediments or sun medallions on the houses; facades read as a repeated flat texture (windows painted on, no depth) in `ref` and `gate_plaza`. |
| 4. Towers / skyline | 2 | No spire anywhere in the set. The plaza "towers" (`ref_play`, `fountain`, `gate_plaza`) are plain cylinders with one gold band and a blank cap; the castle (`palace_approach`, `ref_mid`) is a single flat facade with two tiny blue-roofed corner turrets; no sky is visible in any shot, so there is no stepping-up silhouette at all. |
| 5. Statues / fountain | 7 | Strongest category. `boulevard`: huge gilded figure on a laurel-wreathed white plinth, mirrored gilded pairs on sun-medallion plinths on both sides; `fountain`/`ref_mid`: tiered white-and-gold basin with blue water and a gold statue on top, flower boxes on the rim. Falls short on finish: the water is a flat blue fill with no sheen or spray, the central statue is a different (taller, single) composition rather than the target's white figure on a three-tier bowl, and the gilded statues are one repeated model. |
| 6. Gardens | 6 | `boulevard`, `fountain`: cone topiary in white-and-gold planters surrounded by pink rose beds in matching pairs; `palace_approach`: continuous rose planters on grass strips both sides; lawn parterres with gold grid lines in `gate_plaza`. Weaknesses: planters are plain boxes rather than gold urns, the lawns are flat saturated green with no bed pattern, `grave_row` lawns are mower-striped turf, and `gate_plaza`/`ref` right side is a bare lawn with benches, not a parterre. |
| 7. Paving | 7 | Pink/sage/cream checker everywhere, gold concentric rings around the fountain and gold lines on the boulevard axis (`overview`, `fountain`) match the target's plan. Missing the white-and-gold border bands at plaza edges and along building bases, no sheen or tile joints (tiles are large flat colour blocks), and in `inner_gate`/`overview` the checker stops dead against dark purple-cracked cobbles with no threshold. |
| 8. Lighting / atmosphere | 4 | Warm gold key and soft shadows are right (`palace_approach` character shadow). Everything else is off: the whole frame is overexposed and washed to cream with a milky haze on near objects (`ref`, `gate_plaza`, `transit_yards`), colours are desaturated against the target's crisp pastels, heavy depth-of-field blurs the townhouses in `gate_plaza`/`ref_mid`, visible grain/noise texture on every surface, no sky or mountain backdrop in any shot, and the lamp globes are unlit grey or render as solid black spheres (right side of `palace_approach`, left-centre of `ref`), posts are brown rather than pink. |
| 9. Overall quality | 5 | The plaza/boulevard set (`boulevard`, `fountain`, `palace_approach`) would pass as a good indie game at a glance. The set as a whole does not: blown-out low-contrast image, blurry and grainy, black lamp globes, and the three inner districts (`hungry_quarter`: half-timbered cottages with pink tile roofs and striped awnings; `grave_row`: blue-roofed sheds and a long blank wall; `the_reek`: low pink-capped walls and bins) look like a different, cheaper game sitting inside the capital's walls. |

## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. **Build the skyline (criteria 4, 1, 8).** Behind the palace facade at the end of the boulevard, add a stepped castle mass: three receding tiers of white-and-gold wall, each tier carrying 4-6 slender towers with pointed pastel blue/pink spires and gold finials, the tallest on the centre axis, so it is visible above the stairs from `ref`, `palace_approach` and `ref_play`. Give the four cylindrical plaza towers (`ref_play`, `fountain`) a pointed pastel spire and gold cap each. Then make sure the sky is actually in frame: lower the far-end wall height or pitch the standard cameras up a few degrees so blue sky, a mountain backdrop and a distance-haze band sit behind the spires.

2. **Fix the exposure and the lamps (criteria 8, 9).** Bring exposure down about one stop and restore saturation so the pink/sage/cream read as pastels instead of cream-on-cream; restrict haze to distance (none on objects within the plaza); cut the depth-of-field radius so the townhouse rows stay sharp in `gate_plaza`/`ref_mid`; remove the grain overlay. Make every lamp globe a warm-white emissive sphere with a glow sprite and recolour the posts pink; find and fix the globes that render as solid black (right lane of `palace_approach`, left-centre `ref`).

3. **Finish the townhouses and extend them into the inner districts (criteria 3, 2, 1, 9).** Cap every townhouse with a pastel slate roof (pink, blue or sage, mirrored across the axis) with gold pinnacles at the corners and a pediment carrying a sun medallion over the centre bay; add balcony depth under the window boxes so the facades stop reading as a flat texture. In `hungry_quarter`, `grave_row` and `the_reek`, replace the cottages, blue-roofed sheds and low pink walls with 3-4 storey versions of the same cream-and-gold townhouse module in mirrored pairs along each district's axis, keeping only the broken props (sludge, bins, ghosts, wheels) as the district's identity. Also remove the random elements on the plaza's right side (`ref`, `gate_plaza`): the bench lawn, the angled street of pink-roofed houses, the manhole covers and the easel signboards.
