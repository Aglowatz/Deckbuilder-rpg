## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. **Lighting and sky (raises 8, 9, 1).** Cut the fog/exposure on the inner-city camera so the cream plaster, pastel roofs and gold trim keep their saturation and the figures throw a soft but visible shadow; put a blue sky with a hazed mountain line above the castle wall so it is visible in the `ref` and `palace_approach` framings; replace the lamp posts with pink posts carrying warm glowing globes (and fix the black-disc globes on the right side of `palace_approach`).
2. **Spires and a stepped castle (raises 4, 1).** Behind the boulevard end wall, add a stepped white-and-gold castle mass with three tiers of slender towers topped by blue and pink pointed spires; put a slender spire tower at each end of every townhouse row and at the four plaza corners, and turn the two blank gate piers in `ref_mid` / `inner_gate` / `overview` into ornate towers with sun medallions, gold trim and spires so every frame has spires stepping up toward the castle.
3. **Taller, dressed townhouses and formal gardens (raises 3, 6, 2).** Raise the boulevard townhouses to five storeys with a balcony and flower box on every window line, a pediment with a sun medallion on each bay and a pastel slate roof with pinnacles; in the districts keep the same facade kit at the same height instead of the two-storey cottages. Replace the plain lawns in `gate_plaza` and `grave_row` with parterres (low hedge patterns, rose beds in white planters, cone topiary in gold urns at the corners) and remove the loose benches, planters and manhole from the plaza paving.
