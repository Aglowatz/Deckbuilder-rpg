# Critic 3 - the story and the writing (Nov 17)

Read `docs/review/critic_nov17_common.md` first (the rules, the commands, the report). Then this brief. Nothing else - except the one
file named below.

**Your lens:** the main story and its writing: the emotional arc, the characters' voices, the clarity, the jokes, the payoffs. Judge it
like a narrative reviewer who loved Inscryption's mystery, Sea of Stars' warmth and Octopath's character chapters. Blind readers rated a
recent rewrite about even with the old text; find out whether the story works, where it does not, and which single lines are confusing
or wrong.

**Budget: 6 runs, 70 minutes** (most of the sitting is reading). Lens name: `story`. Report: `docs/review/critic_nov17/03_story.md`.

## What to read and play

1. **Read the main story in order:** `C:\Dev\Deckbuilder-rpg\docs\review\critic_nov17\story_script.md` (you may read this file). It is
   every cutscene, memory, ending beat and main-cast conversation, exported from the game in the order a player meets them. Read all of
   it. Note the line (quote it, with its speaker and the section) wherever something is confusing, contradicts an earlier line, repeats,
   falls flat, or is out of voice.
2. **Play a few scenes to see them as a player does** (text against pictures, pacing, portraits):
   - the opening: a new game from the title (`button New Game` and read through the forest scene, about two runs);
   - a memory with Elder Maren: `dev load bot_refusemancer_town_after_refusemancer`, walk to Maren's home (map: "Elder Maren"), `key E`,
     and read the memory scene slowly, shooting each picture;
   - the ending: `dev load c13end` (the moment before the last boss falls) if it loads; otherwise skip it and say so.

## The story facts the game must not contradict

- The hero is **the Wanderer**, kept ten years in a cell with **bars on every side** in the Path-ology Lab. His real name is **Prince
  Tessar Wayweaver**; nobody says "Tessar" before the reveal (Maren's fourth memory, or Primm in his castle).
- **Elder Maren** is an old tortoise who once tutored the royal children at the castle. She never built the cell. **Her eyes are failing**,
  which is why she does not know the Wanderer until the fourth memory.
- **Primm** ("His Perfection") rules from the Capital and wants everything the same; his emblem, a smiling face, belongs on the regime's
  banners only.

## Your report also has

- **The arc in five sentences:** what the story is, as you understood it.
- **Characters:** the three best-written characters and the three weakest, one line each with a quoted line.
- **Lines to fix:** up to 15 single lines that are confusing, contradictory or wrong, each with its speaker, section, the line quoted,
  what is wrong, and a suggested replacement (one line, same length or shorter, same voice). These count as your "top 10 problems"
  only if they are among the ten worst things; structural problems (pacing, payoffs, clarity of the mystery) come first.
