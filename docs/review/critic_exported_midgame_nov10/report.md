# Midgame critic, exported build v1.0.0-rc3 (Nov 10): the Endless Buffet to the Test Kitchen boss

(Handed back by the no-context critic; its file write was refused in its sandbox, so the report is saved here verbatim. Brief:
`docs/review/critic_exported_midgame_nov10_brief.md`. Scripts: `docs/review/play_scripts/xm_01.txt` to `xm_33.txt`. Screenshots:

- **Scores:** clarity **7/10** (last night 5), fun **6/10** (last night 4), polish **7/10**.
- **Runs / game time:** 33 runs, 2450.8 s of game time = **40.8 min**. One game at a time; no SCRIPT ERROR in any of the 33 game logs.
- `dev travel dungeon D-TTK` used from run 24 and `D-TTK -2` from run 28, after 23.3 min of game time on foot (reached the door's doorstep at 18.3 min; two further approaches were each eaten by the same 11-turn roaming fight beside the door).

## Top 10 problems (ranked)

1. **The main quest never mentions the gate or the side quest that opens it.** `xm17_quests.png`. Expected: after "Shut down the Test Kitchen", "Sir Loin's gate on the way wants Old Meatloaf mended - see Basil". Shown: "Where to go: The Test Kitchen, at the far end of the halls". Only the sign at the gate (60 m and a river away) says it. Fix: add that clause to the step's "Where to go" and tag Mend the Meatloaf "(needed for the gate)" in the Side tab.
2. **A roaming Gelatin Sentinel camps the Test Kitchen door.** `xm21_n3.png`, `xm22_door.png`, `xm23_after_pilot.png`. Expected: reach the door and press E. Shown: an 11-turn, 115 s fight on both approaches (the second won on the run's last second, reward never collected). Fix: keep roamers 10 m from dungeon doors and gates, or let the door's E win over a roamer contact within 3 m.
3. **Every fight is 9-11 turns, 100-120 s at 1x.** `xm22_after_pilot.png` (turn 7, sentinel still 10/16), `xm27_after_pilot.png` ("in 9 turns"), `xm32_end.png` (turn 9, 2 HP left). Expected: roamer/first room done in 5-6 turns. Fix: roamer/first-room HP 15-16 -> ~10, or default Speed 2x after 10 wins.
4. **The boss's token swarm makes the table unreadable.** `xm29_after_pilot.png`: 12 enemy cards overlapping, 7 identical Sashimi Weenies, names truncated ("SASHIMI WEE", "KITCHEN POR"). Fix: stack identical tokens with a count ("Sashimi Weenie x7") once the row exceeds 8.
5. **"Setting Up" is an unlabeled hourglass icon.** `xm29_enemy_turn.png` (no text anywhere), `xm31_icon_hover1.png` (hover: "Its units do not attack in turns 1-2; they can attack from turn 3. Watch what it builds."). Expected: a one-line banner on turn 1. Fix: show each boss-only rule as a 4 s toast at fight start and name the icons on the pre-fight card.
6. **Toasts stay 20-30 s and stack.** `xm16_meatloaf.png` ("You already took the one lying here..." still up 20 s and three conversations later), `xm20_basil.png` ("Old Meatloaf is mended!" for 28 s). Fix: 4 s timeout, cleared by any dialogue or area change.
7. **The interact prompt prefers plaques/props to the quest NPC.** `xm10_a.png` ("[E] Read the plaque" with Tarragon 2.8 m away and named on screen), `xm09_a.png` ("[E] Look him over"). Fix: quest-available NPCs get a 3 m range and top priority in the pick.
8. **Walking costs HP you cannot heal after battles.** `xm02_walk1.png` (BONK -2 HP three seconds after arriving), `xm17_n2.png` ("Glug!" -1 per river touch, x4), `xm06_battle_start.png` (gravy -1). 4-6 HP lost per trip before any fight. Fix: make the meatball a visible rolling object; river pushes back without damage on first touch.
9. **The "Point me to..." list reorders between opens.** Run 2 row 1 = Tarragon; run 7 the same row set the pointer to Mirepoix (`xm07_d.png` label). Fix: fixed order (goals, then places alphabetically) and highlight the current target row.
10. **Stale favour toast, and Escape over pop-ups.** `xm12_m4.png`: "Basil has a favour to ask. Talk again." remains after accepting. `xm33_west.png`: Escape over a Quest Complete card opens the Pause menu on top and E then closes nothing. Fix: clear the toast on accept; Escape closes the top pop-up first, pauses second.

## 1. How I played

- 33 runs, each 35-172 s, 2450.8 s of game time in all (40.8 min). No run started while another was open.
- Start: `dev load bot_refusemancer_enter_gourmand` (level 14, 22 HP, 1095 gold). Every later run loaded the previous run's `dev save`; each load put the hero back at the Buffet entrance (50, 86) with full HP, so every run is a fresh walk from the pantry door. Quest state, gold and items carried over.
- Where I got to: Tarragon (favour: Pantry Run), Basil (favour: Mend the Meatloaf, then The Golden Reservation), salt, basil leaf, truffle, honey spot, the hidden chest, Old Meatloaf mended, Sir Loin's gate opened, Layer-Cake Town, the Test Kitchen's doorstep (8 m from the door) at 18.3 min of game time. Two more runs to the door were both intercepted by the roaming Gelatin Sentinel standing beside it (an 11-turn fight that filled the whole run).
- `dev travel dungeon D-TTK` from run 24 and `dev travel dungeon D-TTK -2` from run 28, after 23.3 min of game time on foot. Inside: Prep Station Sous-Chef (won in 9 turns), Frozen Line Cook (enemy at 2 HP when the run ended), the Doppelganger three times (lost in 7 turns once; two runs ended at turns 6-8 with the boss at 12 HP).
- Fights: first turn by hand, then `pilot`. No `dev autowin`, no `dev heal`, none of the forbidden commands.

## 2. Scores (last night: clarity 5, fun 4)

- **Clarity 7/10** (up from 5). What moved: the gate now tells you exactly what opens it (sign: "Admission: a letter from Basil, proving Old Meatloaf has been mended", `xm19_g3.png`), Basil says "Sir Loin will open his gate for you now" when you hand in, the map's "Point me to..." draws a dotted route and labels the arrow, and the Defeat card explains what beat you. What did not move: the main quest's own text ("Shut down the Test Kitchen - Where to go: The Test Kitchen, at the far end of the halls", `xm17_quests.png`) never mentions the gate or that a *side* quest is the key, and the boss's "Setting Up" rule is an unlabeled hourglass you only read by hovering.
- **Fun 6/10** (up from 4). The hub is genuinely charming: bounce pads that fling you onto the Butter Butte, Old Meatloaf's note, the Lazy Susan river crossing, a quiz master, a hidden chest, good jokes in every line. What holds it down: every fight is 9-11 turns and 100-120 s at 1x, a roamer camps the dungeon door and costs 2 minutes per approach, the boss floods the table with 12 overlapping Sashimi Weenies, and the zone chips 1-2 HP off you for walking.
- **Polish 7/10.** The art (hub, map, dungeon map, boss cut-outs) is excellent and nothing crashed or errored. The rough edges are small and repeated: toasts that stay 20-30 s and stack, a "has a favour to ask" toast that survives accepting the favour, Escape opening the pause menu on top of a dialogue or reward card, the interact prompt preferring plaques to quest NPCs, and the route line drawn straight through the Soup Fountain, which blocks the hero.

## 3. Finding the way

Arrival to the Test Kitchen's door: 18.3 min of game time to stand 8 m from it (run 21), 23.3 min when I gave up on getting through the door on foot. A human would be faster (my walks are blind 1-6 s holds); the sequence itself is about 6 minutes of necessary content: Tarragon, three pickups, Basil, mend, letter, gate.

1. **Arrival (0:00, `xm01_arrive.png`).** HUD tracker: "» Help the kitchen: finish a favour in the Endless Buffet". `J`: "Where to go: Basil, Sous-Chef Tarragon or Madame Mirepoix" (`xm01_quests.png`). `M` shows the three with Q markers and a long "Point me to..." list (`xm01_pointme.png`) including "The Test Kitchen (Dungeon entrance, usually level 14)" and three golem gates by name. Good start.

I walked past him twice (`xm02_walk3.png`: the arrow points back down the screen). He stands 18 m from the entrance beside the Rift Express beam, which draws the eye instead. Confusion: in run 7 the same list row now pointed at Mirepoix (`xm07_d.png`) - the list reorders between opens.

## 4. Battles

- **Hand numbers.** "2/3" under a unit is attack/defense, legible at hand size; the energy cost is a small icon in the card's top-right corner I could not read until I clicked a card and got "Needs 4 energy; you have 0 (each ready Infrastructure gives 1 a turn)" (`xm22_played.png`). That sentence taught me the economy; it should be visible before the first failed click. Right-click preview is good: big card, text, keyword glossary, and for enemy units "In this fight: 2/3 (printed 1/1)" with each bonus explained (`xm31_enemy_preview.png`, `xm26_preview.png`).
- **"Turn N" and "Your turn".** Clear: "Turn 1 / Your turn" with Start-Main-Combat-Main 2-End highlighted and a sentence per phase (`xm05_e6.png`). The opening-hand card ("2 infrastructure, 4 other cards in your 6", Keep / Mulligan (free)) is a good nudge. During the enemy's turn I saw log lines ("The Doppelganger is thinking...", "Gelatin Sentinel plays Sous Chef", "attacks with Sashimi Weenie and 3 more") rather than a big "Enemy's turn" label; fine.
- **Pre-fight card.** Each room opens with "Before the fight: <enemy>" listing the rules (`xm26_room1.png`). "Dress Code Violation: Refusemancer units on both sides get -1 attack" told me plainly my Path is penalised in this whole zone.
- **"Setting Up".** Never saw the words during the fight. Only hovering the hourglass among eight rule icons shows "Setting Up: Its units do not attack in turns 1-2; they can attack from turn 3. Watch what it builds. Applies to the enemy only." (`xm31_icon_hover1.png`). Without the hover, turns 1-2 just looked like a boss dropping a sleeping Sashimi Weenie and doing nothing (`xm29_enemy_turn.png`). Once read, it is a good rule: the run where I played infrastructure every turn stayed at 22 HP into turn 6 (`xm31_end.png`).
- **How a loss read.** Very well: "Defeat / The Doppelganger wins after 7 turns. / You are carried out to the respawn point at full HP. The dungeon starts over from its first room. The cards and gold you already won are kept. / What beat you: Cake Golem dealt 13 of the 27 damage you took (2 hits). / Tip: You played no Infrastructure on 4 of your 7 turns..." (`xm30_after.png`). Then at the respawn: "Losing costs nothing: your cards, gold and items are all kept." It could add that the restart means the Sentinel and the 60 m walk again.
- **Length.** Room/roamer fights: 9 turns (Prep Station Sous-Chef, `xm27_after_pilot.png`), 9+ turns (Frozen Line Cook, 2 HP left at time-out), 11 turns (Gelatin Sentinel). Each 100-120 s at Speed 1x with the pilot. Boss: 7-8+ turns.
- **Board.** By turn 7-8 the enemy row holds 8-13 cards; at 12 they overlap and names truncate (`xm29_after_pilot.png`).

## 5. Smaller notes

The route line is drawn through the Soup Fountain, which physically blocks the hero (`xm17_map_route.png`); Basil's "south-west" for the truffle is west; the quest NPC "Basil" and the pickup "Giant Basil Leaf" share a name; dungeon rooms are all "???" with hover "Unknown. Step on it to find out", so the first choice between two rooms is a coin flip (`xm24_arrive.png`, `xm32_node_hover.png`); the "Retreat" button has no tooltip (`xm32_retreat_hover.png`); the energy-cost icon on hand cards is too small; the small white pointer arrow is easy to lose (`xm02_arrow.png`). Dev snapshots saved inside the dungeon reloaded at the zone entrance with the dungeon reset - if the player's own save behaves the same, a quit inside the Test Kitchen loses the run; not confirmed.

What I liked: the map (`xm13_map.png`) and the dungeon map (`xm24_arrive.png`) are beautiful and informative; the gate sign, the pre-fight rules card, the right-click preview with "In this fight: 2/3 (printed 1/1)", the Defeat recap and the "Losing costs nothing" toast are exactly the plain text a new player needs; the hub's writing is consistently funny ("I'm not crying, I'm just standing near some onions").
