# Critic 2 - the late game and the ending (Nov 17)

Read `docs/review/critic_nov17_common.md` first (the rules, the commands, the report). Then this brief. Nothing else.

**Your lens:** the last hours of the game. Does the endgame pay off? Is it clear where to go, is the final boss a worthy climax, does the
ending land, do the credits roll, and is it clear what to do after them? **No reviewer has ever reached the credits**, and the last one
scored clarity 5: "nothing tracks the ending after Primm". Judge it like the last act of Sea of Stars or Octopath Traveler.

**Budget: 14 runs, 80 minutes.** Lens name: `late`. Report: `docs/review/critic_nov17/02_late.md`.

## What to play, in order

1. **The Capital, all four lands freed:** `dev load bot_refusemancer_capital_ready` (a hero in Crosspath with all four Path zones freed,
   about level 18). Walk the imperial road north out of town into the Capital (it is on the map). Follow the quest tracker (top right) and
   the map's "Point me to...". Get past the great gate's guard, find the resistance's hideout **the Crease** under the city and its people
   (Wren, Fig Sly's Black Market), and **the Old Service Tunnels** if you can find them (`dev travel dungeon S-CAP` after one run of
   trying).
2. **The castle and Primm:** walk to the castle on its hill and enter (`dev travel dungeon D-PC` after one run of trying). Play through it
   to **Primm, and beat him** - every phase. Play his fight by hand where you can, use `pilot` where you must. If you lose, use the
   defeat screen's "Fight Primm again"; after two losses you may `dev level 24` and `dev heal` and try again (say so). Do not `dev win` him.
3. **The ending and the credits:** watch the whole ending (`key E` slowly; shoot each picture), **the credits to their end**, and what
   comes after them.
4. **The first postgame steps:** what does the game tell you to do now? Follow the quest tracker and the map for at least two runs: the
   gathering in the Capital, then whatever it points to next. Do the markers lead you somewhere real?

If a run ends mid-fight or mid-ending, `dev save` often so the next run can continue (a snapshot does not keep a dungeon's room map:
re-enter with `dev travel dungeon D-PC` after a load and say so).

## Your report also answers, in one short paragraph each

- **Wayfinding:** at each step from the Capital's gate to the postgame, did you know where to go? Where did you guess?
- **The climax:** was Primm a worthy final boss (rules, phases, difficulty, presentation)?
- **The ending:** did it land emotionally? Did the pictures and the words agree? How long was it?
- **The credits and after:** did they roll, how did they look, and was the next goal clear?
