## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. Lighting and backdrop (raises 8, 9, 1). Drop exposure/bloom by about one stop so cream plaster reads cream and gold trim reads gold, not white. Put a real sky behind the castle: blue with soft clouds and a pale mountain band, with a haze gradient that lightens the castle and townhouse tops with distance (visible in `ref`, `palace_approach`, `gate_plaza`). Give statues, lamp posts, planters and the fountain a proper warm-key shadow with a soft edge, and remove the bright spotlight disc under the player.

2. Skyline and roofs (raises 4, 3, 1). Add slender towers with pointed pastel spires (blue, pink, sage cones with gold finials, 2-3 townhouse heights) at the four corners of the plaza and in pairs flanking the castle stairs so the silhouette steps up to the castle, and raise the camera or lower the castle so the spire line is actually in frame in `ref` and `palace_approach`. Cap every townhouse row with a pastel slate roof, a central pediment with a sun medallion and pinnacles at the corners instead of the flat cut-off in `palace_approach` and `boulevard`.

3. Surface quality and the districts (raises 9, 6, 2). Cut the grain/noise filter to a fraction of its strength and sharpen the texture filtering so plaster, plinth carving and windows are crisp (`ref_mid`, `boulevard`). Replace the flat forward-facing statue cut-outs with meshes or at least two-angle sprites. In the districts, line the blank curtain walls of `transit_yards`, `hungry_quarter`, `grave_row` and `the_reek` with the same townhouse facades used on the boulevard so each district is a street, not a lot, and turn the striped lawns in `gate_plaza` and `grave_row` into parterres (box-hedge quadrants, rose beds, cone topiary in gold urns on pedestals) with the benches squared to the axis and the manhole covers removed from the Showcase.
