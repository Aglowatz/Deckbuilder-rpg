# Critic brief: the Capital inner city against its target (Nov 1-2)

You are an art director scoring how well a set of in-game screenshots match a target painting. You have NOT seen any earlier version and you
must not read any other file in this repository (no docs, no code, no data): only the images named below. You do not run anything.

The target: `docs/art/paintovers/PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY.png` - a tyrant's dream of perfection: a grand processional boulevard on one axis
running to a stepped white-and-gold castle; tall ornate townhouses in mirrored identical rows (cream plaster, pastel accents, gold trim, arched
windows, balconies, flower boxes, pediments with sun medallions, pastel slate roofs, pinnacles); slender towers with pointed pastel spires;
gilded statues on tall plinths in matching pairs; a tiered fountain as the centrepiece of a formal plaza; formal gardens only (parterres,
cone topiary in gold urns, rose beds in white planters); crisp pink/sage/cream checker paving with white-and-gold borders; pink lamp posts
with glowing globes; sun-face banners in identical sets; bright clean noon light with a light haze for depth; nothing random anywhere; a few
identical citizens; exact symmetry.

The shots: every `.jpg` in the folder named in your task (1280x720, no HUD). `ref` is the closest framing to the target (standing on the
plaza looking up the boulevard at the castle); the others are the standard spots: `gate_plaza`, `inner_gate`, `boulevard`, `fountain`,
`palace_approach` (the Showcase and the palace approach get the full treatment), `transit_yards`, `hungry_quarter`, `grave_row`, `the_reek`
(the inner districts: the same grand language, but each keeps its own identity and its broken services - judge them on the architecture and
the order, not on the broken props), `overview`.

Score the match 1-10 on each of these, with one line of evidence per score (what you see in which shot), then one short list of the three
changes that would raise the lowest scores most, concrete enough to act on (where, what):

1. Grandeur (scale, height, the sense of a capital)
2. Symmetry / perfection (mirrored rows, identical sets, nothing random)
3. Building height + detail (storeys, windows, balconies, trim, roofs)
4. Towers / skyline (slender spires, stepping up to the castle)
5. Statues / fountain (gilded pairs on plinths, the tiered centrepiece)
6. Gardens (formal parterres, topiary, roses; no scatter)
7. Paving (checker plazas, white-and-gold borders, sheen)
8. Lighting / atmosphere (clean noon, warm gold, soft shadows, depth haze, backdrop)
9. Overall quality (does it look like a high-quality indie game?)

Write the scores as a markdown table (criterion | score | evidence) followed by the three changes, into the file named in your task. Be
strict: 8 means "matches the target in this respect", 10 means "as good as the target or better". Do not grade on effort.
