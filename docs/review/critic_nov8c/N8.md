# Critic N8 - The Wayward Path: the first hour, and the new painted art

## 1. Summary

The Wayward Path looks like a much bigger game than it plays like in its first hour. The painted art is the star: the wake-up still, the portraits, the Forgotten Cave node map, the Shattered Gallery map, Professor Pump's classroom, Tilly's and Alembic's shops and the pack shop would all hold up in a Steam trailer. The card game underneath is readable and the tutorial tips are short and funny. Two things hold the first hour back. First, wayfinding: the opening forest is so dark that the hero vanishes behind the foliage, and in Crosspath the first goal ("talk to Elder Maren on the square") points at someone who is not on the square, not on the map and not in the map's "Where to?" list. Second, the tutorial cave is a five-node, all-or-nothing run: one loss at node 3 throws you back to the bedroll in the forest and the whole cave starts over. Part B is mostly a pass. The quiz, the shops and the Gallery are excellent. Dex's matching game and the thorn wall look unfinished next to them, and the fountain statues are too small to read.

**How I played.** About 80 minutes of wall clock over 35 runs, all from `C:\Dev\wp_critic`. I picked **Gourmand** after reading `More` on every column. "Hold the line with sturdy chefs and cook Ingredients into huge Golems" was the most concrete promise, and the full starting-deck list under More sold it. Part A: a fresh `dev newgame` covered the wake-up, the Rescuer conversation and the Path choice, then `dev save n8_forest`. I walked to the cave, read the cave tips, and played the first duel's opening turns by hand: infrastructure, Line Cook, phases, the End Turn guard. I used `pilot` for the rest. Over four cave attempts I won the first fight four times (4, 4, 5 and 10 rounds) and passed the Hollow Well. I **lost** once to the Hollow Stalker (node 3, 12 rounds, the pilot playing my hand), which sent me back to the bedroll. Two attempts died to driver timing on the victory screens. Because the cave cannot be snapshotted mid-run and each attempt cost about 7 minutes, I used my **one allowed `dev travel`: `dev travel town`** to reach Crosspath. That skipped the cave's arrival beat, so my Crosspath read starts at level 1 with 120 gold, not the cave's ~160 gold and XP. A `dev save` made in town reloads into the forest, so every later town run repeated `dev load n8_forest` + `dev travel town` (I count that as the same jump). In Crosspath I opened the map, Where to?, the Character page, and Tilly Tonic's shop. With 120 gold I could afford any one item on the shelf (50-100 g), and nothing on the high shelf (opens at level 17). I never found Elder Maren, so I never reached the Gourmand gatekeeper (Chef Fennel Gravois, listed under Where to?). Part B: `n6_postgame` for the Gallery, the Gainlands quiz, Dex, the Wardrobe and Packs. `n6_midgame` plus `dev essence/gold` for Alembic. `n8_forest` plus `dev gate refusemancer` and `dev freed *` for the thorn wall and the fountain statues.

## 2. Scores

Overall, both parts:

| | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fun | 6/10 | Card combat is tidy and the minigames add texture, but the first hour has too much dead time: a 5-node cave you replay from scratch after one loss, and a town where the first goal cannot be found. |
| Clarity | 5/10 | In-battle teaching is very good (tips, the "What beat you" box, the End Turn guard), but world wayfinding is poor: a dark forest, a quest pointing to the wrong place, no map marker for the goal NPC, and quiz answers with no visible right/wrong reveal. |
| Difficulty | 6/10 (slightly too hard and too swingy early) | The first enemy has 3 HP, then the third cave fight (Hollow Stalker, 5 HP, Cannon Fodder chip damage) can end the whole run, with HP carrying over and no healing unless the well's 69 % test passes. |

## 3. Findings

1. **The first town goal points to the wrong place and the map does not show it.** The tracker says "Settle in: talk to Elder Maren on the square". She is not on the square, and on an early save neither the town map nor its "Where to?" list (16 entries: vendors, challengers, exits) includes her. On the mid-game save the map shows her at the far north, beside the Forgotten Cave and the Capital road, nowhere near the square. I walked the square, the fountain ring, the NE shops and the west road and never found her. Caveat: I arrived via `dev travel town`, which may have skipped an arrival beat that places her. Even so, the text says "square" and the map has no marker for her. Where: `n8_b1_01_town.png`, `n8_b4_01_whereto.png`, `n8_p9_01_map.png`. **Severity: major (borderline blocker for a new player).** Fix: put Maren on the square for this beat, or change the text to "by the cave gate north of the square". Always give the current main goal a map pin and a Where to? entry. A HUD goal arrow in town would help too.

2. **The tutorial cave is all-or-nothing and a loss sends you back to the forest.** Five nodes, HP carries over, "only shrines heal". The Hollow Stalker at node 3 beat my pilot-played Gourmand deck, and the result was "the dungeon starts over" plus a reload at the bedroll with no prompt pointing back to the cave. Where: `n8_a11_n3_b.png` (Defeat: "You are carried out to the respawn point... the dungeon starts over"), `n8_a11_n5_c.png` (back at the bedroll, no hint). **Severity: major.** Fix: for the tutorial cave only, restart at the node you lost on, or heal to full between fights. At least respawn at the cave mouth, not the bedroll.

3. **The opening forest is too dark and the hero gets lost behind the foliage.** On the walk to the cave, the hero disappears entirely behind a large bush (`n8_a2_09_walk.png`: a beacon arrow and a ground ring, no player). The clearing is mostly near-black green, and the cave arch has a flat black slab across its top that reads as a missing texture. Where: `n8_a2_09_walk.png`, `n8_a3_03_ring.png`. **Severity: major (first 60 seconds of the game).** Fix: raise the ambient light and the lantern pool, fade foreground foliage when it covers the hero, and fix or texture the slab over the arch.

4. **The quiz gives no visible right/wrong feedback, and the obvious answer was marked wrong.** To "What do the Beefcakes use to keep the lights on across the Gainlands?" I chose "A Giant mills and colossal hamster wheels". The title art, the Gainlands set and the starter card Hamster Wheel Runner all show mills and hamster wheels. The board jumped straight to Question 2. The only feedback was a tiny, almost transparent bubble in the top-left corner: "Hmm, no." No answer was highlighted. Where: `n8_p5_06.png` (the question), `n8_p6_01_answered.png` (the jump to Q2; the faint "Hmm, no." sits over the window art). **Severity: major (inside an otherwise lovely feature).** Fix: after each answer, flash the right answer green and the wrong pick red for about 1.5 s. Move Pump's reaction into a readable bubble. Re-check that question's answer key: if mills/wheels is wrong, the setting tells the player otherwise.

5. **Dex's matching game looks like placeholder UI.** The cards are flat dark rectangles with a "?" on a plain gradient. There is no table, no Dex portrait and no painted card back, while the quiz next door has a full painted classroom. The "New quest" toast covers the title on open. 14 moves for 8 pairs is a tight cap, but that is fine for a "beat Dex" goal. Where: `n8_p6_05_dexgame.png`, `n8_p7_03_flip.png`. **Severity: major for the look, minor for play.** Fix: use the game's existing painted card back, a felt-table backdrop and a small Dex portrait with a bark per pair found. Delay the quest toast until the board is dismissed.

6. **The thorn wall does not read as thorns, and it does not change after Moss is beaten.** The "wall of thorns" is a column of ordinary autumn trees with some purple vines. Nothing "breathing" is visible, and it blends into the regular treeline. After `dev gate refusemancer` it is pixel-for-pixel the same; only the prompt changes to "[E] Travel to the Verdant Dump". A flat grey untextured ground slab shows behind it on the left in both states. The green caption text is laid over green grass and is hard to read. Where: `n8_p14_04.png` (sealed), `n8_p15_01_after.png` (after). **Severity: major (a gate that does not look like a gate).** Fix: use dense dark bramble with thorns and a slow pulse, and part or wither it once Moss is beaten (a short opening beat would be ideal). Hide or texture the grey slab. Give captions a dark backing plate.

7. **The fountain in the town square looks like an untextured stand-in, and the statues are unreadable.** The centrepiece of Crosspath is a pale lavender, flat-shaded blocky structure in the middle of richly painted half-timbered houses. With all four zones freed (`dev freed *`), the plinth statues are tiny brown figurines, a few pixels tall at the default camera, that I could not tell apart. Where: `n8_b1_01_town.png`, `n8_p12_05_fountain.png`. **Severity: major (look).** Fix: give the fountain the town's painted stone material and water. Make the statues 2-3x larger, give them a light pass or rim glow, and add a short "a statue of Grandmaster Flex now stands here" caption when one appears.

8. **The town camera is half roof.** At the default angle, foreground roofs fill the right third and lower-left of the screen on the square. Where: `n8_b1_01_town.png`, `n8_p12_05_fountain.png`. **Severity: minor-major.** Fix: fade or cut away near roofs (you already fade foliage elsewhere), or pull the default camera in.

9. **The Character page hint overflows its box.** "Equipment unlocks with the quest "Some Assembly Required" (from Elder Maren)" spills past the right edge of its pill. Where: `n8_b3_02_char.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: wrap it to two lines or widen the pill.

10. **Long dialogue lines are cut off in the box text.** Professor Pump's "Fifteen of them concern the biceps..." line fits on screen, but its label was truncated with "You can'..." while revealing, and it wraps "brain-/cep" across lines. Where: `n8_p5_02.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: cap line length or let the box grow by one line, and avoid hyphenated wraps.

11. **Battle hand and board readability.** In the first duel, a played Infrastructure becomes a tiny card stuck just above the hand (`n8_a6_07.png`, at about 712,650), not on the board where the player's units go. In the Gallery boss fight the hand grows to 12 cards in two overlapping rows, and the "Rules of this fight" caption is overdrawn by the resource orb icons (`n8_p3_03_mid.png`, top left). The "End / Turn" medallion splits the word oddly. Where: `n8_a6_03_cook.png`, `n8_a6_07.png`, `n8_p3_03_mid.png`. **Severity: minor-major.** Fix: give infrastructure a visible row on the player's side of the board, cap the hand fan to one row with tighter overlap, and move the orb counter clear of the rules caption.

12. **The Gallery map shows a stale tooltip and a hidden entrance.** After `dev travel dungeon S-GALLERY -2` and stepping onto the last node, the "The Gallery of Good News" tooltip (an earlier node) stays open behind the pre-fight modal. The entrance node "The Cracked Frame" turned into a "?/???" node. Where: `n8_p2_04_board.png`. **Severity: minor (may only happen through the dev jump).** Fix: close node tooltips when a modal opens, and keep visited/entrance nodes labelled.

13. **The Card Packs shelf scrolls sideways with no affordance.** Seven packs fit, and the eighth ("Gilded Refusemancer Pack") is cut off at the right edge with no arrow or scrollbar. The HUD said "27 unopened packs" while the page said "28 unopened" after `dev packs`. On a pack opening, the three revealed cards are small and high on the screen above a big summary box. Where: `n8_p8_03_packs.png`, `n8_p8_06_openall.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: add arrows or wrap the shelf to two rows, refresh the HUD count, and show the revealed cards larger and centred.

14. **Name labels overlap NPC barks in the world.** At the quiz corner, "Dex Shufflewick" is drawn on top of Professor Pump's bark bubble, and the two NPCs stand inside each other. The "[E] Talk" prompt does not say who. Where: `n8_p4_04.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: offset barks above name plates, space the NPCs apart, and name the target in the prompt.

15. **The Wardrobe cloak icon does not match the hero.** The "Plain Cloak (Natural)" tile shows a red hooded cloak while the hero wears a teal-green one. Where: `n8_p8_01_wardrobe.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: tint the tile with the equipped dye.

16. **Alembic's essence bars look like meters but do not fill.** Each essence row has a short coloured lozenge of the same length whether the jar holds 8 or 2. The brew panel is mostly empty. Where: `n8_p11_02.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: fill the bars against the 60 cap (or drop them), and show the two picked Paths and possible results in the empty brew panel.

17. **The Tilly shop tip covers the high-shelf label, and a fifth high-shelf slot is cut off below the panel.** Where: `n8_b7_04.png`. **Severity: minor.** Fix: anchor the tip lower or to the side, and fit or scroll the high shelf.

18. **Smaller notes.**
    - **J (quests) and C (character) do nothing in the opening forest**, with no "unlocks in town" message (`n8_a3`, play.log). Show a short "Your quest log opens once you reach town" toast.
    - The forest minimap and map are blurry pixel blobs (`n8_a1_05.png`, top right).
    - A `dev save` made in Crosspath reloads into the forest (dev tooling only).
    - No `SCRIPT ERROR` appeared in any `stdout.log`. Only exit-time leak warnings showed up (RID/PagedAllocator leaks, "RenderingServer::get_singleton() is null" on every quit). They are harmless to players, but worth a cleanup.

## New art check

- **Shattered Gallery (map and boss board): fits, standout.** The pastel mirror-palace map with its three wings and the dark-blue cracked-mirror boss board are the best art in the game. Small issues: path bars cut across some painted walls, and a stale node tooltip shows during the pre-fight modal (`n8_p1_04_last.png`, `n8_p2_04_board.png`, `n8_p3_02_mid.png`).
- **Trivia quiz (Gainlands classroom): fits.** The painted classroom, chalkboard and rhino professor portrait are lovely, and the text on the board is very readable. The feedback bubble is nearly invisible and the answers have no reveal (`n8_p5_06.png`, `n8_p6_01_answered.png`). The world-side "classroom" is just a blackboard and stumps, next to flat blue squares that look untextured (`n8_p4_04.png`).
- **Dex's matching game: out of place.** It has plain "?" rectangles on a gradient, no painted table, no card back and no Dex; it looks like debug UI next to the quiz. The card faces themselves are fine (`n8_p7_03_flip.png`).
- **Wardrobe: fits.** The painted wardrobe backdrop, hero on a plinth and painted hat/cloak icons are cohesive. The cloak tile colour does not match the dyed hero (`n8_p8_01_wardrobe.png`).
- **Card Packs page: fits.** The pack art is rich and on brand per Path, and the shop backdrop is warm. The shelf is cut off on the right with no scroll cue, and the revealed cards are small (`n8_p8_03_packs.png`, `n8_p8_06_openall.png`).
- **Auntie Alembic's cauldron: fits.** It has a strong portrait and a nice cauldron icon. The layout feels empty and the essence bars are decorative only (`n8_p11_02.png`).
- **Thorn wall at the Dump gate: out of place.** It reads as autumn trees, not thorns, it never opens, and there is a grey untextured slab behind it (`n8_p14_04.png`, `n8_p15_01_after.png`).
- **Fountain statues: out of place.** They are too small and dark to read, on a fountain that itself looks untextured (`n8_p12_05_fountain.png`).
- **Old lever (SW of town): not found** in the time I had. My west walk was blocked by a barrel and a stall (`n8_p13_03.png`).

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The painted stills and portraits.** The wake-up still (`n8_a1_02_wake.png`), the Rescuer and Wanderer portraits, Tilly, Alembic and Professor Pump. They set the tone instantly and stay consistent.
- **The painted dungeon maps.** The Forgotten Cave (`n8_a4_01_cave.png`) and the Shattered Gallery (`n8_p1_04_last.png`) are beautiful and readable, with clear glowing next-step nodes and check marks.
- **The Path choice screen with `More`.** Three sample cards per Path, a one-line pitch, then the Resource and the full starting deck (`n8_a2_03_more.png`). This is how to let a player choose with confidence.
- **In-battle teaching.** Short, funny, sequenced tips ("No pressure. (Some pressure.)"), gold glow on playable cards, the "End your turn? You have 1 energy left and can still play 3 cards" guard, and the "Needs 3 energy; you have 1" message.
- **The defeat screen's "What beat you" box.** "Cannon Fodder dealt 8 of the 10 damage you took (4 hits)" plus a concrete tip (`n8_a11_n3_b.png`). It is clear and kind. Keep it, and copy it into the victory screen for close wins.
- **The Hollow Well event.** It states the odds ("about 69 % to pass"), the stakes, and offers a "Leave it" option. Great risk design.
- **The pre-boss "Before the fight" rules card.** Every rule in play is listed and colour-coded before the Gallery boss (`n8_p2_04_board.png`).
- **The quiz board presentation and the shop screens.** Big readable type, painted settings, and in-character shop barks ("Don't mind the scorch mark, that was a Tuesday!").
