# Capital inner city, cycle c4: critic scores against PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY

Shots scored: ref, ref_mid, ref_play, gate_plaza, inner_gate, boulevard, fountain, palace_approach, transit_yards, hungry_quarter, grave_row, the_reek, overview (docs/art/capital_rebuild_nov1/c4).

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Grandeur | 6 | `ref` and `inner_gate` do frame a wide plaza, paired colossal pillars and a castle filling the sky, but the castle is a flat pale facade that meets the plaza directly behind the fountain with no rising middle ground (the stair in `palace_approach` is small and sits on a wall), so it reads as a backdrop wall, not a capital climbing to a palace. |
| 2. Symmetry / perfection | 7 | `ref_play` and `fountain` are properly mirrored (paired topiary beds, paired gold statues, paired lamps, paired guards). `gate_plaza` breaks it: green lawn and benches on the left, plain sandstone paving and a blue-roofed kiosk on the right, a manhole cover and a stray bench mid-plaza; `palace_approach` hangs different signboards, an easel and a horn on the two lamp rows. |
| 3. Building height + detail | 5 | `palace_approach` townhouses are four storeys of blue arched-window decals on flat cream planes with gold trim and a few flower boxes, but no balconies, no pediments or sun medallions, no ground-floor arcades, and flat cut-off tops instead of pastel slate roofs; the same two facades repeat the full length of the boulevard. |
| 4. Towers / skyline | 4 | Only the castle backdrop supplies spires (small blue cones at the top corners of `ref`); the townhouse rows end in flat rooflines, `overview` and `palace_approach` show no spires above the rows, and no slender freestanding towers step up toward the castle anywhere. |
| 5. Statues / fountain | 8 | `ref` has the full set: three gilded figures on white-and-gold plinths (laurel wreath on the plinth in `boulevard`), a tiered fountain with water, sun medallion on the basin, rose pots on the rim. Deductions: the statue is a sprite with pixelated edges that the camera clips through in `boulevard`, and the target's pairs flank the plaza further apart. |
| 6. Gardens | 6 | Cone topiary in white-and-gold planters ringed with pink roses (`ref`), rose borders flanking the boulevard (`palace_approach`) and striped lawn parterres in `grave_row` are the right language; but no gold urns, no parterre patterns, large flat green lawn planes with scattered benches in `gate_plaza`, and dark conifers on the castle terraces instead of clipped cones. |
| 7. Paving | 7 | The pink/sage/cream checker with gold rings around the fountain and gold boulevard edge lines (`fountain`, `overview`) matches the target closely; tiles are character-sized rather than fine, the gold borders are thin and blurry, there is no sheen, and `gate_plaza` has a plain sandstone patch on the right. |
| 8. Lighting / atmosphere | 4 | Every shot is overexposed and cream-tinted: the sky is almost absent (a thin strip in `gate_plaza` and `transit_yards`), the castle facade is washed to near-white with no contrast, buildings and statues cast no visible shadows, the only crisp shadow is the hero's in a spotlight pool (`ref_mid`), and heavy haze flattens the plaza and castle into one plane. |
| 9. Overall quality | 5 | Coherent art direction and a convincing plaza (`ref_play`), undercut by billboard facades, sprite statues with hard pixel edges (`boulevard`), blurry pillar textures, cardboard pink-roofed sheds in `hungry_quarter`, flat cut-out wheels in `transit_yards`, and the blown-out exposure throughout. |

## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. Lighting (scores 8, 9, 1): drop exposure and ambient fill by roughly a stop in the Capital, remove the cream colour cast so the sky reads blue and the castle reads white-with-gold against it, turn on sun shadows for the castle, pillars, statues and townhouses (soft, about 45 degrees), and reduce the depth haze to a light far-distance band only. Re-frame `ref` so a strip of sky and the castle spires sit above the facade.

2. Skyline (scores 4, 1, 3): give the townhouse rows pitched pastel slate roofs with gold pinnacles and pediments along the ridge, then place a mirrored set of slender towers with pointed pastel spires behind the rows on both sides of the boulevard, stepping taller toward the castle; add two or three terraced steps with balustrades between the fountain plaza and the castle base so the palace rises instead of standing as a wall behind the fountain.

3. Facade detail and plaza symmetry (scores 3, 2, 6): on the townhouse facade add a ground-floor arcade, balconies with flower boxes on every window of one storey, pediments with sun medallions over the doors, and shrink the window decals so storeys read; in `gate_plaza` mirror the halves (same lawn-and-rose parterre on both sides, replace the blue kiosk and sandstone patch, remove the manhole and loose benches) and put the cone topiary in gold urns with parterre hedging as the target shows.
