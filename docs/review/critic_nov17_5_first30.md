# Critic 5 - the first 30 minutes and the menus (Nov 17)

Read `docs/review/critic_nov17_common.md` first (the rules, the commands, the report). Then this brief. Nothing else.

**Your lens:** the first impression. An itch.io player downloads a free game and decides in 30 minutes whether to keep playing. Play
exactly what that player plays, **from the title screen, with no jumps at all** for the first hour of your sitting: the title, the
settings, a new game, the opening forest, the tutorial cave and its first fights and tutorials, arriving in the town (Crosspath), the
first errands, the shops, the map, the quest log, the Character page, the Deck Station, the pause menu. Judge it like the first half hour
of Slay the Spire, Sea of Stars or Dredge: does it hook you, does it respect your time, is it clear, does it feel good to touch?

**Budget: 14 runs, 80 minutes.** Lens name: `first30`. Report: `docs/review/critic_nov17/05_first30.md`.

## What to play

1. **Run 1:** the title screen; open the settings and look at every tab (shoot them); change one setting; back; **New Game**. Read the
   opening yourself (`key E`, `wait 1`, `shot`), not with `skip_dialogue`.
2. **Runs 2-8:** play on from where the last run stopped: `dev save n17first30_<nn>` at the end of each run and `dev load` it at the
   start of the next (that is the only dev command you may use here). The forest, the tutorial cave (its rooms, its fights: play at least
   two fights yourself, by hand), the town, the first quests the game gives you, the shops, the map (`M`) and its "Point me to...", the
   quest log (`J`), the Character page (`C`), the Deck Station (`B`), the Card Packs page (`P`), the pause menu (`Escape`).
3. **Runs 9-11: a controller.** From your latest snapshot, put the mouse and keyboard away: `pad` and `stick` only. Walk, talk (`pad a`),
   open and close the menus (`pad start`, `pad b`), move through a shop and a menu with the D-pad, and play one fight's turn with the pad
   if you can. Does every screen work with a controller? Are the button prompts right?
4. **The rest:** anything that confused you, again, to be sure.

## Your report also answers, in one short paragraph each

- **The hook:** at which minute did the game earn your attention, or lose it?
- **Clarity:** at each step, did you know what to do next? Where did you guess?
- **The tutorials:** too many, too few, too wordy, well placed?
- **The menus:** which screen is the best and which the worst, and why?
- **The controller:** what worked and what did not (each screen you tried)?
