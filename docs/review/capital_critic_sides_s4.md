# Capital critic: the SIDES, set s4 (Nov 2)

Target: `docs/art/paintovers/PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY.png`. Shots: the 23 jpgs in `docs/art/capital_rebuild_nov1/s4/`. Centre shots used only as the standard. Scale: 8 = matches the target in this respect, 10 = as good as the target or better.

Grandeur | 5 | `side_w` / `side_e`: the side plazas are one fountain ring and one townhouse block each side, then open checker to the colonnade; `overview_w` / `overview_e` show flat empty paving with single-storey huts, the tram shed and the wheels as the only massing beyond the two plinths. East plaza (`side_e_mid`, `overview_e`): tram shed right only, rift left only, a black cable scribble by the right plinth. `grave_arcade`: huts paired, rift right only. `hungry_quarter` and `ward_wall` are the only shots where left truly reads like right. |
| 3. But the west/east townhouses are cut flat at the top with no roof, pinnacle or pediment (`side_w_mid`), facades are painted-flat with no relief, and the tram shed and grave huts are single-storey boxes with black interiors (`side_e`, `grave_row`). |

Water is a flat cyan disc with no spray, the statues are billboards that turn edge-on from the mid cameras (`side_e_mid`, right plinth), and there is no second rhythm of smaller statues: the lamp ring and the lawns carry none, and the grave and ward districts have no statue at all. |

Paving cleanliness | 4 | Checker and gold rings are crisp (`overview_w`), but the plazas carry black ground under every rift (`transit_yards`, `hungry_row`), a black cable draped by the east plinth (`overview_e`, `side_e_mid`), grey-and-orange vats with green blobs on the west plaza (`side_w`), a basin with black pipes and a bare post (`grave_row`), a bucket and a grey ring on the ward paving (`ward_wall`), and loose rose baskets everywhere. ## The three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. **Formalise the rifts and the dead ground under them (scores 6, 5, 2).** Give every purple rift (west lawn edge, east plaza, grave lawn right, hungry plaza centre) a white-and-gold octagonal kerb the width of the fountain ring, a cone topiary in a gold urn at each of the four corners, and keep the black soil and crystal strictly inside the kerb. Mirror each kerb across the plaza axis with an identical sealed kerb (same stone, no hole, a sun medallion on the lid) so left reads like right. Drop the rift glow to a pale lilac rim light rather than a saturated spot.

2. **Replace loose baskets with parterres on both side plazas and lay the lawns out as gardens (scores 5, 2, 1).** Around each side fountain put four of the centre's white-planter rose beds with a cone topiary (as in `ref`) at the four diagonals of the gold ring, and delete the loose rose baskets. Cut the west lawn and the grave lawn into a cross of checker paths with gold borders, a gold urn with topiary at every path corner, and rose beds in white planters along the long sides; keep the grave stones as two straight rows inside the beds. This gives the side districts a real plan instead of turf.

3. **Re-skin and pair the service props, and finish the side townhouses (scores 6, 3, 8).** Paint the paste vats, the grave basin and its pipes, the cable, the buckets and the bins in the city white with gold collars and a pastel accent, and place each in an exact mirrored pair on the plaza's axis (vats flanking the plaza entrance, the cable run as a straight white line between two gold-capped posts rather than a black droop). Make the two ground-wall decals on the west townhouses identical sun medallions. Cap the west and east townhouses with a pastel slate mansard roof, a gold pinnacle at each corner and a central pediment with a sun medallion, like the hungry-quarter block, so the plazas are framed by roofs rather than by cut-off walls.
