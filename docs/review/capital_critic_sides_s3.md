# Capital critic: the SIDES, set s3 (Nov 2)

Scored against `PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY.png`. Fresh eyes, only the brief, the target and the 23 shots in `capital_rebuild_nov1/s3`. The centre shots (`ref`, `ref_mid`, `ref_play`, `boulevard`, `fountain`, `palace_approach`, `gate_plaza`, `inner_gate`, `overview`) were used only as the standard. The wheels, rifts, cable and service props are taken as fixed by story; they are scored on how well they belong, not on being there.

## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. **Mirror each side plaza end to end (raises 2, 1).** East plaza: the wheel-left / pavilion-right pairing must become a pair of each - put a second pavilion mirroring the first on the west flank of the east plaza, and set the two wheels as a matched pair on the plaza's south edge, each on a white-and-gold plinth base with a sun medallion and a gold rim, so they read as the tyrant's monuments rather than parked machinery (`side_e`, `reek_wall`). West plaza: equal lamp counts each side of the ring (4 and 4, or 6 and 6 - `side_w`), and give every rift a formal frame: a low white-and-gold balustrade ring with four pink lamps on its corners, matching the fountain ring, over a cream-tile floor instead of black earth (`side_e_mid`, `grave_arcade`, `hungry_quarter`).

2. **Close the back of each side plaza and add the second rhythm (raises 1, 4).** The rear of `side_w` / `side_e` / `grave_row` is a flat medallion wall over an empty lawn. Replace the lawn strip with a terrace of cream checker, and line the rear wall with a row of 4-6 smaller gilded figures on short plinths with a cone topiary in a gold urn between each (the target's second rhythm). Scale the side fountains up ~1.5x and crown each with a small gilded figure so they match the centre's silhouette at half size; give `hungry_quarter` a real centrepiece in place of the bare white cylinder (a tiered fountain, same model as the west plaza).

3. **Sweep the paving and house the services (raises 6, 5).** Remove every loose object sitting on the checker: the single flower baskets (`side_w`, `transit_yards`, `overview_w`), the chair/ladder and the pot (`ward_wall`), the loose benches. Put the flowers back on the low planter walls in continuous troughs. Replace the flat striped lawn with cream checker plus hedge-edged parterre beds of pink roses (`grave_row`, `side_w_mid`). House the service props: the domed bin goes into a white plaster kiosk with gold trim set in a matched pair either side of the plaza entrance (`the_reek`); the cable runs along a white-and-gold rail on posts at regular spacing, both sides, instead of lying across the stone (`side_e`); the dead lanterns and grey-paste props get the same treatment - a white-and-gold casing, placed in rows.
