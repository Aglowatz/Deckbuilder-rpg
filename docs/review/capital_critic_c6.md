## Three changes that would raise the lowest scores most

1. **Build a real castle skyline behind the plaza (raises 4, 1, 8).** Replace the flat cream wall at the end of the axis (`palace_approach`, `ref`) with a stepped castle mass: three tiers stepping back and up from the stair, each tier lined with slender white towers topped by pointed blue and pink spires with gold finials, a central spire tallest; add a ring of smaller blue-capped turrets above the plaza facades so every shot from the plaza sees spires over the rooftops, and put mountains and a blue sky with a few clouds behind it.

2. **Fix the exposure and add sky, shadows and haze (raises 8, 9).** Pull exposure down about one stop so the cream plaster reads cream, not white; enable soft directional sun shadows from the facades, statues, lamps and topiary (the target has long soft shadows from the left); add a light blue-white distance haze that starts behind the fountain and fades the castle tiers; remove the hard circular spotlight pool under the hero.

3. **Give the townhouses relief and roofs, and mirror the plaza flanks (raises 3, 2, 6).** On the boulevard and plaza facades (`boulevard`, `palace_approach`), add protruding balconies with balustrades under every second-storey window, a gold cornice line between storeys, recessed window reveals, a pediment with a sun medallion over each entrance, and a pitched pastel slate roof with gold pinnacles so the fronts stop at a roofline instead of a flat cut; then replace the left lawn-and-benches and the right kiosk flank of the plaza (`fountain`, `ref`) with one identical set on each side - a rose parterre in a white-and-gold planter, two cone topiaries in gold urns, two pink lamp posts and a sun banner - so the plaza is symmetric about the axis.
