# Art-director critic report: nov12 "after" set (113 shots)

Saved by the main session from the critic's final message (the same brief, `docs/review/critic_art_nov12_brief.md`; a NEW no-context agent
that opened only the 113 files in `docs/overnight_shots/nov12/after` and never saw the before set or its scores). The score table below is
rebuilt from its rows (`tools`-free: `docs/review/visual_nov12/scores_after.csv` holds the same numbers).

**Overall: 6.2 / 10** (the before set's critic: 6.3). The painted 2D layers (dungeon maps 7.7, battles 7.6, UI 6.8) are close to
shippable. The walkable 3D world averages 5.4, a competent prototype, and is what stops the game reading as one premium world. D.N.A. (4.7)
is the weakest zone; Crosspath (6.9) is the only 3D zone near the painted layers.

Read with care: this critic scored the UNCHANGED content lower than the first one did (dungeon maps 8.2 -> 7.7, Crosspath 7.5 -> 6.9, the
forest 6.2 -> 5.7: none of them touched), so about half a point of the difference between the two reports is the reviewer, not the game.

Scoring note from the critic: the Capital shots and the two D.N.A. arrival-adjacent shots were scored from a single viewing.

## 3. The jarring things

- **Flat untextured surfaces.** D.N.A. floor plane and grey slabs (`dna_arrival_*`, `dna_hub_default`, `dna_door_low`); Capital lilac
  blocks (`capital_before_resistance_camp_*`), maroon box (`capital_*_grave_row_*`), cream stairs (`capital_before_castle_door_*`); Dump
  deck and posts (`dump_arrival_*`); Buffet mustard band, brown sea and pink slabs (`buffet_arrival_*`, `buffet_mid2_*`,
  `buffet_mid1_low`); Gainlands blue ground rectangles (`gainlands_hub_default`, `gainlands_mid2_default`).
- **Low-poly blocks next to painted art.** Faceted green hedge blobs (`buffet_door_*`, `buffet_mid1_*`, `dump_hub_*`); grey bear and pink
  pig statues (`dump_door_*`, `dump_mid2_*`); faceted lamp caps and spheres (`gainlands_arrival_low`, `dna_hub_low`, `dna_mid1_low`);
  faceted leaves and rocks (`forest_arrival_*`); flat-shaded canopy chunks (`dump_door_low`, `dump_mid2_low`).
- **Tiling and repeats.** Gainlands fortress brick with moire (`gainlands_door_*`), Buffet cracker walls (`buffet_door_*`), D.N.A.
  wallpaper and marble (`dna_hub_*`, `dna_door_*`), Capital cobbles (`capital_before_gate_road_default`).
- **Post-processing.** A sharpen/painterly filter adds crawling grain and halos to every 3D shot, worst in the Dump (`dump_hub_default`,
  `dump_mid1_default`) and Gainlands (`gainlands_mid1_default`). The painted backdrops are posterised and washed by haze
  (`gainlands_hub_default`, `buffet_arrival_default`).
- **Lighting.** Hard near-black cast shadows on orange rock (`gainlands_mid1_*`, `gainlands_arrival_default`); sawtooth and dithered
  shadow edges (`capital_before_castle_door_default`, `capital_before_gate_plaza_default`); D.N.A. uniformly dim green-grey with no key
  light; the forest muddy, the hero lit only by a small pool.
- **Sprites versus world.** Sprites carry more saturation and a crisp outline, and contact shadows are faint or missing
  (`gainlands_hub_default`, `buffet_hub_default`). Foreground sprites balloon in the low camera (`gainlands_mid1_low`, `buffet_mid2_low`,
  `dump_hub_low`).
- **Voids and edges.** D.N.A. grey floor to the horizon; Buffet flat brown sea; Capital bare dirt strip
  (`capital_before_the_reek_default`, `capital_before_transit_yards_default`); translucent ghost building
  (`capital_before_transit_yards_*`).
- **Placeholder-looking items.** `ui_map` blob map; locked pack domes in `ui_shop_cards`; smiley portraits in
  `capital_before_castle_door_*`; blue panel in `capital_after_castle_door_*`; flat heart planes in `dump_door_*`; flat windmill sails in
  `dump_mid2_*`.
- **Marker geometry in world shots.** Yellow triangles, shields and floor rings, plus the tan beam up the dungeon doors, sit in the scene
  as flat unlit polygons (`dna_hub_low`, `gainlands_door_*`, `dump_door_*`).

## 5. Five best-looking shots

1. `ui_title` - one painting, one light source, a clear near/mid/far read; the logo and buttons share the art's gold and wood.
2. `dungeon_S-GOUR` - warm lantern orange against teal water, dense but ordered; three nodes on calm wooden discs.
3. `dungeon_D-TUT` - cool blue cave with warm fire accents; the path is a light ribbon on dark ground.
4. `battle_zone_beefcake_start` - a calm mid-value wooden floor with the detail at the edges; cards and frames share the palette.
5. `crosspath_street_low` - the only 3D frame with hand-painted materials on every surface, one golden-hour key, sprites at the right scale
   and value. Runners-up: `gainlands_arrival_low`, `buffet_arrival_low`.

What these share: a limited palette with one warm and one cool family; a single clear key light; detail at the edges with a quiet centre; no
flat-colour or faceted geometry in frame.

## 6. Capital before vs after

The city changes, but only slightly. The after shots are warmer and brighter (lighting 6.8 against 5.4) and bunting is strung at the castle
door and plaza. The geometry, the crowd level and the ground read the same; Grave Row keeps its untextured maroon box; the bunting poles are
plain grey; one new panel at the castle door looks like a glitch. Net effect: a player would notice a nicer time of day, not a celebration.

What would sell it: crowds in the plaza and on the palace approach; confetti and petals in the air and on the paving; long banners in the
four Path colours on the townhouses and the wall; lit lanterns and garlands across every street, not one string; market stalls and tables;
fireworks or streamers in the sky; painted fountain water; the oppression dressing visibly removed; flowers and candles in Grave Row.
