# Art-director critic report: nov12 "before" set (113 shots)

Saved by the main session from the critic's final message (brief: `docs/review/critic_art_nov12_brief.md`; a fresh no-context agent that
opened only the 113 files in `docs/overnight_shots/nov12/before`).

Caveat from the critic: it scored in passes from notes; frame positions are most exact for the Capital, D.N.A., Dump, buffet_mid2 and the
D-* dungeon maps, coarser elsewhere.

Overall score: **6.3 / 10**. The 3D world alone (74 shots) is **5.6**; the painted 2D layers (dungeon maps, battles, UI) are **7.6**. That
gap is the headline: the paintings are premium, the walkable world is a competent prototype with visible placeholders.

## 3. The jarring things

- **Flat untextured surfaces.** The lavender city wall and foreground slabs (capital_before_gate_road_*, capital_before_resistance_camp_default), the brown box planter (all four grave_row shots), the gridded grey-green plane (dna_arrival_*), teal floor slabs (dna_mid1_*, dna_door_* bottom edge), orange bridge deck (dump_arrival_*), mustard wall and brown plane (buffet_arrival_*, buffet_mid2_default right), pink and green stall boxes (buffet_mid2_*), blue mats and pink block (gainlands_hub_*), cliff tops (gainlands_arrival_default, gainlands_mid1_*).
- **Pure-colour or pure-white areas.** Every Capital window is a flat blue (before) or flat cream (after) cut-out. The after-state stairs and walls clip to white (capital_after_castle_door_*). Buffet windows and the Buffet door are blank cream rectangles. A flat navy rectangle hangs on the palace in capital_after_gate_plaza_* and capital_after_palace_approach_*.
- **Pure black.** D.N.A. buildings are unlit black boxes in all ten shots; dna_mid2_default is 40% black. Gainlands pylons are black (gainlands_mid2_*).
- **Low-poly blocks next to painted art.** Capital: faceted lamp globes in every Capital shot (worst in capital_*_hungry_quarter_low and gate_plaza_low); megaphone (capital_*_palace_approach_low, right). D.N.A.: faceted sphere on a pedestal (dna_mid1_low, top centre). Dump: grey raccoon statue (dump_door_*, upper left), green topiary balls (dump_hub_low edges), pink blob statue (dump_mid2_*), floating island chunks and blocky tree canopies (dump_door_*, dump_arrival_*). Buffet: hedge blobs, boulder and bollards (buffet_door_*, buffet_mid1_*). Forest: angular leaves (forest_arrival_*).
- **Placeholder-looking art.** Smiley-face portraits on easels and the smiley on the palace front (capital_*_castle_door_*, palace_approach_*, gate_plaza_*); lollipop flowers in the brown box; flat-colour windmill blades (dump_mid*, dump_hub_default); flat-colour card pack silhouettes (ui_shop_cards); battle item slots.
- **Ghost or half-faded geometry caught in frame.** Translucent beige building with line-drawn props (dump_hub_* centre, dump_mid1_* right); translucent grey-blue buildings (capital_before_transit_yards_* upper left, capital_before_the_reek_low right edge).
- **Stray quads and billboards.** Giant yellow quad (dna_hub_low, lower right); oversized padlock (dna_door_low, lower right); giant paper sheets (dna_arrival_low); oversized quest marker (dna_mid1_low); white square particles (dump_arrival_*).
- **Tiling and stretching.** Brick wall (gainlands_door_*), cyan water (gainlands_arrival_default), stripe-stretched rock (gainlands_mid1_*), cracker paving (all Buffet), checker floor shimmer (Capital), marble repeat (dna_door_*).
- **Map edges.** Bare dirt strip along the bottom (capital_before_the_reek_*, capital_before_transit_yards_*); brown void (buffet_mid2_default, right); grey plane (dna_arrival_*).
- **Lighting.** Capital after is blown out; D.N.A. is muddy and under-lit; Buffet is an orange monochrome with haloed over-sharpening; Capital before has jagged, dithered shadow edges (capital_before_castle_door_*, grave_row_default).
- **Sprites.** A whited-out NPC (dump_mid1_default, lower left). Duplicate NPCs in rows (gainlands_mid1_*, the four identical courtiers in capital_before_grave_row_default and capital_before_hungry_quarter_default). Sprites in the bright Capital have faint contact shadows and read as pasted on.
- **Broken shot.** capital_before_resistance_camp_low shows only ground texture and the inside of a wall.
- **Style split.** The painted dungeon maps and title screen promise a richness the 3D zones do not deliver; dungeon_S-GALLERY is flatter than its siblings; the title art is glossier than everything in game.

## 4. Top 25 problems, most noticeable first

1. **D.N.A. buildings are unlit black boxes.** All ten dna_* shots, upper two-thirds of frame; dna_mid2_default left 40%. The zone reads as unfinished. Fix: lighting plus material (facade texture, window glow, rim or fill light).
2. **Capital after-state is blown out.** All capital_after_*; worst in castle_door_default (stairs, centre) and grave_row_default. Form and detail disappear, so the "celebration" looks like an exposure bug. Fix: lighting and post-processing (pull exposure and bloom, restore shadow contrast).
3. **Ghost buildings caught mid-fade.** dump_hub_default and dump_hub_low (centre), dump_mid1_default (lower right), dump_mid1_low (right), capital_before_transit_yards_* (upper left), capital_before_the_reek_low (right edge). They look like a rendering glitch. Fix: geometry and shader (dithered cut-away or full hide, never 50% alpha with outlines).
4. **Flat single-colour placeholder blocks.** capital_*_grave_row_* (brown box, lower right), capital_before_resistance_camp_default (lavender slabs, bottom), buffet_mid2_* (pink and green stall), buffet_mid1_default (pizza disc and cylinder, foreground), gainlands_hub_* (blue mats). Fix: material or new painting.
5. **Main dungeon doors do not read as doors.** buffet_door_* (blank cream rectangle, centre), dna_door_* (blank brick wall above stairs), gainlands_door_* (portcullis painted flat on brick). These are the zone's destination shots. Fix: geometry plus new painting for each door.
6. **Faceted low-poly props beside painted art.** Capital lamp globes (every Capital shot), megaphone (palace_approach_low, right), dump_door_* raccoon (upper left), dump_hub_low topiary (edges), dump_mid2_* pink statue (left), dna_mid1_low sphere (top centre), buffet_door_* hedges. Fix: geometry (smooth shading, more segments) or painted replacements.
7. **Smiley-face placeholder portraits and emblem.** capital_*_castle_door_* (easels left and centre), capital_*_palace_approach_* (easel lower left, palace front top centre), capital_before_gate_plaza_* (palace front). Fix: new painting.
8. **Windows are flat colour cut-outs.** Every Capital shot (blue before, cream after); buffet_hub_*, buffet_door_*, buffet_mid1_*. Hundreds of them per frame. Fix: material (painted window texture with frame, reflection and depth).
9. **Broken low camera at the Resistance Camp.** capital_before_resistance_camp_low, whole frame. A player choosing the low camera here sees nothing. Fix: geometry or camera collision.
10. **Flat untextured city wall and grey gate road.** capital_before_gate_road_* (wall across the upper half), capital_before_resistance_camp_default. First view of the final city is the drabbest. Fix: material (masonry) plus dressing.
11. **Navy rectangle on the palace after the boss falls.** capital_after_gate_plaza_* and capital_after_palace_approach_*, top centre. It reads as a hole in the building. Fix: new painting (a torn or replaced banner with a motif) and geometry.
12. **Stray giant quads and billboards.** dna_hub_low (yellow quad, lower right), dna_door_low (padlock, lower right), dna_arrival_low (paper sheets, upper right and left), dna_mid1_low (marker, left of centre). Fix: geometry and scale clamps by camera distance.
13. **Tutorial battle has no board art.** battle_tutorial_start and battle_tutorial_mid, whole background. The first fight a player sees is the worst-looking one. Fix: new painting.
14. **Map edges show voids and bare strips.** capital_before_the_reek_* and capital_before_transit_yards_* (bottom strip), buffet_mid2_default (right), buffet_arrival_default (brown plane), dna_arrival_* (grey plane both sides). Fix: dressing and geometry (skirts, backdrop, fog).
15. **Gainlands materials tile and stretch.** gainlands_door_* (brick), gainlands_arrival_default (water, cliff tops), gainlands_mid1_* (rock faces). Fix: material (larger texel variety, triplanar, colour variation).
16. **Buffet is an orange monochrome with haloed sharpening.** All buffet_*; worst buffet_arrival_default. Props and paths merge and edges ring. Fix: lighting and post-processing (cooler fill, reduce sharpen), plus a second ground material.
17. **World map looks like a debug view.** ui_map, centre. A blocky near-black map inside an ornate frame. Fix: new painting.

## 5. The five best-looking shots

1. **crosspath_street_low.** Warm low sunset, long soft shadows, painted textures on every surface, sprites sitting in the same light as the buildings. This is the target for the whole world.
2. **dungeon_D-HOG.** Clear value structure (dark cliffs, bright path), a warm and cool palette with one accent red, and dense storytelling detail at every node.
3. **battle_zone_refusemancer_start.** The board painting matches the zone, cards read cleanly against it, and frame, cards and board share one saturation level.
4. **ui_title.** One focal point, controlled palette, rich materials. It sets a bar the 3D world needs to approach.
5. **crosspath_square_low.** Same strengths as the street: prop density with clear walkable ground, a readable silhouette against the sky, nothing flat or faceted in frame.

Runner-up within the weaker zones: dump_mid2_low (painted trees, saturated but controlled light, real density; only the pink statue and blades let it down).

What they share: a limited palette with one accent, a directional warm light with visible soft shadows, no surface without painted texture, and density that still leaves the path clear. Pull the Capital and D.N.A. toward that lighting model first.

## 6. Capital before vs after

The city does change, but it does not read as a celebration. What changes after the boss falls: windows go from blue to glowing cream and the whole city is washed to pale white-gold; the gold statues are toppled into brown rubble (gate_plaza, castle_door); the fountain is dry (gate_plaza); the smiley on the palace is covered by a flat navy rectangle (gate_plaza, palace_approach); the purple crystals and corruption are gone (grave_row, hungry_quarter); there are fewer courtiers in the streets.

Net effect: emptier, paler and partly broken. It looks like the morning after a siege, shot overexposed. The before state, with gold statues, running water, a blue sky and crowds, is the more festive of the two. (The after set has only five locations.)

What would sell a celebration:
- **Crowds.** More townsfolk than before, in varied sprites, facing each other, not fewer.
- **Colour added, not removed.** Bunting and streamers across the avenues, confetti or petals in the air and on the ground, coloured banners replacing the navy rectangle.
- **Water back on.** The fountain running, with the toppled statues cleared or replaced by something the people put up.
- **Warm, controlled light.** A golden-hour key with real shadows, like Crosspath, instead of a bloom wash; lanterns lit.
- **Signs of people taking the city back.** Market stalls and food tables in the squares, hand-painted portraits in place of the placeholders, open doors and lit interiors.
- **A changed sky.** The before sky is already clear blue, so the after sky needs its own look (sunset, fireworks or lantern release).
