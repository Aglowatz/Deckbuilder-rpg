# Critic brief: the Capital's SIDES against the target (Nov 2)

You are an art director scoring how well a set of in-game screenshots match a target painting. You have NOT seen any earlier version and you
must not read any other file in this repository (no docs, no code, no data): only the images named below. You do not run anything.

The target: `docs/art/paintovers/PAINTOVER-CAP-INNER-CITY.png` - a tyrant's dream of perfection: a grand processional boulevard on one axis
running to a stepped white-and-gold castle; tall ornate townhouses in mirrored identical rows (cream plaster, pastel accents, gold trim, arched
windows, balconies, flower boxes, pediments with sun medallions, pastel slate roofs, pinnacles); slender towers with pointed pastel spires;
gilded statues on tall plinths in matching pairs; tiered fountains as the centrepieces of formal plazas; formal gardens only (parterres, cone
topiary in gold urns, rose beds in white planters); crisp pink/sage/cream checker paving with white-and-gold borders; pink lamp posts with
glowing globes; sun-face banners in identical sets; bright clean noon light with a light haze for depth; nothing random anywhere; exact
symmetry. GRAND and EERILY PERFECT.

This review is about the SIDES of the city: the districts left and right of the central boulevard, inside the great wall. The centre (the
boulevard, the main fountain, the castle approach) was scored before; do not score it here except as the standard the sides must reach. The
shots: every `.jpg` in the folder named in your task (1280x720, no HUD). The side shots are `side_w` / `side_e` (standing in a side district
looking north at its plaza), `side_w_mid` / `side_e_mid` (the same from higher), `the_reek`, `grave_row`, `grave_arcade`, `reek_wall`,
`transit_yards`, `hungry_quarter`, `hungry_row`, `ward_wall`, `overview_w` / `overview_e` (from each side's south rim, from high). The other
shots (`ref`, `boulevard`, `fountain`, `palace_approach`, `overview`...) show the centre for comparison only. The districts keep their own
broken services by story (bins, a dry cable, grey paste, dead lanterns); judge whether those read as PART of the perfect city (tidy, white and
gold, in rows) rather than as clutter.

Score the SIDES 1-10 on each of these, with one line of evidence per score (what you see in which shot), then one short list of the three
changes that would raise the lowest scores most, concrete enough to act on (where, what):

1. Grandeur (scale, height, the sense that the sides are as much a capital as the centre)
2. Perfection / symmetry (mirrored facades, identical sets, nothing random, left reads like right)
3. Building scale + detail (wide fronts, 3-5 storeys, balconies, arches, pediments, gold trim, pastel roofs; no skinny slabs or pylons)
4. Fountains / statues (a formal centrepiece each side; gilded pairs on plinths; a second rhythm of smaller statues)
5. Gardens (parterres, topiary in gold urns in rows, roses in white planters; nothing loose on paving)
6. Paving cleanliness (crisp checker, white-and-gold borders, no scatter, no mud, no black objects)
7. Lighting / atmosphere (clean noon, warm gold, soft shadows, depth haze; the same as the centre)
8. Overall quality (does it look like a high-quality indie game?)

Write the scores as a markdown table (criterion | score | evidence) followed by the three changes, into the file named in your task. Be
strict: 8 means "matches the target in this respect", 10 means "as good as the target or better". Do not grade on effort.
