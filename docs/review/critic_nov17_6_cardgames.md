# Critic 6 - the new card games (Nov 17)

Read `docs/review/critic_nov17_common.md` first (the rules, the commands, the report). Then this brief. Nothing else.

**Your lens:** the small card games inside the four Path zones, built yesterday and never played by a person. Each zone has one host's card
game and a teacher's **card quiz**:

| Zone (`dev travel zone ...`) | Card game (host) | Quiz (teacher) |
|---|---|---|
| the Gainlands (`beefcake`) | **Reps** (Chet Thunderabs) | Professor Pump |
| the Endless Buffet (`gourmand`) | **Order Up** (Chef Flambe) | Professor Pate |
| the Verdant Dump (`refusemancer`) | **Sort It Out** (Recycle Rhonda) | Druid Thistlewick |
| the D.N.A. (`necrocrat`) | **File in Order** (the Mail Room's filing terminal) | Director Ectomund |

Judge them like the side games of a polished RPG (Sea of Stars' Wheels, the Witcher's Gwent, Inscryption's side puzzles): are the rules
clear from the screen alone, is it fun more than once, is the reward worth it, does it look and feel finished, does it work with a
controller?

**Budget: 14 runs, 80 minutes.** Lens name: `cardgames`. Report: `docs/review/critic_nov17/06_cardgames.md`.

## What to play

Start each zone with `dev load bot_refusemancer_capital_ready` then `dev travel zone <id>` (a hero who has freed every zone, so every host
is there), or the zone's own snapshot if your brief run shows that one fails. Find the host and the teacher on the map ("Point me to..."
or walk; `key E` to talk; a host may talk once before opening the game: talk again).

- **Each of the four card games: play it to a win and to a loss** (lose on purpose if you have to). Read its "How to play" first and say
  whether it was enough. Shoot the start, the middle and both result screens.
- **The card quiz in all four zones:** at least one pass and one fail somewhere. Is the header and the question clear?
- **With a controller:** play one whole card game and one quiz with `pad` only (D-pad to move, `pad a` to choose, `pad b` to back out).
- **With the mouse:** the rest.

## Your report also has

- **One line per game:** its own score /10 and the single change that would make it better.
- **Rewards:** what each game paid, and whether it felt worth playing again.
- **Controller:** what worked and what did not, per screen.
