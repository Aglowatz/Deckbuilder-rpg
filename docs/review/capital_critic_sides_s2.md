## Three changes that raise the lowest scores most

1. **Pave the south rim (raises 6, 7, 1).** In both side plazas the ground between the plaza and the south wall is raw dirt in every side shot (`side_w`, `side_e`, both `_mid`, `overview_w`, `overview_e`). Extend the pink/sage/cream checker to the wall base, close it with a white kerb and gold border line, and run a balustrade with a row of white rose planters along the wall foot, like the kerb rows in `hungry_row`. Remove the yellow road stripe seen in `side_e_mid`.

2. **Make the broken services into matching white-and-gold monuments, mirrored (raises 2, 6, 4).** East: cut the three wheels (`transit_yards`, `side_e`) to one pair, half the size, each on a white plinth with gold trim and a sun medallion, placed symmetrically to flank the plaza axis exactly like the statue pair; delete the black cable line (`overview_e`). West: replace the two flat yellow basins (`side_w`, `overview_w`) with the same kind of plinth-mounted pair in the mirrored positions. Everywhere: put the purple crystal corruption inside a round white planter ring with a gold rim on the plaza axis (one per side), not as a black splat off to one side; take the bucket, pot, ladder-frame and loose chair out of `ward_wall`. The districts can keep their story props, but each one needs a plinth, gold trim and a twin.

3. **Give each side plaza a formal garden and a real fountain (raises 5, 4, 1).** Around the lamp ring in `side_w` / `side_e`, lay four parterre quadrants: low rose beds in white planters with gold trim, a cone topiary in a gold urn at each corner, in the same positions on both sides. Raise the side fountain to two tiers with visible water jets and a small gilded figure on top (half the size of `fountain`), and swap the brown lamp posts for the pink ones. In `grave_row` / `grave_arcade`, replace the striped lawn with the same parterre pattern and set the headstones in a straight white-and-gold-edged row behind a low hedge, so they read as a formal garden with monuments rather than a field. Reface `reek_wall` and `ward_wall` with the Hungry Quarter townhouse front (arched windows, balconies, pediments, pink roof) so the sides stop reading as slabs.
