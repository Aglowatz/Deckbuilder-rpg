# Critic C4b - the finale (Primm's Castle, Primm, ending)

## 1. Summary

The finale looks and reads like a game that knows exactly what it wants to be. The Capital approach, the hand-painted castle node map, the gate scene where the Rescuer turns out to be Elowen Vey and gets dragged off, and Primm's Model Room monologue are the best things I saw. Primm is a properly written villain: a tidy, polite tyrant who wants every unit to be a 3/3, and whose phase-2 line "Why must anything ever be five?" ties the boss rule straight into the theme. Mechanically the finale falls apart. Primm's two rules together (every unit is 3/3, and at most two non-infrastructure cards a turn) clog the hand with more cards than you can play, so a forced end-of-turn "Too many cards - choose 1 card to toss" step appears early. That step stalled my hand-written turns and also stalled the game's own built-in pilot on round 2 (`pilot: stuck on turn 4`, the fight never moved again). Phase 1 ends on a full "VICTORY! You defeated Primm - Phase 1: Standardization" screen, which leaks the internal phase name and kills the tension. In the time I had, I never reached the ending or the credits, so I can't judge them. The castle's ordinary fights pay 20 gold and 30 XP each, which makes them feel like busywork at level 20. Nothing pointed me to the Crease: no NPC, map marker, quest line or node name mentioned it on the path I walked.

**How I played.** 22 runs (`c4b_r1` to `c4b_r22`), about 100 minutes of wall clock. Path: Beefcake, level 20, 1,330 gold, deck "Beefcake deck" (47 cards, 19 infrastructure). I opened no shop: no shop showed up on the castle route, and I had no time to go back into the Capital, so I bought nothing. Dev commands used:
- `dev load c4_approved` at the start of every run.
- `dev save c4b_door` once. It's useless: a load puts you back at the Capital's Gate Road with full HP, not at the castle door.
- `dev travel dungeon D-PC` from run 8 on (6 times), because walking to the door doesn't always work (see finding 6).
- `dev travel dungeon D-PC -2` 10 times to reach Primm within the time limit.
- `dev save c4b_after` once (snapshot only, not reused).

No win, level or gold commands. I walked in by foot twice (r3, r6/r7: about 45 s from the Gate Road, and Tidy-Bots took 4-8 of my 23 HP on the way). By hand I played 3 castle nodes: Courtyard of Perfect Hedges (fight), Grand Foyer (event) and the gate story scene. The other castle fights I left to `pilot`. **Primm by hand:** the play driver runs a fixed script and can't react mid-fight, and the shuffle isn't deterministic, so I couldn't replay a known opening. My two hand attempts (r16, r20) used scripted clicks and drags. Both got stuck in Primm's turn flow by round 2-3: an "End your turn?" prompt, then the toss step. That isn't a loss, but it isn't a win either. A `pilot` attempt (r21) beat phase 1 in 8 rounds (I ended on 18 of 31 HP) and reached the phase-2 mirror monologue, then the run ran out of time. A second pilot attempt (r22) got stuck on round 2 at the same toss step. So I did not see phase 2 played out, the ending or the credits.

## 3. Findings

1. **Primm's fight gets stuck in an end-of-turn discard step, and even the built-in pilot can't get past it.** With "at most two non-infrastructure cards a turn" plus normal draws, the hand overflows by round 2-3. The turn then stops on "Too many cards / Choose 1 card to toss" with a disabled `Toss (0/1)` button. Space does nothing, and the only instruction is in the small phase panel on the right. In r22 the game's own pilot logged `the turn is not moving; passing the rest of it` and then `stuck on turn 4`, and the fight never advanced again (Primm 56, me 31). In r20 my scripted turns sat in the same state for about 5 minutes. Where: `c4b_r22_q03.png`, `c4b_r20_t3_b.png`. **Severity: blocker** for a scripted or new player, **major** for a human who works out "click a card, then Toss". **Fix:** let Space or End Turn toss the oldest or lowest-value card automatically, or make the overflow a soft cap (discard at end of turn without a prompt). Put a big centred prompt ("Hand full: click a card to toss") over the hand, not in the side panel. Consider giving Primm's 2-card rule +1 card per turn to the player, or a smaller draw, so the boss's signature rule doesn't become a chore.

2. **Phase 1 ends on a full victory screen with the internal name.** "VICTORY! You defeated Primm - Phase 1: Standardization in 8 rounds." with a Continue button, the same screen as a hedge-trimmer guard. The battle HUD also shows "Primm - Phase 1: Standardization" in the action log and above his nameplate. Where: `c4b_r21_p01.png`. **Severity: major** (it deflates the finale's biggest beat). **Fix:** no victory panel between phases. Shatter the board, play Primm's "Why will they not all be three?" line straight away, and show "PHASE 2: THE MIRROR" as a title card. Show his name as just "Primm" (with a phase pip if needed).

3. **Nothing points to the Crease.** On the Capital avenue, the castle map's main line (Courtyard, Foyer, Gallery, Mirrors, Ministry, Safe Room, Archive, Royal Stair) and in every dialogue I read, nothing mentions a hidden part of the Capital. The quest log only says "Storm Primm's castle". The second tracked quest is "A Pinch of Everything", with its marker at the Road Back to Crosspath. The map shows a dark, unexplored Capital apart from the avenue strip. Where: `c4b_r1_02_map.png`, `c4b_r13_01_enter.png`. **Severity: major** if the story needs it. **Fix:** have the Resistance Safe Room node (or Elowen's last line at the gate) name the Crease and add a map pin or a tracked-quest step for it. A cracked-wall node on the castle map with a tooltip would also work.

4. **Castle fights reward almost nothing at level 20.** Courtyard guard: "+20 gold, +30 XP". There are about 25 nodes ("Twenty-odd rooms of portraits and good intentions") between you and Primm, and every one is "???". Where: `c4b_r11_n1_c.png`, `c4b_r13_01_enter.png`. **Severity: major** (pacing: the last dungeon feels like padding). **Fix:** shorten the main line to about 6 rooms with optional side rooms, show node types once a neighbour is cleared, and raise castle rewards (gold sized to the shop curve, a card pick, or a relic) so the finale crawl has stakes.

5. **"Enter the castle" popup is titled "???" and states rules that no longer apply.** The title is "???", and the text says "Your zone's broken-service debuffs apply to every duel" while the HUD says "Services restored: 4 of 4". Where: `c4b_r3_06_e.png`. **Severity: minor-major** (it's confusing right at the threshold). **Fix:** title it "Primm's Castle". Drop or condition the debuff sentence on services still broken. Ideally say what the freed services give you instead.

6. **Walking to the castle door is unreliable and costs HP.** The palace avenue's fountain blocks the straight line (r2: stuck at z 62.9 for 15 s). Walking around it sometimes lands you at the door (r3, r4) and sometimes not (r7: ended at the Gate Plaza beside the guard). Tidy-Bots knock you down for -2 HP each time ("A Tidy-Bot knocks you flat. Please remain orderly. (-2 HP)"), so I entered the final dungeon at 19/23. Where: `c4b_r1_05_walk.png`, `c4b_r2_10_in.png`. **Severity: major** (HP carries into the castle). **Fix:** leave a clear lane past the fountain, or point the goal arrow round it. Turn the Tidy-Bots off (or make them harmless) once the gate exam is passed, at least on the avenue.

7. **Clicking a castle node you can't reach does nothing.** Clicking 458/632 or 593/472 before they were open gave no feedback (r11, r12). The only hint is a slightly brighter rim on the reachable nodes. Where: `c4b_r13_01_enter.png`. **Severity: minor-major.** **Fix:** pulse the reachable nodes, and on a click on an unreachable one show "Not reachable yet - clear an adjacent room".

8. **Narration is shown under a character's nameplate.** "The Model Room is round and domed..." and "A man in a pressed grey coat..." appear under **PRIMM, "HIS PERFECTION"**. The leaders' arrival narration ("Grandmaster Flex steps out from behind a pillar...") is under **THE FREED LEADERS**. Where: `c4b_r14_02_click.png`, `c4b_r14_i05.png`. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** use the nameless narration box (as in the gate scene, `c4b_r6_s02.png`).

9. **The narration doesn't match the art.** The text says "a man in a pressed grey coat and a thin gold circlet stands with his back to you". The Model Room still shows a blond man in white-and-gold robes, facing you, eyes closed. Where: `c4b_r15_j7.png`. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** rewrite to "a man in white and gold, bent over the table with tweezers", or repaint.

10. **The freed leaders' blessings are mostly useless to my Path.** As a pure Beefcake I get "Gourmand units get +1 defense", "Necrocrat cards cost 1 less" and "Refusemancer units get +2 defense", and Flex's "+1 attack" is then overwritten by Primm's "every unit starts as 3/3" (buffs still apply, so it's kept, but the screen doesn't make that clear). Where: the "Before the fight: Primm" panel, dump in `c4b_r15` play.log. **Severity: minor** (it's a nice emotional beat, but the payoff is flat). **Fix:** have each leader give a buff keyed to the player's own Paths, or a universal effect.

11. **The "ENEMY TURN" banner overlaps the action log line.** "Primm - Phase 1: Standardization plays Feastforge" runs underneath the big red "ENEMY TURN" text, and both are unreadable. Where: `c4b_r16_b02.png`. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** move the action toast below the banner, or delay it until the banner fades.

12. **The card tooltip sticks over the hand after a play.** After clicking Powerhouse, its rules tooltip (Exhaust / Iron) stays in the middle of the hand and covers 3-4 cards until the mouse moves well away. Where: `c4b_r16_b06.png`, `c4b_r16_b09.png`. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** hide hover previews when a card leaves the hand.

13. **"End your turn?" fires while the player still has a legal play.** The check is fair, but under Primm's rule it said "You have 1 energy left and can still play 2 cards this turn" on round 2, which nudges new players into the overflow trap in finding 1. Where: `c4b_r20_t2_a.png`. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** fine as is; just pair it with the finding-1 fix.

14. **Small text problems.** The skip prompt reads "Skip this scene? / Skip this scene? You haven't seen it." (title repeated in the body). Mid-scene lines say "E / Click to close" when it continues. Victory screens print both "VICTORY!" (banner) and "Victory!" (panel). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** de-duplicate the strings and use "continue" except on the last line.

15. **`dev save` keeps the zone but not the position.** A snapshot saved at the castle door loaded at the Gate Road, 60 m away, with HP reset to full. Noted for the tooling (players don't see `dev`), but the same rule may apply to the real save: check where a normal save inside the Capital puts you. **Severity: minor (tooling).**

No `SCRIPT ERROR` appeared in any of the 22 runs' `stdout.log`. Only the usual shutdown leak warnings (RID and PagedAllocator), every time on exit.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **Primm's writing and concept.** A polite, exhausted perfectionist who thanks his staff "daily, in writing", wants everyone to be a 3/3, and fights you as yourself in phase 2 ("Everything you can do, I can do... Let us see how you like fighting yourself."). The rule is the theme. Keep every line.
- **The battle board is the Model Room's miniature Patchwork.** Fighting on top of his toy kingdom is the best staging idea in the game (`c4b_r16_b02.png`).
- **The Model Room still** (`c4b_r15_j7.png`) and the **gate scene still** with Elowen, the guards and Percival Plume (`c4b_r6_s02.png`). Lovely, readable, on-tone.
- **The gate scene reveal.** "Don't look so surprised. Somebody has to watch the door while you knock it down." / "Elowen. Her name is Elowen, and all that way I never once asked her." Short, earned and sad.
- **The castle node map art** (`c4b_r6_s17.png`): each room is a painted diorama (library, kitchens, clock tower, mirror hall), and the red carpet runs up to the Model Room.
- **The "Before the fight" rules panel** lists every rule, blessing, Primm's Standing Army and Setting Up turns, and even flags a hidden trait. That's the clarity tool the rest of the fight needs.
- **Event nodes with real choices**: Grand Foyer's "heal 3 / 50 gold with a -1 max HP curse / +1 defense boon" (`c4b_r12_n2_a.png`) is a good three-way choice.
- **Captain Spotless**: "You are approved. I have always supported this outcome. Please remember that exact wording." Perfect.
