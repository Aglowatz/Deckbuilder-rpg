## Your brief

1. Read **the main story in order** (Prologue -> Crosspath -> the four zones and their dungeons -> the Capital and Primm's Castle ->
   Ending), then **a sample of 10 side quests** (pick them from the zones, the town and the postgame; name them).
2. Judge:
   - **coherence** - is the kingdom's story clear and consistent; do the mysteries (who the Wanderer is, what Primm wants) build and pay off;
   - **emotional beats** - where they land and where they fall flat (cite the lines);
   - **voice** - is each main character's voice distinct (name the main cast and say, for each, whether you could tell their lines apart
     without the speaker name);
   - **humour** - does it work, does it repeat, does it undercut moments that should be serious;
   - **clarity** - does dialogue tell the player what to do next without sounding like a tutorial.
3. List **the 10 weakest moments**, each with the line IDs, why it is weak, and a **rewrite direction** (what the moment needs; not a full
   rewrite).
