# Critic C3: the midgame (Nov 6)

## 1. Summary

The midgame of The Wayward Path looks lavish and is written with warmth. The dungeon maps, boss stills and NPC portraits are as good as anything in the genre. But the difficulty curve breaks in two directions. The Buffet's gatekeeper (Chef Fennel) fell in 7 rounds without scratching me (21/21 HP). Then both main-dungeon bosses, The Doppelganger and Compostella, flattened the same deck in exactly 5 rounds (23 and 29 damage taken against 21 HP). Neither boss fight was close: I left the Doppelganger at full HP 16 and Compostella at 19 of 25. Between fights the game spends too much time on friction. You walk around a town whose map is still mostly black at level 13, wrestle with an interaction priority that sends you to a signpost instead of the shop beside it, and click through 13 lines to reach a crafting screen. Opening packs gives you a paragraph of card names and no reveal. The story does build: the Grand Chef unmasking as a Doppelganger that serves "Primm's Perfection", and a druid possessed by the Dump itself, made me want the next zone. The systems around the fights mostly leave that pull unspent.

**How I played.** I did 21 scripted runs (c3r1 to c3r21) in about 95 minutes of wall clock, as the snapshot's level-13 Beefcake. Shops: I had 600 gold at the start, then 575 after Alembic's 100-gold brew (+75 gold quest reward), then 645 after the Fennel quest step, then 625 after the Buffet's "dish duty fee" for losing. At Auntie Alembic I could afford the brew (6+6 starter essence and 100 gold) and got The Ghost Express Depot (Epic). I opened the snapshot's 12 ordinary packs with "Open all"; I never got the 2 gilded packs open. **Dev commands:**
- `dev save/load` to chain runs.
- `dev encounter Fennel`, to fight the Buffet gatekeeper without walking there. My first walk to the pantry door turned into a fight with town navigation. That fight pays nothing, so I then used `dev gate gourmand` to mark him beaten.
- `dev encounter Moss` plus `dev gate refusemancer` for the Dump. The `Moss` query actually started a "Hollow Stalker" fight, so **I never fought Moss Mulligan**.
- `dev travel zone gourmand/refusemancer` (twice).
- `dev travel dungeon D-TTK -2` and `D-ROT -2`, to go straight to each boss. 7 nodes were auto-cleared each time, so I saw none of the dungeons' ordinary fights or events.
- `dev travel dungeon S-IRON`, to look at the Iron Cave.

No win, autowin, level or gold commands. **Honest caveat:** my hand-played turns mostly failed. Hands differ on every run, and two pre-fight panels (rules, then a boss card) swallowed my blind clicks. So every fight's outcome came from `pilot`. I played roughly two turns of the Fennel fight myself. I fell well short of the four hand-played fights the brief asked for. **Skipped:** the zones' favour quests (both main lines are gated on "finish a favour in the zone"), side quests beyond Alembic's, Pip's shop, a full Iron Cave run, the gilded packs, and Elder Maren's third memory.

## 2. Scores

| | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fun | 5/10 | Lovely boss reveals and maps, but a trivial gatekeeper is followed by bosses that end things in 5 rounds, and too much walking and clicking separates the good parts. |
| Clarity | 6/10 | The quest log, "Where to go" lines, pre-fight rule panels and the "What beat you" recap are excellent. Navigation, interaction priority, the crafting selection and the locked item slots are not. |
| Difficulty | 4/10 (badly judged in both directions) | The gatekeeper is far too easy (won at 21/21 HP). Both main bosses are far too hard for the same deck at the stated level: 5-round wipes, each boss dropping only to about 75 % HP or not at all. There is no middle step. |
| Text | 7/10 | Real voices with jokes that mostly land ("Don't drink it. People drink it."; Old Hob's "Not on my list. Off you pop."), and the boss lines have menace. A few slips: "Four Paths in one card" after a two-Path brew, and a speaker label that spoils the Doppelganger reveal. |
| Look | 7/10 | The painted dungeon maps, boss stills and portraits are top-tier. The zones' walkable 3D is muddier: haze over the Dump, flat placeholder-like blocks in the Buffet, a hero hidden behind roofs in town, and a flat green vector cauldron in an otherwise painted shop. |

## 3. Findings

1. **Main-dungeon bosses are a wall after a trivial gatekeeper.** Chef Fennel lost in 7 rounds and I took no damage (`c3r13_01_result.png`). The Doppelganger then won "after 5 rounds", and I took 23 damage: "Sashimi Weenie dealt 12 of the 23 damage" (`c3r17_05_result.png`). Compostella also won after 5 rounds: "Curbside Rat dealt all 29 damage you took (6 hits)", with her still on 19 of 25 HP. Both bosses have "Setting Up: for its first 2 turns its units do not attack". The kill still comes on round 5, so the boss needs only about three attacking turns. Compostella fields a 4/7 Possum Prowler on turn 1, and her "Wild Growth" buffs every unit each turn. The pilot plays simply, and a human would do better. But the jump from a 21/21 gatekeeper win to never getting a boss below 75 % tells you the curve is wrong. A real player at this level will bounce off, lose gold ("Dish duty fee: -20 gold", `c3r17_06.png`), and go back through the dungeon. **Severity: major.** **Fix:** make the gatekeeper a real check (more HP, a rule twist, a threat that must be answered), and soften the boss stack. Drop one of the four dungeon rules or the +1/+1 or +0/+1 boss rule, or start the boss's buffs one turn later. Then re-sim a typical level-13 deck against both bosses until it wins about 50-60 % on the first try.

2. **At level 13 all 4 item slots are locked, yet the player holds items.** The Character screen shows "EQUIPPED 0 / 4 unlocked … Additional slots unlock as you level up", while the inventory holds 4 item types, including 4 of one (`c3r1_04_char.png`). In battle the four item slots show only greyed padlocks (`c3r12_05_t2a.png`). Either this is a bug in the snapshot or the progression, or the unlock is far too late. Both are bad: items can't help against the boss walls in finding 1. **Severity: major.** **Fix:** check the slot-unlock table. A player at level 13 should have at least 2-3 slots. If 0 is intended, say on the screen which level unlocks the first slot.

3. **Town navigation and interaction priority waste minutes.** At level 13 the Crosspath map is mostly unexplored black, with four disconnected islands (`c3r1_03_map.png`). Walking the 12 world units from the fountain to Alembic took four tries. The hero catches on an invisible block at (32.3, 19.2), and the same key presses gave different results on different runs. Standing between the shop door and a signpost, E always chose the signpost ("East: Market Street … East: The Endless Buffet", run c3r5) even when the alchemist was equally close. The overhead camera often puts the hero behind roofs or as a tiny figure (`c3r4_06.png`). The goal pointer and "Where to?" list help, but you can't actually walk the route they point to. **Severity: major** (it's the midgame's main tax on pacing). **Fix:** give shops and NPCs priority over signposts, and prefer what the hero faces. Reveal the whole town map once Crosspath has been visited (by level 13 you've crossed it many times). Fade the roofs in front of the hero. Consider a "walk to selected goal" or a breadcrumb trail from the Where-to list.

4. **Pack opening has no ceremony, and "Open all" is quietly incomplete.** "Open all (12 packs)" sat above 14 listed packs. It opened 12 and showed one wall of text: "New: Adventurer's Pack, Late-ish Courier, Mirrored Hall, …" (`c3r2_06_open1.png`). The gilded packs stayed behind with no explanation (`c3r2_08_open1c.png`). Four toasts stack over the pack list and its Open buttons. Pokemon TCG Pocket and Slay the Spire both know the reveal is the reward. **Severity: major** (for fun). **Fix:** for a single pack, flip the cards over one at a time and make rares pop. For Open all, show a grid of card faces with "NEW" badges rather than a sentence. Say "Open all (12; gilded packs open one at a time)". Move the toasts so they don't cover the pack list.

5. **The crafting screen eats the first click, then resizes under the cursor.** On entering Alembic's brew screen, the first essence row you click is not selected. Gourmand then Necrocrat left only Necrocrat "✓ Selected" (`c3r7_02_picked.png`), and Beefcake was dropped the same way in c3r8. After the second pick the whole panel rescales: rows move from x=665 to x=611 and the Craft button jumps from y=532 to y=759 (`c3r8_02b.png`). Pressing Enter on a focused row then deselected it. **Severity: major** (a player who can't tell why Craft stays grey will think it's broken). **Fix:** don't spend the first click on dismissing the speech bubble, and keep the layout fixed (reserve the space for the odds grid from the start). Label the button state with something like "Pick one more Path".

6. **A first-time tip covers the "End your turn?" confirm buttons.** The "Tools" tip box sits right over the End Turn / Keep playing buttons of the confirm dialog (`c3r11_03_endconfirm.png`). **Severity: minor-major.** **Fix:** don't show tips while a modal is up (queue them), or anchor the tip away from the modal.

7. **Both main dungeons have the same node graph.** Test Kitchen and Rotheart: 12 nodes, the same lattice, start bottom-left, boss top-right, and "-2" is node 12 with "7 cleared on the way" in both (`c3r15_06_nearboss.png`, `c3r18_05_rot.png`). The painted art differs beautifully, but you'll feel the shape repeat by the fourth zone. **Severity: minor-major** (variety). **Fix:** give each main dungeon its own topology (a loop, a fork with a locked shortcut, a gauntlet), or a signature node type that only that zone has.

8. **The speaker label spoils the Grand Chef reveal.** The very first narration line, "The Grand Chef turns from the stove wearing the smile from every portrait…", is captioned "THE DOPPELGANGER" before the mask comes off (`c3r16_01_boss.png`). The unmasking still (`c3r16_04.png`) is one of the best moments I saw, and the label steals it. **Severity: minor** (story impact is high). **Fix:** caption her "The Grand Chef" or "???" until the line where she peels the face off.

9. **Wrong number in Alembic's line.** After a Beefcake + Necrocrat brew she says "There. Four Paths in one card. Don't tell it it's unusual." (run c3r9, `c3r9_02_crafted.png`). It was two Paths. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** "Two Paths in one card…", or insert the Path names.

10. **Getting to the crafting screen takes 13 dialogue lines.** On the first visit, Alembic's lore ("the Wellspring that fed all four Paths…") is charming, but a player who came to craft has to tap through every line. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** after the first two lines, offer "Brew now / Tell me more".

11. **The pre-fight panel and boss card double up before every boss.** A rules panel ("Before the fight: …", `c3r19_04.png`), then a boss name card ("Grand Chef of the Test Kitchen (allegedly)"), then the opening-hand popup. That's three modal steps before turn 1, on top of a two-part boss conversation (a map dialogue, then a full-screen still where one line repeats across two presses). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** merge the rules into the boss card, and let one press skip both.

12. **Hover shows the same card three times at once.** Hovering a hand card brings up a large preview top-left, a raised copy in the hand and a keyword tooltip, all showing the same text (`c3r20_04_t2played.png`). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** keep the raised copy plus a keyword tooltip, and drop the far-left duplicate (or the reverse).

13. **Walkable zones look rough next to the painted assets.** The Dump's "Compost Commons" sits under a heavy purple-sepia haze that flattens contrast (`c3r18_04_dump.png`). The Buffet entrance mixes painted broccoli trees with flat untextured blocks and a blank grey signboard (`c3r15_02.png`). The crafting shop's green vector cauldron icon clashes with the painted Alembic and backdrop (`c3r6_04.png`). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** cut the zone fog density or lift its black level. Texture or replace the box primitives. Use a painted cauldron.

14. **On defeat, the zone banner and the dialogue fire together.** After losing the boss you land in the Buffet, where "THE ENDLESS BUFFET / Home of the Gourmand Path" plays on top of Old Hob's dialogue box (`c3r17_06.png`). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** skip the zone title card when you come back from a defeat, or play it after the dialogue.

15. **Stray glyph labels in dumps.** Labels like "YZ abcdefghi", "vwxyz 012345" and "6789 .,:;!?'" show up at the top-left in town (runs c3r1 and c3r13). They look like font-warmup labels, and none showed in my screenshots. **Severity: minor** (harmless if they're never visible). **Fix:** make sure they're off-screen or freed after warmup.

No `SCRIPT ERROR` lines in any of my 21 runs' `stdout.log`.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The dungeon maps.** Test Kitchen, Rotheart and Iron Cave are gorgeous painted isometric boards, each with a strong identity (`c3r15_06_nearboss.png`, `c3r18_05_rot.png`, `c3r21_01_iron.png`).
- **Boss stills and reveals.** The Doppelganger peeling off the Grand Chef's face (`c3r16_04.png`), and Compostella's "I... am... THE... DUMP… I am what GROWS BACK… and I am HUNGRY." These moments make you want the next zone.
- **The "What beat you" defeat recap.** It names the card, the damage share and a concrete tip (`c3r17_05_result.png`). Keep it, and add it to close wins too.
- **Pre-fight rules panel and dungeon Rules (R).** The rules are clear and stated up front, including "Setting Up: watch what it builds" and the honest "Hidden trait ?".
- **Quest log quality.** Each quest has a "Where to go" line, a reward preview and tracking in the HUD (`c3r1_02_quests.png`).
- **Alembic's brew odds grid.** Every possible result card is shown with its percentage before you pay (`c3r8_02b.png`). Exemplary.
- **NPC voice and humour.** "Don't drink it. People drink it." "Hm? You? Not on my list. Off you pop. Heh." The battle barks too ("TASTE IT! Even the BISCUIT is for the war effort!").
- **Battle board art.** The picnic-blanket table for the Buffet and the forest floor for the Dump give every fight a place.
