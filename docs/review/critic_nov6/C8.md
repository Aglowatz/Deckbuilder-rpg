# C8 - Balance and economy critic (desk review with the game's simulators)

## 1. Summary

The middle of the curve works. From the D.N.A. onward, ordinary fights sit at 90-100 %, and the elites and bosses mostly land in or near their bands for three of the four Paths. Each Path also feels clearly stronger in its own home zone, which is a nice touch. The ends of the curve are where it breaks. **The Shattered Gallery's full run is close to unbeatable: 1 clear in 40 runs, all Paths together** (Necrocrat 1/10, the other three 0/10), and the deaths pile up at the third wing (the Doppelganger, Hortensia and Rotheart Echoes). Those are two "Normal" nodes and one Elite, yet they are the hardest fights in the game. **The first main-dungeon boss, Chancellor Clench, is a wall for the Gourmand** (0 % in 8 games, 17 % in 12). **Beefcake falls off from mid-game**: it is the strongest Path at level 3 and the weakest from level 10 on (Grimsby 33 %, Rotheart 33 %, Siphon 17 %, Betty Bones 8 %). Necrocrat runs the other way: rough in the Gainlands (elites 30-40 %), then it flattens everything (Primm 100/80/100). Primm is too easy for every Path. **Refusemancer has the roughest start**: cave clear 79 %, the first gatekeeper (Brick Bronson) 25 %, the Restless Dead 38 %. ZoneOrderEase does turn too-hard first dungeons into winnable ones (17-58 % without it, 67-92 % with it), but it works through foe HP alone, and that is a clumsy lever. It cuts the Dump's foes to 0.36 HP and still leaves 67 %.

**What I ran.** All runs were headless, one Godot process at a time, seed 7000, with the expected player from `ProgressionModel`. Outputs are under `docs/review/critic_nov6/C8_sims/`. Wall time was about 1 h 40 min of simulation (22:15-23:52).

## 3. Findings (worst first)

1. **The Shattered Gallery is effectively unbeatable: 1 clear in 40 full runs.**
   - **What.** Clears by Path: Necrocrat 10 %, Beefcake 0 %, Gourmand 0 %, Refusemancer 0 %. Runs die mostly at the third wing: the Doppelganger Echo (node 9) 13 times, the Hortensia Echo (node 10) 12 times, the Rotheart Echo (node 11) 3 times. A few die at Plume (node 5) or Grimsby (node 7), and the Gourmand dies 3 times at the Perfect Copy.
   - **Single fights from full HP** (Beefcake / Necrocrat / Gourmand / Refusemancer): node 9 is 25/80/63/17 against an 80-100 target; node 10 is **0**/70/50/**0** against 80-100; node 11 is 25/80/-/50 against 60-75; the boss is 33/50/-/83.
   - **Why.** The wing rules stack: Silvered gives +1/+0, Reflected Order adds +1 cost to your first card, and Perfect Form copies your first unit for the enemy. By the third wing all three apply on top of each Echo's own rule. Hortensia's Echo adds a Topiary every second turn with +1/+1 tokens, and that compounds with Perfect Form. Meanwhile nodes 4 and 7 (Clench and Grimsby Echoes, the Elites) score 88-100 %, because their signature cards are being dropped (see finding 6).
   - **Where.** `C8_sims/seq_gallery_*.md`; rows S-GALLERY:9/10/11/13 in `all_paths_table.md`; `data/dungeons/dungeon_content.json` → `S-GALLERY.foes` 9/10/11; `data/encounters/modifiers.json` → "Echo of Hortensia Clipwell", "Echo of the Rotheart".
   - **Severity.** Blocker (for the postgame's headline content).
   - **Fix.** Pick one of two options:
     - (a) Stop stacking Perfect Form on the third wing's Normal fights. Apply it only to the Elite and the boss.
     - (b) Keep the rules and cut Echo HP for nodes 9 and 10 from 20 to 14. Drop Hortensia's `token_stats` from +1/+1 to +0/+1, and slow its Topiary from every 2 turns to every 3. Lower the Rotheart Echo's `start_of_turn_heal` from 2 to 1.
     - Also swap node kinds so the hardest fight is labelled Elite. Then re-run the sequence; aim for 30-50 % clears per Path.

2. **Chancellor Clench (first main-dungeon boss) is a wall for the Gourmand.**
   - **What.** Gourmand wins 0 % (8 games) and 17 % (12 games, `one_D-HOG_13.md`; first-player wins 17 %) against a 40-60 target, at L8. Refusemancer gets 38-42 %. The Gourmand's other Gainlands fights are fine (normals 95 %). Its starter cards show no positive impact: G-01 +2, G-06 -2, G-08 -2, C-03 -7.
   - **Where.** Row D-HOG:13; `C8_sims/one_D-HOG_13.md`; `modifiers.json` → "Chancellor Clench, Iron Regent" (2 starting Iron, a T-21 every 5 turns); `dungeon_content.json` → `D-HOG.boss`.
   - **Context.** The early study sends the Gourmand to the Buffet first, so this wall bites when a Gourmand follows the default zone order, or comes to the Gainlands second without much new gear.
   - **Severity.** Blocker for the Gourmand.
   - **Fix.** Cut Clench's starting Iron from 2 to 1, or his boss HP by about 15 %. Alternatively, give the Gourmand starter one more cheap blocker against Iron-pumped attackers. Re-check that Beefcake (67 %) does not leave its band.

3. **Beefcake's curve is inverted: strongest at level 3, weakest from level 10.**
   - **What.** The gatekeepers are 75-92 % for Beefcake, all above band. The bosses then collapse:

     | Boss | Level | Beefcake | Other three Paths | Target |
     |---|---:|---:|---|---:|
     | Grimsby | 11 | 33 % | 75-90 % | 40-60 % |
     | Compostella the Rotheart | 17 | 33 % | 50-100 % | 40-60 % |
     | Siphon without Elowen | 25 | 17 % | 50-63 % | 40-60 % |
     | Betty Bones | 10 | 8 % | 30-67 % | 40-60 % |
     | Gallery Hortensia Echo | 26 | 0 % | | 80-100 % |

   - **Dead cards.** The card-impact "drag" lists keep naming Beefcake's own mid-game cards as losers. **B-23 Snack Bar** appears 14 times, average -12. **B-24 Overclocked Mill Worker** appears 9 times, average -11. B-12 Leg Day Survivor averages -14, B-05 Hamster Wheel Runner -10 and B-15 Kettlebell -9. Snack Bar's "Pay 1: +1/+0 until end of turn, once per unit" is too expensive to matter at L10+.
   - **Where.** `enc_all_beefcake.md`, "Outside the target band" list; `data/cards/B-23.tres`, `B-24.tres`, `B-12.tres`.
   - **Severity.** Major.
   - **Fix.** Pick one or both:
     - Rework Snack Bar to cost 1 with "Pay 1: +2/+0", or make it free once per turn.
     - Give B-24 Overclocked Mill Worker +1 defense (to 2/4) or +1 attack.
     - Then raise Beefcake's reference deck around L10-17: add a removal card such as B-36 Desk Flip, which is positive (+8) where it appears.
   - Separately, trim the Brick Bronson and Moss Mulligan gatekeepers by about 1 HP each only if Beefcake still sits above 85 % after the fix.

4. **Necrocrat snowballs: rough in the Gainlands, then over-tuned for the rest of the game.**
   - **Gainlands (rough).** Elites 30-40 %: Vivienne Quota (D-HOG:6) 40 %, Trophy Hall Honor Guard (D-HOG:11) 30 %, Cell Block Brawler (normal) 50 %. Chef Fennel gatekeeper 40 %, Moss Mulligan 50 %.
   - **L11 onward (over-tuned).** The Path is at or above the top of every band: D.N.A. / Buffet / Dump bosses 90 / 90 / **100**; Capital elites 93 %; **Primm 100 / 80 / 100**; Lab elites 100 %. It is the only Path to clear the Gallery even once.
   - **Where.** Necrocrat column of `all_paths_table.md`; `enc_necrocrat_*.md`.
   - **Severity.** Major. The late game has no tension for the most popular "control" Path, and the first zone may make players quit it.
   - **Fix.** For the start: Trophy Hall Honor Guard begins with 1 Militia token (`modifiers.json`); drop that token, or give the Necrocrat starter one more early blocker. For the late game: the Necrocrat reference/progression deck seems to scale too well, so look at the cards with positive drag, N-01 Skeleton Clerk (+1.2 average, 10 times) and N-15 (+13 vs Clench). A cleaner lever is the boss side: give Primm phases 1 and 3 about +15 % HP across the board (see finding 5).

5. **Primm is too easy for every Path.**
   - **What.** Phase 1 is 75-100 % and phase 3 is 50-100 % (Beefcake 50 is the only in-band phase-3 result), against a 40-60 target. The arena tier-3 Announcer (67-100 %) is harder than the final boss for the Gourmand and the Necrocrat. The run's climax lands below several side-dungeon bosses: Automaton 13-42 %, Betty 8-67 %, Swolympus 42-67 %.
   - **Where.** Rows D-PC:10:phase1/2/3; `modifiers.json` → "Primm (standardization)", which only adds 4 starting Militia (T-16).
   - **Severity.** Major.
   - **Fix.** Give phase 1 a stat rule (+1/+1 on his units) rather than tokens alone. Raise phase-3 HP by about 20 %. Target 50 / 45 / 40 % across the three phases.

6. **Gallery Echoes lose their signature cards (data bug).**
   - **What.** Every Gallery sequence logs `ZoneDecks: NB-16 is enemy-only and Echo of Chancellor Clench is not its owner; dropped` (60 times). The same happens for N-33 on the Echo of Mortimer Grimsby (52 times). The two Echoes that are meant to be the first and second wing's Elites are therefore playing stock decks, and they score 88-100 % against a 60-75 target. This explains part of the Gallery's back-loaded difficulty.
   - **Where.** `C8_sims/gallery_dropped_cards.txt`; `dungeon_content.json` → `S-GALLERY.foes.4.extra {"NB-16":1}` and `foes.7.extra {"N-32":1, "N-33":1}`; the cards' `availability` / `availability_source` in `data/cards/NB-16.tres` and `N-33.tres`.
   - **Severity.** Major (silent content loss).
   - **Fix.** Add the Echo names as allowed owners of NB-16 and N-33 (or let an Echo inherit its original's ownership). Then re-run the Gallery; nodes 4 and 7 should drop to about 65-75 %.

7. **Side-dungeon bosses are spikes far above their level.**
   - **Betty Bones (S-NECRO:3, L10).** Wins of 8 / 30 / 63 / 67 % against a 40-60 target. Her "Surly" rule gives +3/+1 to all her units at L10. The main-dungeon bosses near that level only get +1/+0 to +1/+1 (Grimsby, the Doppelganger).
   - **The Old Kingdom Automaton (S-TOWN:3, L7).** 42 / 30 / **13** / **17** %. Before it, the Gourmand also drops to 75 % on the trash (S-TOWN:1/2).
   - **Colonel Crumb (S-PASTE:3).** Refusemancer gets 17 %.
   - **Where.** Those rows; `modifiers.json` → "Betty Bones, Surly Secretary", "The Old Kingdom Automaton"; `dungeon_content.json` → `S-TOWN.foes.3` (hp 23 with C-32 and C-33 extras).
   - **Severity.** Major. These are optional, but a no-grind player meets them on the way, and a 10 % wall reads as a bug.
   - **Fix.**
     - Betty: Surly from +3/+1 to +2/+1, or HP from 36 to 30.
     - Automaton: drop one of its two extras (C-33), or cut HP from 23 to 19.
     - Re-run aiming for 40-55 %.

8. **Refusemancer has the roughest start.**
   - **Tutorial and Crosspath.** The whole cave clears 79 % (the other Paths 92-96 %), with 3 losses at the Warden and 2 at the Mossy Gallery. Single fights: Warden 75 %, Store Room Ghosts 75 %.
   - **First zone, in the default order.** The Brick Bronson gatekeeper is **25 %**. The Restless Dead is 38 % and Clench 38 %. D.N.A. elites: Vellum (D-HFA:11) **25 %**, and Grimsby 38 %.
   - **First zone, own zone first.** Without the ease, the early study's first dungeon clears 17 %.
   - **Where.** `tutorial.md`; Refusemancer column; `early_no_ease.md`.
   - **Severity.** Major.
   - **Fix.** Raise the Refusemancer starter's early defence: replace one 1-drop with R-09 Heap Tortoise-style bodies, or start it with 1 Garbage. Or ease Brick Bronson against non-Beefcake Paths only (he is at 92 % for his own Path).

9. **ZoneOrderEase helps, but it acts through foe HP alone, and the HP factors are too blunt.**
   - **What.** The early study clears its first dungeon at these rates (12 runs each; the Necrocrat is not in the study at all):

     | Path (zone) | Places early | HP factor | Attack ease | Cleared with ease | Cleared without |
     |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
     | Beefcake (Gainlands) | 0 | 0.85 | 0 | 75 % | 58 % |
     | Gourmand (Buffet) | 2 | 0.72 | -1 | 92 % | 33 % |
     | Refusemancer (Dump) | 3 | 0.36 | -1 | 67 % | 17 % |

   - **Why it is blunt.** The Gourmand is overshot: 92 % is above the 80 % a first dungeon wants. The Refusemancer gets a near-trivial-HP Rotheart and still only 67 %. That means its deaths come from foe damage, not from fights running long. An HP factor of 0.36 also makes bosses evaporate in a way players will notice.
   - **Where.** `C8_sims/early.md` vs `early_no_ease.md`.
   - **Severity.** Major (feel). Minor if the numbers are tuned.
   - **Fix.**
     - Set an HP-factor floor of about 0.65.
     - Make the attack ease grow with places early: -1 at 2 places, -2 at 3 places.
     - Bring the Gourmand's factor up to about 0.8.
     - Add the Necrocrat (D.N.A., 1 place early) to the study.
     - Aim for 75-85 % first-dungeon clears for every Path.

10. **Long troughs of 100 %, "fast" filler.**
    - **What.** Every freed-zone roamer (8 rows), every zone-final normal, and the D-PC, S-CAP, S-PASTE and S-BEEF normals are 100 % for all four Paths, mostly under 6 turns. From L13 on, normal fights stop being decisions.
    - **Where.** `freed:*`, `zone:final:*`, `D-PC:2/4/6/13/15` rows.
    - **Severity.** Minor. Normals are meant to be easy, but not 100 % with 20+ HP left.
    - **Fix.** Give freed-zone normals +10 % HP per zone freed after them, or a +1/+0 "late" rule from L16. Aim for about 85-90 % wins with a real HP cost, so the HP pool in sequences matters.

11. **Siphon is binary: Elowen is the whole fight.**
    - **What.** Without Elowen, 17 / 50 / 63 / 50 %. With Elowen, 83 / 100 / 100 / 100 %. One run-state flag swings the fight by 40-65 points.
    - **Where.** D-LAB:16 vs D-LAB:16:elowen; `modifiers.json` → "Dr. Ambrose Siphon" (five Protocols: +2/+1, a 3/3 every 3 turns, drain 1, heal 1, first card +1 cost).
    - **Severity.** Minor (postgame).
    - **Fix.** Have Elowen jam one Protocol per turn and also take the stat rule down to +1/+1 only while she is there. Without her, drop one Protocol (the first-card tax). Target about 45 % without her and 70 % with her.

12. **Vanilla filler in reference decks.**
    - **What.** C-02 Village Militia (2 mana, 2/2, no text) appears in drag lists 14 times at -4 on average. C-15 Desperate Measures and G-20 Plate It drag -58 and -42 in the spells-only arena.
    - **Where.** `data/cards/C-02.tres`; the arena row.
    - **Severity.** Minor.
    - **Fix.** Give C-02 a small keyword (Hustle or +0/+1), or make it 1 cost.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The middle of the curve, L10-21, for three Paths.** Main-dungeon bosses sit at 50-75 % for the Path that fits, elites at 63-90 %, and normals at 90-100 %. The Doppelganger (D-TTK:13) and His Majesty the Raccoon (S-REF:3) are well judged for most Paths.
- **Home-zone identity.** Each Path does best in its own zone: Necrocrat D.N.A. boss 90 %, Gourmand Buffet boss 100 %, Refusemancer Dump 100 % across the board. Keep that flavour when retuning; just don't let it hit 100 %.
- **The tutorial for three Paths.** The cave clears 92-96 % with real HP pressure (13-15 HP left). Only the Refusemancer needs a nudge.
- **The Capital lieutenants.** Mirror Knight (D-PC:5, 63-90 %) and Royal Staircase Guard (D-PC:9, 75-100 %) are mostly in or near band.
- **Swolympus Ancient Spotter (S-BEEF:3).** 42-67 % at L23. It is a good postgame boss; it gets its difficulty from stats (+5/+4) without feeling unfair in the sims.
- **Cards that pull their weight.** B-06 Portal Ripper (+17 vs Clench), B-36 Desk Flip (+8), N-01 Skeleton Clerk and N-15 (+13), and R-08 Possum Prowler (positive in 7 of 7 appearances) are all doing their job.
- **The tools.** The per-encounter sim and card-impact lists made this review possible in two hours. One request for the tools: let `--filter` take several keys, and drop the 10-minute ceiling by allowing `--resume`.
