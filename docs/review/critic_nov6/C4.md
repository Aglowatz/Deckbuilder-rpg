# Critic C4 - The endgame (Nov 6)

## 1. Summary

I did not get to Primm. In about 90 minutes and 31 scripted runs I got from the `n6_lategame` save in Crosspath to the Capital, past Captain Spotless's gate exam, up the Palace Approach and into Primm's Castle. The castle's arrival cutscene (the Rescuer arrested at the gate by a peacock courtier) is the best moment I saw. Then I got stuck in the castle's first fight, Hedge Trimmer Guard, and the time ran out. So **the Crease, the Model Room, Primm's real fight, the ending and the credits are unreviewed.** That gap is itself the main finding: the late game has a lot of friction. It is hard to find the way out of town. The quest log, the map goal and the brief all point different ways. A roaming hazard drains HP on every walk to the gate. A "hard" gate exam beat my level-20 deck 2 times out of 4 with the built-in pilot. And the first castle fight stalled the pilot at round 2 in both tries. The art, the big set-piece stills and the Capital's look are excellent. Getting through it is the problem.

How I played:
- **Runs:** 31 runs (`c4r1` to `c4r31`), each started from the title screen. Path: Beefcake, level 20, 1200 gold at the start, 1330 after the exam win, minus two 20-gold "paperwork fees" for the losses.
- **Shops:** I visited none. The quest log never sent me to one, and I didn't have the time. Gold was never the limit.
- **Fights:** 4 tries at the Spotless exam with `pilot`: 2 won, 2 lost. 2 tries at the first castle node: turn 1 played by hand, then `pilot`, which stalled both times. 1 extra attempt to play a turn by hand failed: a drag made during the "Start / opponent is thinking" window did nothing.
- **Dev commands:** `dev save` / `dev load` to chain runs. `dev travel dungeon D-PC` three times, to re-enter the castle after loads. I had already walked in legitimately in `c4r27`. Side effect worth knowing: `dev travel` refills zone HP to 23/23. Snapshots always restore you to the zone's entrance, not to where you saved. In the Capital that meant running the Tidy-Bot gauntlet again on every run.
- **Hand play:** I played battle input by hand where the blind-script format allowed (mulligan, clicking or dragging cards, Space through phases). I never got a hand-played Primm fight.

## 3. Findings

1. **The first castle fight stalls: two tries, pilot "stuck" at round 2, no progress.**
   - **What:** Hedge Trimmer Guard, the first node of Primm's Castle. Rules: "Perfect Order: Units can't have more than 2 keywords" and "Mirrors: The first card each player plays each game is copied for the opponent."
   - In both runs (`c4r29`, `c4r31`) the pilot reported `stuck on turn 4 ... battle still running` after 120+ decisions. The board still read **Round 2**, both players at full HP, and my side showed no units or infrastructure at all, with a hand of 11+ cards and "Needs 4 energy; you have 1".
   - In `c4r30`, clicking a Powerhouse by hand did play it. So the input works, but something in this fight leaves the player energy-starved, or keeps the pilot from completing actions. It could be the Mirrors copy taking the first infrastructure, or played infrastructure not being shown on my side.
   - **Where:** `c4r31_05_stall.png`, `c4r29_08_end.png`, `c4r30_02_click.png`.
   - **Severity:** blocker for me (I couldn't get past it in budget); probably major for a human.
   - **Fix:** Reproduce this exact fight with the Mirrors rule. Check what happens when the first card played is an Infrastructure (copying a basic land is a degenerate case; exclude Infrastructure from Mirrors). Show the player's infrastructure row clearly on the board, and log "Mirrors copied X" in big text.

2. **Getting from Crosspath to the Capital is unsignposted, and the way in is invisible.**
   - **What:** The main quest wasn't tracked on load; the HUD showed a side quest. After I tracked "Free the Four Zones", the step read "Go to the Capital" with no direction. The map's goal marker sat on **Gatekeeper Brannoch**, not on a road or exit. The "Where to?" list has no Capital entry. Fast travel excludes it.
   - I entered the Capital only by stepping into an unmarked trigger (`portal_final`) behind Brannoch while holding S. There was no prompt, no door and no confirmation.
   - The brief said "the road leads under the town first". The game never showed me such a road.
   - **Where:** `c4r1_03_map.png`, `c4r3_02_whereto.png`, `c4r11_02_maren.png` (a big "The Capital" title shows over the town's north road, which visibly ends in rocks and trees), `c4r14_01_bran.png`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Fix:** Auto-track the main quest when its step changes. Put the goal marker on the actual exit. Give the exit a visible arch or signpost and an "[E] Travel to the Capital" prompt. Add the Capital to "Where to?". If the route is meant to go under the town, mark that entrance.

3. **The Tidy-Bot gauntlet costs 4-6 HP on every walk up the Gate Road, before a "hard" fight where HP carries in.**
   - **What:** Zone HP is 23 max. Every approach (runs 15, 16, 18, 21, 22, 25) got "A Tidy-Bot knocks you flat. Please remain orderly. (-2 HP)" two or three times. The bot is hard to see until it hits (a red full-screen flash).
   - Snapshot loads restore you to the entrance, and so does a defeat ("Off you pop"), so a player retrying the exam pays this tax every time. They arrive at Spotless with 17/23 against his 46 HP.
   - **Where:** `c4r15_02.png`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Fix:** Make the bots visible patrols with a telegraph and a gap you can time. Or make the hit a stun or knockback with no HP loss. Or heal to full at the exam ("the Standard requires visitors in pristine condition" would be on-theme).

4. **Captain Spotless's exam is a coin flip at level 20.**
   - **What:** With the built-in pilot I won 2 of 4. One loss was a blowout: "Resistance Scout dealt all 20 damage you took (4 hits)", Spotless untouched at 46 HP, "You ended with 7 cards still in hand."
   - The intro text calls it "a hard card battle, his deck fair to nobody". As the gate to the finale that's fine in spirit, but 46 HP vs 17-23 HP is a big asymmetry.
   - Losing costs 20 gold and the walk back. It doesn't cost progress, which is good.
   - **Where:** `c4r22_01_end.png` (defeat recap), `c4r20_04_after.png`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Fix:** Cut his HP to about 30, or let the player enter at full HP (see 3). Keep the excellent defeat recap.

5. **The Crease is never pointed to.**
   - **What:** From the gate to the castle door the Capital is one straight corridor: Gate Plaza, then Palace Approach, about 25 seconds of walking. The map showed only two "Anonymous Complaint Box" interactables and some dark side openings. Neither the quest tracker nor any dialogue I saw mentioned the Crease, so I walked straight to the castle.
   - **Where:** `c4r26_10_map.png`.
   - **Severity:** major (if it's story-critical, players will miss it the same way).
   - **Fix:** Add a tracker step ("Find a way into the Crease") with a map marker, or have an NPC on the Approach pull the player aside.

6. **The Capital is beautiful but thin to explore.**
   - **What:** I found no shops, no named NPCs between the gate and the castle except "A Perfectly Happy Citizen" (four of them, same name), and no side doors I could enter.
   - **Where:** `c4r24_01_in.png`, `c4r26_07.png`.
   - **Severity:** major for "is the Capital worth exploring": right now it isn't.
   - **Fix:** Give each citizen a distinct line and name ("A Perfectly Happy Baker"), open 2-3 side doors with micro-stories about Primm's regime, and put a last shop or healer here.

7. **Roaming townsfolk body-block the hero in Crosspath.**
   - **What:** Holding W for 3-9 seconds near the square repeatedly moved the hero nothing. A "Townsfolk" barking NPC stood right in the path (the hero was stuck at the same spot for 15+ s in `c4r7` and `c4r12`).
   - The fountain also walls off the obvious north route. The real road north is a gap between the card and pack shops that you can't see.
   - **Where:** `c4r7_02.png`, `c4r4_03_walk.png`.
   - **Severity:** major (it reads as "controls are broken").
   - **Fix:** Make roamers non-blocking for the player (or have them step aside), and widen and mark the north street.

8. **Toasts stick for the whole scene.**
   - **What:** "A Tidy-Bot knocks you flat..." stayed on screen across the whole Spotless conversation. "Welcome to Primm's Perfection. Everything is perfectly fine." stayed for the entire walk from the plaza to the castle door, about 30 s. "Correction fee: -20 gold (logged)." also lingered.
   - **Where:** `c4r24_01_in.png`, `c4r26_07.png` (bottom text).
   - **Severity:** minor.
   - **Fix:** Time-limit toasts (3-4 s) and clear them on dialogue open and zone-label change.

9. **Bark text and name labels overlap.**
   - **What:** "Four plinths round the fountain..." is drawn over two "Townsfolk" labels.
   - **Where:** `c4r4_03_walk.png`.
   - **Severity:** minor.
   - **Fix:** Stack barks above the name plate, and hide the name while a bark shows.

10. **After all four zones are freed, the town still talks as if nothing happened.**
    - **What:** "Four plinths round the fountain, and nobody remembers what stood on them." The plinths are still empty, and the HUD says "Services restored: 4 of 4". The map still labels the main goal "Free the Four Zones" while the zones are done.
    - **Where:** `c4r4_03_walk.png`, `c4r15_05_map.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor (a missed payoff).
    - **Fix:** Put a statue or emblem on each plinth as zones are freed. Rename the quest step to "Storm the Castle" once 4/4.

11. **The exam's battle backdrop doesn't match the setting.**
    - **What:** The Spotless fight at the white-and-gold Capital gate uses a dark purple wasteland background. The castle fight, by contrast, has a lovely gilded hall.
    - **Where:** `c4r20_03_battle.png` vs `c4r29_02_played.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Use the Capital gate still or a gate-plaza backdrop for the exam.

12. **Inputs during the opponent's "Start" window are silently dropped.**
    - **What:** I dragged a card while the turn banner read "YOUR TURN" but the phase box still said "The opponent is thinking...". Nothing happened, with no feedback.
    - **Where:** `c4r29_02_played.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Either queue the action, or snap the card back with a "Wait for your main phase" tooltip. Don't show "YOUR TURN" until input is accepted.

13. **Every other E press only finishes the typewriter, and there is no talk prompt at range.**
    - **What:** Each dialogue line needs two presses at normal pace, which is OK. But Elder Maren and Brannoch gave no response from 2.4 m with no "[E] Talk" prompt. Players will think E is broken.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Show the prompt as soon as you are in range, and widen the talk radius slightly for quest NPCs.

No `SCRIPT ERROR` lines appeared in any `stdout.log` (`c4r1` to `c4r31`). There is only the usual engine leak noise at exit.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The castle-gate cutscene.** "Great news, citizens! The traitor has been located! Please hold your applause until she has been removed." The still of the Rescuer between golden guards, with the peacock courtier holding his papers, is a real set piece and a strong twist hook (`c4r27_09.png`).
- **The Capital's art direction.** Pastel checkerboard, gold statues, smiley-sun banners, "A Perfectly Happy Citizen". It's an instantly readable satire of enforced perfection (`c4r24_01_in.png`, `c4r26_07.png`, `c4r26_09.png`).
- **Captain Spotless as a character.** His portrait, his voice ("Do not look nervous. Nervousness is a Violation. (He polishes a button.)", "I have measured twelve thousand visitors and approved four.") and the "retroactively approved" payoff (`c4r16_06_sp.png`).
- **Old Hob the Gravedigger's defeat scene** ("Hm? You? Not on my list. Off you pop. Heh." / "The shovel doesn't polish itself.") with a small gold fee instead of lost progress. Death is funny and cheap.
- **The defeat recap.** "What beat you: Resistance Scout dealt all 20 damage you took (4 hits)", plus a concrete tip. It's exactly the clarity the rest of the endgame lacks.
- **The pre-fight rules card** ("Before the fight: ... Hover an icon for its text; the same icons stay on screen during the fight") and the end-turn safety check ("You can still play 1 card this turn").
- **The zone title banners** ("The Capital - Seat of His Perfection") and the card art and frames in battle.
