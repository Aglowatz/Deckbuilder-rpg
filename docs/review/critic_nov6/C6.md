# C6 - Story and dialogue review (desk review, Nov 6)

## 1. Summary

The Wayward Path has the best line-by-line writing I have seen in an indie RPG of this size, and a main plot that doesn't hold up at the moments that matter most. The four zone stories are self-contained, warm, funny, and each has a real emotional turn: Flex and Clench, the Doppelganger getting a name, Agnes taking the office she never wanted, Compostella letting the tree rot. Those are an 8-9. The Capital's satire is sharp, and the facade citizens' "slip" lines are properly upsetting. The weak part is the spine. The "who am I" mystery is given away in every zone by the same hint template, and the Wanderer never reacts to any of it. The reveal (Memory 4) gets one line of reaction from the hero, and he spends it comforting Maren. The final confrontation with Primm is a collage of other characters' slogans, and Primm never answers for the murder of the king and queen or the ten years of draining. The ending text contradicts itself: the castle falls, then "does not fall", and Maren is the "first" to say the prince's name although she has already said it several times. The emotional climax of the Rescuer thread rests on a line that contradicts the prologue, and the credits roll while she is still in a cell. Every villain, from Quota to Primm, is redeemed in three or four lines. The pieces are excellent, but the finale doesn't land.

**What I read.** All 4,316 rows of `edit_nov5.csv`, in order. I read the main story in full: Prologue, Crosspath first visit, all four zones with their gatekeepers, main dungeons and after-freeing states, Crosspath between zones (memories, cave-mouth scenes, the Old Well), the Capital, Primm's Castle, the Ending and the Postgame. I skimmed the UI, sign and minigame strings for tone and repetition. I checked `data/story/capital_story.tres` for the key order of the ending beats and for `primm_reveal`. Side quests sampled (10): **Something in the Store Room** (town), **Spot Me!** (Gainlands), **Mend the Meatloaf** and **The Golden Reservation / Omakase** (Buffet), **Number 4,000,212 / Waiting Room of Eternity** (D.N.A.), **Who Ate the Compost? / Trash Panda Throne** (Dump), **Form 27-B/6: A Burial Permit** and **The Wheel Never Stops** (Capital), **The Water Under the Well** (secret, town) and **Under the Forest / the Path-ology Lab** (postgame). I also followed the Isla/Brannoch thread and the Old Hob thread end to end.

IDs below are shortened. `intro::X` means `data/story/intro_story.tres::X`, and the same pattern applies to `gainlands::`, `gourmand::`, `dna::`, `refuse::`, `capital::`, `scenes::` (= `data/story/story_scenes.json::scenes/`), `dc::` (= `data/dungeons/dungeon_content.json::`) and `enc::` (= `data/encounters/encounter_scripts.json::`).

**Main cast: could I tell their lines apart without the name?**

- **Elder Maren:** yes. "dearie", pie, tortoise and hare jokes. But "dearie" also belongs to Granny Gristle, and her feeding motif overlaps the Grand Chef's.
- **The Rescuer / Elowen:** yes. Dry, clipped, deflecting ("Four of them. Next question.").
- **Primm:** mostly yes. Courteous, elaborately subordinated sentences. His "I find that..." tic leaks into Mortimer (`dna::cutscene.vacancy.3`) and the Primm guard (`capital::freed_npc.primm_guard::0`).
- **Wren:** barely. Competent but generic resistance-leader lines.
- **Grandmaster Flex:** yes. "son", breathing, short aphorisms.
- **The Grand Chef:** partly. "Have you eaten? Sit." is hers, but "my dear" and the feeding overlap with Maren.
- **Agnes Overdue:** yes, by content (forms, appeals). By texture no: almost every line ends in "dear", which Auntie Alembic, Clipwell and the Grand Chef also use.
- **Archdruid Compostella:** yes. "child", soil, grief.
- **Old Hob:** yes. "Heh." Unmistakable.
- **The Wanderer:** no. He mostly asks questions ("What are you writing?" six times, "Who are you?"), makes neutral quips, or recites other people's lines.

## 3. Findings (worst first)

1. **Weak moment 1: the Primm finale is the hero reciting other people's morals, and Primm never answers for the crime at the centre of the story.**
   - **Where:** `capital::cutscene.primm_p1.2::1` ("whatever you took, it was not the part that matters", Flex's line), `capital::cutscene.primm_p2.2::0-1` ("A name is a blank page until you sign it", the Grand Chef; "nobody files me under 'subject'... No order outranks treating people with respect", Agnes), `capital::cutscene.primm_end.3::0-1` ("Nothing grows if you refuse to let anything go", Compostella; "You took my mother and father... I'm letting it go"), `capital::cutscene.primm_end.4::0` through `primm_end.8::1`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** The climax plays as a greatest-hits reel, so the hero sounds like a collection of fridge magnets rather than a person. The single personal accusation ("You took my mother and father") gets no reply: Primm moves straight on to smiles and mirrors. The Wanderer forgives the man who killed his parents and drained him for ten years in the same breath that he names the loss. There is no anger, no grief and no cost. Primm's final line, an apology "about the hats", is a joke where an acknowledgement should be.
   - **Fix:** Give the Wanderer one line of his own that comes out of what happened in the white rooms (the word "again", the glass) rather than a mentor quote. Make Primm answer the accusation directly, in his own self-justifying register ("They were sentimental. You were a resource; I looked after you"). Then let the hero not forgive yet: let him choose not to correct Primm, which the scene already gestures at in `primm_end.6`, without "letting go" of his parents. Move the hats line earlier, or make it Primm failing to say the right thing and knowing it.

2. **Weak moment 2: the identity reveal gets no reaction from the person it happens to.**
   - **Where:** `intro::memory.4.fragment::0-3`, `intro::memory.4.after::0-4`, `intro::memory.4.reply::0` ("There's nothing to forgive. You gave me a bed and pie..."), `intro::memory.4.after2::0`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** He learns he is the "dead" prince, that his parents were murdered and that he was the lab's subject. His only line reassures Maren. The scene is about Maren's guilt, not his discovery.
   - **Fix:** Put one or two Wanderer beats before the absolution: a question about his parents, the lullaby, the boy hiding behind his mother's cloak, or plain silence followed by something small and specific. Then let him absolve Maren. The pie callback is good. It just shouldn't be his first and only response.

3. **Weak moment 3: the ending contradicts itself three ways.**
   - **Where:** `capital::ending.beat.1::0` ("the castle walls begin to come down") against `capital::ending.beat.8::0` ("The castle does not fall; it opens"), and the post-freeing castle is still standing and walkable (`capital::fx.main_dungeon` freed text, "main body cleared"). `capital::ending.beat.8::3` ("Everything comes back at once: ... the name") repeats a reveal the main path already gave in Memory 4. `capital::ending.beat.10::2` ("It is the first time anyone has said it aloud and meant it") against Maren saying the name in `intro::memory.4.after::0`, `memory.4.after2::0`, `town.elder.stage.4::0` and `known_early::0`.
   - **Severity:** blocker (the story contradicts itself in its final minutes).
   - **Fix:** Decide what happens to the castle and write only that. "Opens" is better and matches the postgame. Branch beat 8 so the "everything comes back" line plays only on the rushed / `primm_reveal` path. Rewrite Maren's line so it is the first time she says it in public, to the crowd, rather than the first time ever. Also, beat 10 is tagged with the speaker "Agnes Overdue" (`ending.beat.10.title`, `ending.beat.10::0-2`), but the spoken line is Maren's. Fix the tag.

4. **Weak moment 4: the Rescuer's name beat rests on a false premise.**
   - **Where:** `scenes::pc_capture/steps/9::1` ("Her name is {rescuer}, and all that way I never once asked her") against `intro::prologue.wanderer.1::0` ("...Who are you?") and `scenes::cave_mouth_1/steps/2::1` ("Who are you?").
   - **Severity:** blocker (an emotional beat contradicts the player's own memory of two earlier scenes).
   - **Fix:** Rewrite toward "I asked her twice, and both times she said 'a friend'" or "I stopped asking". The guilt can then be about letting her deflect, which fits her character better anyway.

5. **Weak moment 5: the credits roll and the town throws a funeral and a homecoming while the Rescuer sits in a cell.**
   - **Where:** `scenes::pc_capture/*`, `scenes::maren_after_capture/steps/4::1` ("That's a door I can't open for you. Not yet."), `capital::ending.beat.1-7`, `intro::still.reunion::2` ("a hooded traveler smiles, leaves still in their hair"), and the rescue gated behind Rip's postgame tip (`Travel: forest tip` lines, `Quest Log "Under the Forest"`).
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** The game's most personal relationship is left dangling through the finale. Nobody in the ending mentions her. The hooded traveller in the reunion still reads as Elowen, who can't be there. Her rescue, which is excellent, is reached through a Beefcake cousin's day off.
   - **Fix:** Have one ending beat acknowledge her absence: the Wanderer looking for her in the crowd, or Wren writing her name in the true record. Or move the Lab before the credits. Make the "hooded traveler" clearly the Wanderer ("you"), or cut it. Give the forest tip a line that connects it to Plume's memo ("beneath the forest... her father").

6. **Weak moment 6: the D.N.A. is freed by a rule nobody ever set up.**
   - **Where:** `enc::Mortimer Grimsby, CE-No/1/line::0`, `dna::dungeon.ha_boss.after::1-2` ("Tuesday is not a business day in this building"), `dna::npc.matching.intro::2` (Kyle: "i was the one who filed the big transfer form"), `scenes::pc_plume_memo/steps/2::2` (Plume: "I drafted... notarized it, filed it").
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** The Tuesday rule appears for the first time at the boss. Not one of the D.N.A.'s many memos or signs mentions it, so the player wins by coincidence. Kyle's guilt about filing the form without reading it is the obvious engine for this payoff, and it goes unused. It also contradicts Plume's claim that he filed it.
   - **Fix:** Plant the rule early: a lobby memo such as "REMINDER: The Department does not file on Tuesdays (Rule 1)", and Kyle's "Tuesday's my rec-room day". Have Kyle admit he filed it on a Tuesday. Plume drafts it, Kyle files it. Then Kyle's away message and his "i think about that more than i let on" pay off.

7. **Weak moment 7: the prince mystery is solved by the player by the first zone, using one template repeated everywhere, and the hero never notices.**
   - **Where:** `enc::Hollow Warden/0/line::0` ("You cannot be him"), `intro::town.elder.stage.2::0`, `gainlands::npc.brenda.return::2-3`, `gourmand::npc.odalys.return::3-4`, `dna::npc.dolores.return::3-4`, `dna::dungeon.ha_records.before::2-4`, `refuse::npc.marigold.return::1-4`, `capital::prop.lost_poster::1` ("ESCAPED ROYAL ASSET. Age: approx. 21."), `capital::npc.hesper.intro::3`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** Each zone has one NPC saying "the little prince... four colours... you've a look of him". The callbacks are lovely when Elowen gathers them (`scenes::cave_mouth_3/steps/3::1`). But the formula is identical each time, and the Wanderer never asks a follow-up question, so the mystery feels like dramatic irony he isn't in on.
   - **Fix:** Cut two of the hinting NPCs, or swap them for less direct clues. Agnes's unfiled form and Bramble's twelfth tree are the strongest, so keep those. Give the Wanderer a short reaction or question after each surviving hint so he is visibly piecing it together, and let a cave-mouth scene show him asking Elowen directly.

8. **Weak moment 8: Primm's own record contradicts itself at his breaking point.**
   - **Where:** `capital::cutscene.primm_p2.3::1` ("I made a list of things other people had already built, and I put my name on it") against `capital::event.pc_early_journals.result.0::0` ("he built a hundred good things"), `capital::dungeon.pc_blueprints.after::0`, `capital::npc.wren.return::1` and `capital::ending.beat.9::2` ("The wells were good"). Also `capital::cutscene.primm_reveal.5::0` ("Your power has kept this kingdom lit") against the Blackout debuff text and Rollo's wheels, which light the facade.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** The game spends a whole archive persuading you he really did good work, then has him confess it was all stolen, then the ending says it was good again.
   - **Fix:** Make the confession about credit and consent, not authorship: "I built them. Then I stopped asking anyone whether they wanted the next one." For the power line, say "kept my hold" or "kept the Paths in one shape", which matches the ward notes at `capital::dungeon.pc_ward.before::1`.

9. **Weak moment 9: the redemption conveyor belt.**
   - **Where:** `scenes::quota_yards_after/steps/1-3`, `scenes::clipwell_lawn/steps/5-6`, `scenes::crumb_paste_works_after/steps/3-4`, `capital::freed_npc.primm::0-2` ("the workshop has a very nice window"), `scenes::lab_siphon_after/steps/3-4`.
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Why:** Three lieutenants in a row are reformed in three or four lines by the same move: a freed NPC offers them a menial job they will hate. Crumb's "Yes, Chef" is earned. Quota's and Clipwell's turns are not. The architect of a regicide ends up with a nice window and drain projects. Universal, instant forgiveness lowers the stakes of everything before it. Clipwell's "I gave her the seed because she was crying" (`scenes::clipwell_lawn/steps/3::1`) is a great line, and it gets thrown away.
   - **Fix:** Keep Crumb. Let one lieutenant refuse redemption and simply leave, and let one be arrested. Give Primm's captivity one line that names its weight ("He asks for the list of names every morning. There are eleven thousand. He is on the third page").

10. **Weak moment 10: the ending states the theme as a sermon, then the credits repeat it.**
    - **Where:** `capital::ending.beat.6::0-2` ("The point was never to be the same; the point was the argument. Different Paths, working together."), `capital::ending.credits.2::0`, and `capital::ending.postgame.body::0` ("What made the kingdom strong was never sameness").
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Why:** Beat 5 already shows the theme perfectly ("The Beefcakes are late, the Necrocrats have brought a form... Nobody agrees on anything"). Beat 6 explains the joke.
    - **Fix:** Cut beat 6, or reduce it to one concrete image. Keep beat 7's crossed-out decree, which says the same thing better.

11. **Gerald both passed on and is still serving coffee.**
    - **Where:** `scenes::hob_town_freed/steps/0::1` ("Gerald's number finally came up... brought his own mug for the journey") against `dna::npc.barnaby.intro.freed::0` and `dna::npc.barnaby.return.freed::1` ("My number's been called. I'm only here because I like the coffee"). Also `dna::cutscene.vacancy.6::0` puts Mortimer "right behind Gerald".
    - **Severity:** major (a beloved side character's fate is contradictory).
    - **Fix:** Pick one. Either Hob says "Gerald's number came up, and he asked if he could stay for one more pot. I said yes", or Gerald's after-freeing lines become a goodbye.

12. **Maren misremembers the Rescuer's arrival.**
    - **Where:** `scenes::maren_after_capture/steps/1::2` ("the day she brought you to my door") against `intro::prologue.rescuer.4::1-3` and `intro::cave_mouth.maren::0-2` (Elowen sends him alone through the cave, and Maren meets him at its mouth).
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** "the day she sent you through my cave."

13. **Elowen's uncertainty about who he is doesn't square with years of bringing him soup.**
    - **Where:** `scenes::cave_mouth_3/steps/1::1` ("At first I told myself you just reminded me of someone... not until I'm certain") against `scenes::lab_study/steps/2::2` ("He told me you were a patient, so I brought you soup") and `capital::dungeon.pc_ward.before::0` (the records say "SUBJECT 1, the Royal Asset").
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Add one line that explains it: she never saw his face in the lab ("they kept you behind frosted glass; I passed the soup through a hatch"), or he was masked. That also strengthens the cell-4 scene.

14. **Kestrel's instructions loop.**
    - **Where:** `dc::quest.side_s_cap.offer::0-1`, `dc::quest.side_s_cap.active::0`, `dc::quest.side_s_cap.done::0`.
    - **Severity:** major (a confusing instruction at the Capital entrance).
    - **Why:** She offers "an older way into the city", tells you to "get yourself inside" first, and only shows the hatch after you are already in.
    - **Fix:** Either she gives you the older way now (the hatch to the Old Service Tunnels) and Wren is the destination, or she says plainly: "Pass Spotless's exam, then knock three times at the manhole. After that, I'll show you the way nobody watches."

15. **Wren's insight lines don't use what the player brought her.**
    - **Where:** `capital::npc.wren.insight.2::0` ("a man who needs to be right, and a man who can't bear to be disagreed with"), `insight.1`, `insight.3`, `insight.4`; and the four quest-givers' "Remember what I told you, it's in your journal" (`capital::quest.cap_burial.done::0`, `cap_wheels.done::0`, `cap_recipes.done::0`, `cap_untidy.done::0`).
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Why:** The insights are generic because they are order-agnostic. The journal line is a visible template repeated four times.
    - **Fix:** Have Wren name the specific insight ("He schedules grief. Write that down"). Vary or cut the journal reminder.

16. **The "can't taste" motif is spread across three characters, which blunts Primm's.**
    - **Where:** `enc::The Doppelganger/1/line::0`, `scenes::crumb_paste_works_after/steps/2::2`, `capital::quest.cap_recipes.ready::3` (Primm "can't taste"), `scenes::crumb_paste_works/steps/2::1`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Keep it for the Doppelganger and Crumb, and give Primm a different sense he lacks, or cut Marlo's insight and use one about surprise.

17. **The Capital re-runs D.N.A. jokes.**
    - **Where:** `capital::fx.permit_3::0-1` (take a number, NOW SERVING: 3), `capital::sign.grave_permit::1` (27-B needs 27-C) against `dna::dungeon.ha_forms.before::0`, `dc::dungeons/D-HFA/events/8/body::0` and the D.N.A. NOW SERVING signs.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Make Grave Row's bureaucracy visibly Primm's tidy version, with a new joke (a "Proof of Grief" form graded on neatness). Or have a character say outright that Primm copied it from the D.N.A.

18. **Recycled structural gags.**
    - **Where:**
      - Wake-up fees five times: `gainlands::fx.wake::1`, `gourmand::fx.wake::1`, `refuse::fx.wake::1`, `capital::fx.wake::1`, plus the gate guard.
      - Quizmasters share one script: "every answer is on a sign", the scholarly sigh, "perfect would make me suspicious" (`gourmand::quiz.result.3::0`, `dna::quiz.result.3::0`), "the certificate is imaginary" (`gainlands::quiz.result.4::0`, `gourmand::quiz.result.4::1`, `refuse::quiz.result.4::1`).
      - The minigame hosts' "judging you, but kindly/quietly".
      - The Hob exchange repeated verbatim six times (`scenes::hob_town_first/steps/2-3`, `hob_beefcake`, `hob_gourmand`, `hob_necrocrat`, `hob_town_after_funeral/steps/1-2`).
      - "1987" as the punchline for both Maren and Agnes (`intro::town.elder.return::1`, Agnes barks).
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Give each quizmaster one unique result line and drop the shared certificate gag after its first use. Vary Hob's answer once it matters: after the Capital, "you're... hm. Someone rubbed you out and wrote you in again." Keep "1987" for Maren only.

19. **Too many characters share "dear/dearie/darling".**
    - **Where:** Maren and Granny Gristle ("dearie"); Agnes, Alembic, Clipwell and the Grand Chef ("dear"); Sable and Madame Mirepoix ("darling").
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Reserve "dearie" for Maren and "darling" for Sable. Give Mirepoix "chéri(e)" or nothing. Cut Agnes's "dear" by half.

20. **Narration is tagged with character speakers.**
    - **Where:** `capital::ending.beat.10::0-2` (Agnes), `capital::freed_npc.primm_guard::0-1` (tagged Primm, spoken by the guard), the Hollow Warden "light goes out" line, and the Flex and Compostella after-node narration (`gainlands::dungeon.hg_boss.after*`, `refuse` rh_boss after). Also the Agnes "ha number" speech tagged `(narration)` in its before-met variant.

21. **The Old Man Mountain path-direction speech is a mechanics dump.**
    - **Where:** `dc::quest.path_direction::2-4` ("{buff}: {buff_text}... {debuff}: {debuff_text} In {rival_zone}...").
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Keep the rivalry flavour ("Beefcake against Necrocrat, Gourmand against Refusemancer") and move the numbers to a tooltip or the zone arrival card.

22. **Real-world dates and an early-internet parody sit in a fantasy kingdom.**
    - **Where:** Kyle's dial-up and Top 8 (`dna::npc.matching.intro::*`), "since '09", "2090", "since 1994", "1291".

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The royal funeral**, `scenes::royal_funeral/steps/*`: Hob finishing the two graves, Agnes tearing up the third form (`steps/3::1`), the king's "Look at you. You came home." (`steps/6::1`), the queen humming the lullaby (`steps/7-8`), and "Right then, Your Majesties. Mind the step." followed by Agnes's "One more week, Hob" (`steps/9-12`). This is the emotional peak of the game, and it pays off Hob's secret, Agnes's form and the Memory 1 lullaby all at once.
- **Isla's homecoming**, `scenes::isla_homecoming/steps/*` and `scenes::brannoch_isla/steps/0::2`, paying off `intro::town.guard::0-1` and `town.guard.capital_offer::4`. "Wrote you in the book. Every day. 'Isla. Not yet.' / Today it says 'home'." Don't change a word.
- **The Doppelganger's arc**: `gourmand::cutscene.reveal.5::0`, `cutscene.wonder.2::0-1`, `cutscene.wonder.6::0-2` (the Grand Chef giving away her name) and `freed_npc.escoffina::2-3` ("I did not know people could be so kind about salt").
- **The Agnes vacancy scene**: `dna::cutscene.vacancy.2-6`, especially "I only ever wanted to leave." (`vacancy.2::1`) and Mortimer's "I would only have to deny it, and I find I do not want to." Also Betty approving Number 4,000,212 and the ghost cheering quietly (`dc::dungeon.dg_s_necro_boss.after::0-1`).
- **Compostella's release**: `refuse::cutscene.sever.3-4`, plus the Wanderer's best line in the game, `cutscene.sever.2::0` ("Something that can never die isn't a gift. It's a grief that never gets to end. Let it rot."). This is his only moment with a real voice; build the finale on lines like it.
- **Flex and Clench**: `gainlands::cutscene.flex.3::0-1`, `enc::Chancellor Clench, Iron Regent/2/line::0` ("Look how far I went. ...Master?"), and Flex kneeling to offer a hand that is "not taken yet" (`gainlands::dungeon.hg_boss.after::1-3`).
- **The Rescuer's voice**: `scenes::cave_mouth_2/steps/*` ("Favourite colour?" / "Four of them. Next question."), `scenes::cave_mouth_3/steps/3::1-2`, `scenes::lab_cell4/steps/*` ("Took you long enough, Tess."), `scenes::lab_study/steps/4::1` ("If you're kind to me now, I won't be able to walk in there").
- **Dr. Siphon**: `scenes::lab_siphon/steps/3-5` ("I held a door, sweetheart. Doors are held every day." / "And it was very good soup."). Polite villainy done perfectly.
