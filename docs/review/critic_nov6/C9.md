# Critic C9 - tech and feel (Nov 6-7, 2026)

## 1. Summary

On this 7 GB laptop with an integrated GPU (AMD Radeon, D3D12 Forward+) the game is **stable and holds 60 FPS almost everywhere**. In eight runs I saw no crash, no `SCRIPT ERROR` and no audio warning. Memory comes back down after travel: the town settles at the same 480 MB static every time across three full travel loops, so I found no leak during play. The weak spots are **loading and hitches**. A zone takes 4-6 s to load and the Capital 7-10 s. The Verdant Dump took **15.5 s** on its first visit in a session. Most first visits to a zone have a **~0.5 s frozen frame about 3 s after the fade-in**. Coming back to Crosspath has a reproducible **0.6 s hitch at ~4.9 s**. The Capital and crowded battles show regular 35-45 ms frames, which you feel as small hitches. **Low-end mode buys almost nothing**: memory and load times match default (Medium) within noise. It also brings a visible artifact: black diamond holes in the Gainlands cliff faces. And the Settings page still shows Depth of field, Bloom and Fog "Full" as on, even though Low-end clearly turns depth of field off. Every run ends with a pile of Godot leak messages at exit (RIDs, ObjectDB instances, "Leaked instance dependency"). The count grows with the number of zone changes.

**How I played.** Eight runs (c9_a to c9_h), about 50 minutes of wall clock, all from `C:\Dev\wp_critic`, one process at a time.
- Default = **Medium** (the preset this install starts on, render scale 58 %): runs a, b, c, h.
- **Low-end (old laptops)**: runs f and g. I set it back to Medium at the end of run g; g_10_restored and its dump confirm "Graphics quality: Medium".
- Runs d and e only found the settings controls.
- Snapshots: `n6_lategame` (most runs), `n6_midgame` and `n6_postgame` (run h).
- `dev` commands: `dev travel town|capital|zone <x>|dungeon D-PC` to reach places for load timing, and `dev encounter lieutenant|Primm|zone:gourmand` for battle starts. The `pilot` played the fights; I did not judge combat balance.
- Gold was a flat 600 / 1200 from the snapshots. I visited no shops (not part of this brief).

## 3. Findings

1. **First visit to the Verdant Dump takes 15.5-17 s with no feedback.** The `dev travel zone refusemancer` jump blocked for 17.2 s (log 116.4 to 133.6). The game's own line says: `load_timing: heap_zone 15524 ms (slowest step: painted: swaps 7360 ms)`. Later visits in other runs took 4.2-6.5 s, so this is a cold-cache cost the first time a player enters the zone. **Where:** run c9_a `stdout.log`; c9_a_a_17_ref_1s.png is the first frame after the wait. **Severity:** major. On a slow laptop 17 s of a stalled screen reads as a hang. **Fix:** do the "painted swaps" work ahead of time (bake the swapped textures at export, or stream them in after the fade-in). At minimum, show a loading card with a progress bar or spinner for anything over 2 s.

2. **A ~0.5 s freeze about 3 s after arriving in each zone (first visit).** Every first zone arrival in run c9_a logs it: Gainlands 538 ms at 3.1 s, D.N.A. 423 ms at 3.1 s, Buffet 512 ms at 3.2 s, Dump 499 ms at 3.1 s, Capital 475 ms at 3.1 s (run b). The first town load has 499-522 ms at 0.6-2.0 s. Repeat visits drop to 28-79 ms, so this looks like shader or pipeline compilation on first draw. **Where:** `load_timing` lines in c9_a and c9_b `stdout.log`. **Severity:** major. The player has just got control and the game freezes mid-step. **Fix:** prewarm the zone's materials behind the fade (draw each one off-screen for a frame before fading in), or use Godot's shader cache / ubershader warm-up in the shipped build. Then re-measure.

3. **A reproducible 0.6 s hitch about 4.9 s after returning to Crosspath.** `load_timing: town ... worst frame ... 662 ms (at 4.9 s)` (run c), 597 ms at 4.9 s (run f, Low-end). Both times it was the town load after a zone tour. **Where:** c9_c and c9_f `stdout.log`. **Severity:** major. It is the most-visited scene and the hitch lands just as the player starts walking. **Fix:** find what is deferred to ~5 s after the town fade-in (the timing suggests a staged build step or a delayed garbage-collection/cache flush) and spread it over several frames or move it behind the fade.

4. **Low-end preset gives almost no performance gain.** Crosspath: static 327 vs 326 MB, video 963 vs 1000 MB. Capital: 483 vs 445-484 MB. Load times are within noise (Gainlands 5.5 vs 5.3 s, Capital 7.7 vs 7.5-9 s). It still runs D3D12 Forward+ (`stdout.log`: "D3D12 12_0 - Forward+"). Right after switching to it the title logged `fps 24 frame_ms 661.6`. **Where:** c9_f and c9_g `play.log`; c9_f_f_01_lowend_set.png. **Severity:** major for its audience: a player on an "old laptop" picks it and gets the same 1.1-1.5 GB process. **Fix:** make Low-end actually cut memory, for example lower texture mip limit / half-resolution painted textures, fewer foliage cards (Crosspath has 651, the Dump 1312), a shorter draw distance and no particles. Consider offering the Compatibility (OpenGL) renderer with a "restart required" note.

5. **Settings rows contradict the Low-end preset.** After choosing "Low-end (old laptops)", the same page still shows Depth of field checked, Bloom checked, Fog quality "Full" and Render scale "Quality preset (58%)". In game, depth of field is clearly off (f_05 vs a_10). **Where:** c9_f_f_01_lowend_set.png vs c9_f_f_05_beef.png / c9_a_a_10_beef_8s.png. **Severity:** minor (confusing). **Fix:** when a preset is chosen, refresh the dependent rows to show what the preset really uses, or grey them out with "(set by Low-end)".

6. **Low-end shows black triangle holes in the Gainlands cliff faces.** The orange cliffs under the plateau show dark diamond and triangle patches at Low-end that are not there at Medium. Several props near the entrance also vanish (gym equipment on the right, banner on the left). **Where:** c9_f_f_05_beef.png vs c9_a_a_10_beef_8s.png. **Severity:** minor. It looks broken, not "simplified". **Fix:** check the cliff-skirt material or shadow settings in the Low-end path (probably missing back faces, or shadow acne once shadow quality drops). Keep the key landmark props in the Low-end cull list.

7. **The Capital stutters while walking: frame time spikes of 36-44 ms in 4 of 10 samples.** FPS reads 60 but the per-frame samples during `hold` walks show repeated spikes (36.6, 36.8, 37.4, 44.2 ms). Crosspath had a 62.5 ms spike. Low-end improves the Capital to one spike in nine samples. **Where:** c9_b `play.log` 45.0-71.9 s; c9_a 52.0 s (62.5 ms). **Severity:** major on a 60 Hz panel; you feel it as judder when the camera pans. **Fix:** profile streaming and culling in the Capital (2298 nodes, the most of any scene). Look for per-frame allocations from the camera band / city camera, and throttle range culling to a fixed time slice.

8. **The Capital "inside the gate" arrival shows untextured grey blocks and a huge dark slab blocking the view.** Walking from the spawn, the camera shows plain grey boxes (stand-in buildings?) on the left, a flat empty floor, and a big dark rectangle filling half the screen. The hero seems to walk under or behind it. **Where:** c9_b_b_05_cap_walk2.png, c9_b_b_04_cap_walk1.png. **Severity:** major (looks unfinished). **Fix:** fade or cut away the occluding wall (as the zones do with fade meshes) and replace or hide the grey stand-ins in this view.

9. **Crowded battles slow down.** By round 10 against Meatloaf Golem, with about 10 infrastructure tokens per side, frame time sat at 30-42 ms and the object count rose from 8.3 k to 12.0 k (nodes 700 to 1887). The fight ran over 120 s at 1x speed without ending. **Where:** c9_h_h_07_end.png; c9_h `play.log` 212-217 s. **Severity:** minor-to-major (late-game boards are where players spend most time). **Fix:** stack identical tokens into one card with a "x10" counter, and pool card nodes rather than creating them.

10. **Exit-time leak spew grows with travel.** Every run ends with `ERROR: N RID allocations of type ...Texture... leaked at exit` (22-32), `WARNING: N ObjectDB instances were leaked` (80-193), `ERROR: Parameter "RenderingServer::get_singleton()" is null` (in ~ImageTexture), `ERROR: 2-7 resources still in use at exit`, and up to 24 × `Leaked instance dependency: Bug - did not call instance_notify_deleted when freeing` plus `PagedAllocator ... GeometryInstanceForwardClustered`. The scene-instance and storage-buffer counts scale with the number of zone loads: run c (12 travels) had 24 leaked scene instances and 48 storage buffers, against 4 and 8 in run a. **Where:** all eight `stdout.log` files (counts in the table below). **Severity:** minor today (memory plateaus during play), but the scaling means something is freed improperly on each zone unload. **Fix:** run once with `--verbose` to name the leaked objects. Free static ImageTextures / singletons in `_exit_tree` before the RenderingServer shuts down. Look for MultiMesh or particle instances created on zone build and never freed with the zone.

11. **Arrival dialogue blocks movement in every zone.** On each zone jump a narration or NPC box opens ("On the arm of the Throne of Gains someone has left a schedule…", Brother Bramble in the Dump). Walking keys do nothing until it is closed. In run a the hero did not move during `hold W 3` in the Gainlands. **Where:** c9_a_a_10_beef_8s.png, c9_a_a_17_ref_1s.png. **Severity:** minor (it may be intended on a first visit; I arrived by jump). **Fix:** let one-line ambient narration auto-dismiss or allow walking under it, and keep the blocking box for real conversations.

12. **The graphics quality dropdown is an unstyled default Godot grey list.** It is the only unthemed widget I saw. It sits on top of the parchment and wood Settings panel. **Where:** c9_e_e_01_quality_open.png. **Severity:** minor polish. **Fix:** give OptionButton popups the UI skin (wood panel, parchment font colour, gold radio marks).

13. **The victory and defeat Continue button shows up late.** `button Continue` was NOT FOUND 0.2 s after the pilot reported "battle over", in three battles. The scene stayed `Battle` for 5+ s until Continue was pressed (c9_b_b_12_back.png shows the Victory card). That is fine for a player, but nothing tells them the button is coming. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** show the result card and its button together, or accept E/Space on the card the moment it appears.

14. **Primm's nameplate text overflows.** "PRIMM STANDARDIZATION" is squeezed into tiny two-line text in the battle portrait plate. **Where:** c9_g_g_06_boss_board.png (top left). **Severity:** minor (this name came from my debug jump, but long titles like "Vivienne Quota, Minister of Efficiency" may do the same). **Fix:** shrink-to-fit with a minimum size, or show only the short name on the plate.

15. **Audio: nothing is wrong in the logs, and nothing more can be judged here.** Music changes town to zone (5 transitions), town to battle (4) and battle to victory/defeat. None of them logged an audio warning, error or stream message (0 audio lines across 8 `stdout.log` files). All six volume sliders sit at 100 % (c9_g `play.log` dump). I **cannot** judge loudness jumps between cues without hearing them. **Severity:** - . **Fix:** add a loudness report (integrated LUFS per cue, delta at each transition) to the game's audio tooling and gate cues more than 3 LU apart.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **No crashes and no script errors** across ~30 scene changes, 5 battles and two graphics presets on a 7 GB iGPU laptop.
- **Memory returns to a stable baseline**: Crosspath sits at exactly 480 MB static after each of three full Dump/Capital/D.N.A. loops. Keep whatever unloads zones.
- **60 FPS average almost everywhere**, including the busy Crosspath square and the painted zones.
- **Battle starts are quick** (0.6-1.1 s by the game's own timing). The boss intro card (Vivienne Quota, "You arrived four seconds late. I have already deducted it from you.") covers the load nicely: c9_b_b_07_battle_1s.png.
- **The built-in `load_timing` log line**, with its slowest step and worst post-fade frame, made this review possible. Keep it in builds.
- **Defeat recap** ("What beat you - Militia dealt 24 of the 33 damage you took (8 hits)" plus a tip) is excellent feedback.
- **Arrival banners and zone HP panels** (c9_a_a_02_load_1s.png, c9_a_a_10_beef_8s.png) make it clear where you are the moment a load ends.
- **Dungeon node map loads fast** (1.8 s) and looks great: c9_h_h_04_dpc.png.
