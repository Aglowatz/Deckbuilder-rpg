# Critic C7 - UI, UX and visual consistency (Nov 6)

## 1. Summary

The Wayward Path has a strong, mostly unified look. The painted title screen, the Deck Station, the shop portraits, the dungeon map and the battle tables are all lovely, and the gold-on-wood frames carry across most screens. What drags it down is a layer of **unfinished polish that a real player hits in the first hour**. A "Quest updated" toast covers the first line of the very first portrait conversation. The Accessibility tab clips its own label. Dropdowns fall back to the grey stock Godot theme. Card rules text cannot be read at 1280x720. The map is still almost all black even after the story is finished. Keyboard support is uneven: some screens show a focus ring and others show nothing, and some popups ignore Esc. Three screens (Wardrobe, Card Packs, the pause backdrop) use a different, plainer style from the rest. The game is pretty and readable at 1080p. At 720p it gets cramped, and the small lilac help text and most card text drop below a comfortable size.

**How I played:** 13 scripted runs (c7_a to c7_m), about 85 minutes of wall clock. Seven runs were at 1280x720, three at 1920x1080 and three at the default 1600x900, used for walking probes. Snapshots: `n6_ref_postcave` (L3, 520 gold), `n6_midgame` (L13, 600 gold) and `n6_postgame` (L23, 1200 gold). This was a screenshot tour, so I bought nothing. Tilly's shelf runs 50-100 gold per item, so all eight items were affordable at every snapshot. `dev` commands I used:
- `dev packs`, to have packs to open.
- `dev travel zone necrocrat/gourmand` and `dev travel dungeon D-HFA`, to reach a zone and a fogged dungeon map.
- `dev encounter` (Unpaid Intern, Shambling Middle Manager, Primm), to open battles.
- `dev win`, for the victory screen, and `pilot`, for the Primm fight, which ended in a defeat screen.

I played one battle hand myself (hover, drag, log, Esc, Tab). I switched Low-end mode on in Settings, screenshotted the town, D.N.A., the Buffet and a battle, then set it back to Medium. A later run confirmed it reads "Medium". No `SCRIPT ERROR` appeared in any `stdout.log`.

**Not reached:** a "Collection Book". There is none in the town Menu (Character, Deck, Quests, Packs, Wardrobe, Glossary, Feats, Settings), so I treated the Deck Station grid as the collection and the Glossary as the bestiary. Also not reached: the pack vendor's shop, and Tilly's shop and pack opening at 1920x1080. My second E after Tilly's first talk did not open the shop at 1080, and an Esc then landed in the pause menu. The dungeon map was only checked at 1920x1080. The postgame Menu also has no bestiary or enemy list.

## 3. Findings

1. **The "Quest updated" toast covers the first portrait conversation.**
   - **What:** When you first talk to Tilly Tonic, a tall, mostly empty wooden panel ("QUEST UPDATED / Meet the Merchants") appears over the left third of the screen. It hides most of the portrait and cuts off the start of every line in the dialogue box ("es! Tilly Tonic! I'm teal today, which means the n"). It stays up for at least two lines.
   - **Where:** `c7_h_h_tilly_01.png`, `c7_h_h_tilly_02.png` (1280x720). Compare the clean `c7_h_h_tilly_04.png` once the toast has gone.
   - **Severity:** major. It is the player's first shop conversation and the first impression of the portrait system.
   - **Fix:** Hold quest toasts until no dialogue is open, or show them as a small banner at the top right. Size the panel to its text, not to the full screen height.

2. **Card rules text is unreadable at 1280x720, in hand and in the Deck Station.**
   - **What:** At 720p, the rules text on hand cards and deck-grid cards is about 6-7 px tall and partly hidden by the fan overlap. You can only read a card by hovering it. The type line is also clipped by the rarity gem on every infrastructure card: "Beefcake - Basic Infrastruct◯e". This happens at 1080p too.
   - **Where:** `c7_j_j_bat_02.png`, `c7_a_deck_01.png`, `c7_l_l_bat_03_hover.png` (the large preview at the left shows "Basic Infrastruct◯e").
   - **Severity:** major.
   - **Fix:** Use a minimum card text size at 720p, or show only cost, name and stats on fanned hand cards and the full text on hover. Shorten the type line to "Basic Infra." or wrap it, and move the gem out of the text box.

3. **Hovering a hand card shows the same card three times.**
   - **What:** Hovering a hand card shows a big preview at the left, a raised copy of the card, and a tooltip box beside it. At 720p the left preview covers the "Battle log" button and the item slots.
   - **Where:** `c7_j_j_bat_03_hover.png`, `c7_l_l_bat_03_hover.png`.
   - **Severity:** major (clutter on the core screen).
   - **Fix:** Keep the raised card plus the keyword tooltip, and drop the left preview for hand cards. Or keep the left preview and only show keyword chips beside it.

4. **Keyboard focus is invisible on several screens, and Esc handling is inconsistent.**
   - **Focus:** Tab and arrow keys show a clear outline ring in the Deck Station (`c7_a_deck_02_kbd.png`) and on the battle item slots. They show nothing in:
     - the pause menu (`c7_a_pause_02_kbd.png`);
     - Settings (`c7_b_settings_02_kbd.png`);
     - the dungeon map (`c7_l_l_dun_05_kbd.png`).
   - **Esc:**
     - Esc did not close the dungeon Rules popup (opened with R; `c7_l_l_dun_05_kbd.png` still shows it after two Esc presses).
     - In Tilly's shop, Esc gave no visible response for 1.5 s and the hero could not walk for the next ~5 s (`c7_i_i_shop_03_esc.png`).
     - In battle with the log open, Esc opened the pause menu instead of closing the log.
     - Settings opened from the HUD Menu returns to the pause menu on Back, but to the town on Esc.
   - **Severity:** major for keyboard and controller players.
   - **Fix:** Use one shared focus style (the Deck Station's gold ring) everywhere. Esc should always close the topmost popup first (rules, log, tip, shop), and Back should return to wherever Settings was opened from.

5. **The map is mostly black, even after the story is finished.**
   - **What:** At L13 and at postgame, Crosspath's map shows five small revealed blobs on a black square. The rest is "unexplored", and roads between known places are not drawn. Labels collide: "The Department of Necrotic Affairs" overlaps "The Restless Cairn" and runs outside the map frame on the left. "Gatekeeper Brannoch" sits on its own icon.
   - **Where:** `c7_a_map_01.png`, `c7_e_e_map_where.png`, `c7_k_k_map.png`.
   - **Severity:** major. The map is supposed to answer "where do I go".
   - **Fix:** Reveal the whole town once it is first entered, or at least streets the player has walked plus a radius. Draw the roads. Add a collision and offset pass for labels, and clamp them inside the frame.

6. **Settings: clipped tab label and unstyled dropdowns.**
   - **Tab:** The selected Accessibility tab reads "Accessibil" at both sizes (`c7_c_set_access_01.png`, `c7_k_k_set_access.png`).
   - **Dropdowns:** The option lists use the stock grey Godot popup with a radio dot, on a page that is otherwise gold and wood (`c7_c_set_quality_popup.png`).
   - **Scroll:** Opening the Text tab after scrolling Display keeps the scroll offset, so "Card text size" is cut off at the top (`c7_c_set_text_01.png`).
   - **Help text:** Small lilac on brown, hard to read at 720p.
   - **Severity:** major for the tab and popups (they look broken); minor for the rest.
   - **Fix:** Rename the tab "Access" or widen the selected-tab ornament padding. Theme the PopupMenu (wood background, gold hover). Reset the scroll on tab change. Raise the help text to ≥14 px with a lighter colour.

7. **Three screens use a different, plainer style.**
   - **What:** Wardrobe, Card Packs and the pack-opening result sit on a flat purple-to-orange gradient with no frame. The Character, Quest Log, Map and Deck Station all have painted, framed backgrounds. The Card Packs list squeezes into the left 40%, with the "Open" buttons floating mid-screen and nothing on the right. At 720p the last row (Prismatic Pack) is cut off with no visible scrollbar.
   - **Button styles:** Card packs on the Character page use dark rectangular "Open" buttons, while the Packs screen uses gold scroll buttons.
   - **Where:** `c7_a_wardrobe_01.png`, `c7_k_k_wardrobe.png`, `c7_i_i_packs_01.png`, `c7_k_k_char.png`.
   - **Severity:** minor/major (cohesion).
   - **Fix:** Give these screens the shared painted frame. Lay the packs out as a grid of pack art with the button under each. Use one Open button style.

8. **Pack opening has no reveal and leaves a stale list.**
   - **What:** "Open all (19 packs)" goes straight to a wall of text listing about 40 card names. There are no cards to look at. Feat toasts stack over the top of the popup. Afterwards the list still shows six Gilded packs although the popup said all 19 were opened. Faded toasts overlap the remaining "Open" buttons.
   - **Where:** `c7_i_i_packs_02_open.png`, `c7_i_i_packs_03.png`.
   - **Severity:** major (the payoff moment, plus a possibly wrong count).
   - **Fix:** Show the new cards as a fan or grid you can flip, with duplicates collapsed. Refresh the list after closing, or explain what Open all skipped. Queue the toasts after the popup closes.

9. **Labels contradict what is shown.**
   - **Item slots:** The Character page says "0 / 4 unlocked" above four open "+" slots. It means 0 equipped. At L3 it says "0 / 2 unlocked" with 2 open slots (`c7_k_k_char.png`, `c7_a_char_01.png`).
   - **Equipment:** "Equipment unlocks after Some Assembly Required" doesn't tell the player that this is a quest name.
   - **Severity:** major (confusing).
   - **Fix:** "0 of 4 equipped". "Equipment unlocks after the quest *Some Assembly Required*".

10. **Grammar and internal names leak into the UI.**
    - "You defeated Shambling Middle Manager in 1 rounds." (`c7_d_lowend_06_end.png`).
    - Deck tip: "A legal deck has 45+ cards, at most **1 colors**" (`c7_a_deck_01.png`).
    - The boss plate and result read "Primm (standardization)", and the plate shows "PRIMM STANDARDIZATION" squeezed onto two tiny lines (`c7_l_l_bat_01.png`, `c7_l_l_bat_05.png`).
    - Enemy name plates in general are two-line and about 8 px at 720p ("UNPAID INTERN", `c7_j_j_bat_02.png`).
    - "Hide log" renders as "Hidelog" (`c7_j_j_bat_10_log.png`).
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Pluralise properly. Show the boss's display name with the phase as a subtitle ("Primm, Standardization phase"). Give enemy names one line with auto-shrink.

11. **The quest log's control hint is hidden behind the quill art.**
    - **What:** "Left/Right: tabs · Up/Down: cho…" is cut off by the quill at the top right at both sizes.
    - **Where:** `c7_a_quest_01.png`, `c7_k_k_quest_side.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Move the hint below the tabs or left of the quill.

12. **Tips pile onto every first visit and cover content.**
    - **What:** The Deck Station tip covers the bottom deck racks (`c7_a_deck_01.png`). The shop tip covers the High Shelf, and a clipped hover tooltip ("…t level") peeks out from behind it (`c7_i_i_shop_01.png`). The Packs tip covers the list.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Anchor tips to empty space, or make them a dismissable side card. Never let a tip overlap a tooltip.

13. **Small, low-contrast HUD and world text.**
    - **What:** At 720p the quest tracker's done lines (lilac on brown) and its "◂ [ 4 of 5 ] ▸" pager are about 8 px. NPC world labels ("BRINDLE THE BAKER", "TOWNSFOLK") are tiny serif caps without a backing plate and get lost on the cobbles. The title screen's footer "v0.5.4 Press Esc in game for the pause menu. F8 to report a problem" wraps into two cramped lines over the art.
    - **Where:** `c7_b_menu_01.png`, `c7_a_town_01.png`, `c7_a_title_01.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Use a minimum 14 px HUD text at 720p. Give world labels a dark pill. Put the version in its own corner and the hint in a single line.

14. **Low-end mode works but looks flat in places.**
    - **What:** The town is nearly identical to Medium (good). D.N.A. becomes flat dark-green slabs, with a cluster of floating black desk shapes on the right (`c7_d_lowend_03_dna.png`). The Buffet's sauce sea is a flat orange plane (`c7_d_lowend_04_buffet.png`). Battle is unchanged (`c7_d_lowend_05_battle.png`).
    - **Settings:** The preset applied and restored cleanly.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Keep a cheap texture on large wall and water planes in Low-end.

15. **Wardrobe preview artefact and icon sizes.**
    - **What:** The hero preview has a rainbow smear at the right hand, at both sizes. Cloak button icons have different scales (Patchwork Cloak's icon is tiny, Royal Mantle's large).
    - **Where:** `c7_k_k_wardrobe.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Check the hand or cloak mask on the preview sprite, and normalise the icon boxes.

16. **Deck Station: an empty count pill on infrastructure cards.**
    - **What:** Infrastructure cards show a blank pill where other cards show "Deck x/3 Own y".
    - **Where:** `c7_k_k_deck.png` (Powerhouse).
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Show "Deck 19 · unlimited" or hide the pill.

17. **The drag picked a different card from the one hovered.**
    - **What:** I hovered Yeet (2 energy), then dragged from the same spot. The game answered "Needs 4 energy; you have 0", so the drag grabbed the neighbouring card after the fan re-flowed on hover.
    - **Where:** `c7_j_j_bat_03_hover.png`, `c7_j_j_bat_05_played.png`.
    - **Severity:** minor.
    - **Fix:** Start the drag on the card that is currently raised under the cursor.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **Title screen**: the painted vista, the logo with the four Path medallions and the wood-and-gold buttons are gorgeous and set the tone (`c7_a_title_01.png`).
- **Portrait dialogue**: big expressive portraits, a clear name tab, a readable 2-line box and the "E / Click to continue" hint. Tilly's frog alchemist matches her shop sign and her personality (`c7_h_h_tilly_04.png`, `c7_m_m_tilly_02.png`).
- **Tilly's shop layout**: the portrait at the left with a speech bubble, a clear item grid with prices and "Owned / uses left", and a teased High Shelf. Keep it, just enlarge the descriptions (`c7_i_i_shop_01.png`).
- **The dungeon map art**: an isometric painted Hall of Final Approvals with ? nodes as fog. The Rules panel is colour-coded ([+] green, [-] red, [*] neutral) (`c7_l_l_dun_01.png`).
- **The battle phase panel** (Start/Main/Combat/Main 2/End with a plain-English hint), the "To Combat [Space]" button and the **Defeat recap** ("What beat you: Militia dealt all 36 damage… Tip: block it…") make losing understandable (`c7_l_l_bat_05.png`).
- **The Deck Station**: filter chips, the cost curve histogram, legality checks with green dots, and deck slots on the shelves. It is the best screen in the game, and its keyboard focus ring is the model the other screens should copy.
- **The Glossary**: tabbed, iconed, short definitions (`c7_e_e_brindle_01.png`, `c7_k_k_glossary_rules.png`).
- **The Settings breadth**: per-text-size options, battle speed, warn-before-end-turn, colour-blind Path symbols, reduce flashing, full rebinding with a controller column. All the right options are there.
- **Low-end mode** applies and reverts cleanly, and battles look the same in it.
