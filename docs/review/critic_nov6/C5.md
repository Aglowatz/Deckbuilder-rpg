# Critic C5: the postgame (Nov 6, v0.5.4)

## Summary

The postgame has good bones and I mostly couldn't get at them. After the credits the game says clearly what's left. The HUD tracker and the quest log name two main quests, "Under the Forest" and "Loose Ends". Both say where to go, and Rip's lines are charming and short. Then the main thread breaks in the first minute. In the forest the quest tracker and goal marker disappear, J does nothing, and the hero would not walk south past the camp where the hatch should be. I tried seven runs and gave up. After a `dev travel` jump, the Path-ology Lab itself was the best thing I saw: a gorgeous painted node map, events with real choices and some quietly sad writing ("The newest notes are only a count of days"). Its boss is a wall: Dr. Ambrose Siphon has 42 life against your 14, with four Protocols, +1/+1 on all his units and a drain. The auto-player lost to him in 4 rounds. I never found the Shattered Gallery in the Capital. It is not in the quest log or the top of the map's "Where to?" list, and the Crease hideout has no map at all. I only sampled one Gallery fight from the debug catalog. It played on a pitch-black board and was lost after the enemy's life went from 3 back up to 9. The rewards I saw don't pull you on: +20 gold and +30 XP for a Lab fight at level 23 with 1,200 gold in hand. Would I keep playing? For the Lab's map and writing, yes. As shipped, a real player is stuck in the forest and never sees it.

**How I played:** 25 runs, about 95 minutes of wall clock, as a Beefcake at level 23 from `n6_postgame`. Gold was 1,200 the whole time (1,220 after one Lab win). I visited no shop, so I can't judge prices. Each run reloaded a snapshot (`n6_postgame`, plus my own `c5_forest`, `c5_gain` and `c5_cap`).

**Dev commands used:**
- `dev travel dungeon D-LAB`, used about 6 times, because the forest hatch could not be reached on foot (finding 1).
- `dev travel dungeon D-LAB -2`, used twice to reach the Lab boss. My time had run out on the stuck forest, so I did not clear the middle of the Lab, including any Elowen rescue.
- `dev travel capital`, used 3 times.
- `dev encounters gallery` / `dev encounter gallery`, used once, because I could not find the Gallery entrance.
- No `dev win`, `autowin`, `level` or `gold`.

**Hand play:** I played parts of three fights by hand: Lab fight 1 (turns 1-3 in one attempt and turns 1-2 in another), and the first two turns of the Gallery sample. The script driver can't adapt to a random hand mid-run, so that play was crude. The pilot played the rest.

**Fights:**
- Lab fight 1: lost once (my hand turns, then the pilot) and won once (pilot only).
- Lab boss: lost (pilot).
- Gallery sample: lost.

**Skipped:**
- Mount Swolympus. I reached Swole Station but ran out of time before finding Old Man Mountain.
- The Elowen story beats.
- The Lab's middle nodes.
- Any real Gallery run.

None of my runs' `stdout.log` files contains a `SCRIPT ERROR`. My last run was stopped by the host for low system memory just as its fight ended. It had finished the fight and saved its screenshots.

## Findings

1. **The forest hatch can't be reached on foot.** Rip says the hatch is "south of the clearing". In 7 runs, holding S, S+A, S+D and A then S for up to 16 s, the hero never got more than a few metres south of the camp. Often he didn't move at all. The minimap reveals a strip going south, but he stops at the same spot every time. **Where:** `c5r9_18_map2.png` (two maps of the same stop after 10 more seconds of S), `c5r10_grid.png`, `c5r5_14_s1.png`. **Severity:** blocker. The postgame's main story starts here. **Fix:** check the collision south of the camp (the bramble wall that "let go" probably still has a collider), and add a walk smoke test from the forest rift to the hatch.

2. **The forest drops all quest guidance.** In the forest:
   - The HUD quest tracker is gone.
   - The map shows no goal marker and has no "Fast travel".
   - J does nothing.
   - There is no visible rift station back.
   - The objective "Take the Rift Express to the forest where you woke" never completes, even after I arrived there that way.

   **Where:** `c5r4_11_forest_arrive.png`, `c5r4_12_forest_map.png`. **Severity:** major. **Fix:** treat the forest as a normal zone in the postgame: tracker, goal pin on the hatch, quest log, and a visible return rift. Complete the objective on arrival.

3. **The Lab boss is a wall.** Dr. Ambrose Siphon has 42 life against your 14 (max life −5 in the Lab). Eight rules are in play:
   - his units get +2/+1, on top of the Lab's +1/+1 on every enemy unit;
   - every third turn a free 3/3 joins him;
   - each of his turns drains 1 life from you to him;
   - your first card each turn costs 1 more;
   - plus a hidden trait.

   The pilot lost in 4 rounds, even though the rules promise his units don't attack for his first 2 turns. "What beat you" blamed a "Line Cook", a strange unit for a lab boss. **Where:** `c5r19_28_fight_start.png`, `c5r19_28_result.png`. **Severity:** major. Losing here feels cheap, not fair. **Fix:** cut it to two Protocols plus one that Elowen jams, or drop the −5 max life for the boss fight. Show his life against yours in the intro, so the player knows what to expect.

4. **The Shattered Gallery can't be found from the Capital.**
   - It is not in the quest log (Main has only Under the Forest and Loose Ends).
   - It is not in the top 17 entries of the Capital map's "Where to?" list.
   - `dev travel capital` lands you in the Crease, where M does not open a map. You have to walk to a row of six unlabelled doors and read their prompts (grave, hungry, reek, plaza, transit, approach); none says "Gallery".

   **Where:** `c5r21_30_capmap.png`, `c5r24_32_where.png`. **Severity:** major. A postgame dungeon nobody finds is content that doesn't exist. **Fix:** give the Gallery a quest entry and a "Where to?" line. Label the Crease doors with their destinations, and let M work in the Crease.

5. **The one Gallery fight I saw felt cheap.** The board was pure black, with no backdrop at all. The enemy, Hollow Stalker, began at 3 life (a quarter bar) and ended at 9. Nothing on screen said why its life went up. Mid-fight the pilot logged "the turn is not moving; passing the rest of it". I lost 0 to 9 in 6 rounds. The defeat tip blamed infrastructure ("You played no Infrastructure on 4 of your 6 turns"); part of that was my scripted clicking. **Where:** `c5r25_33_gal_t2_played.png`, `c5r25_33_gal_result.png`. **Severity:** major. Losing to an enemy at 3 life with no explanation is the definition of cheap. **Fix:**
   - Give the Gallery a battle backdrop.
   - Show heals in the log and as a floating number.
   - Find why the turn stalled (an action the player can't see or take).

6. **Rewards don't match the postgame.** A Lab fight paid +20 gold and +30 XP at level 23 with 1,200 gold in hand. The Lab event's "gain 80 gold" option is worth more than a fight. **Where:** `c5r16_25_result.png`, `c5r17_26_node1_a.png`. **Severity:** major for motivation. **Fix:** postgame fights should pay in things you can't buy: a unique card or cosmetic per boss, a Lab-only card pick. Show the boss's reward before you commit.

7. **Your life total doesn't grow with level.** Run HP is 19/19 at level 23, so it's 14 in the Lab, while normal Lab enemies have 30 life. **Where:** `c5r12_21_rules.png`, `scene` output "run hp 19/19". **Severity:** major. Twenty-three levels feel like they bought nothing. **Fix:** let max life scale gently with level, or say clearly that power comes from the deck.

8. **The hand balloons to 11-14 cards.** By round 3 the hand overlaps so much that card names are unreadable, and "What beat you" twice blamed "9 cards still in hand". **Where:** `c5r15_24_t2_end.png`, `c5r14_23_t3_f.png`. **Severity:** minor to major. **Fix:** cap the hand size, or fan the cards in two rows.

9. **Esc doesn't close the dungeon Rules panel.** It opens the pause menu on top, and the rules stay up (R toggles them). The rules text also shows a raw "[*]" before each line. **Where:** `c5r12_21_rules.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** make Esc close the topmost panel first, and replace "[*]" with the rule icon.

10. **Stale or odd postgame text.**
    - The Palace Approach greets you with "Welcome to Primm's Perfection. Everything is perfectly fine." after Primm is gone.
    - Your HUD says "The Gainlands is free" (good), but the rift list still shows a locked "???" station after the story is done.
    - The Forest station appears in the Crosspath list but shows "???" in the Crease's list.

    **Where:** `c5r23_31_after_shaft.png`, `c5r3_10_rip_10.png`, `c5r21_30_capmap.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** change the arrival line once Primm is beaten, and audit which stations are unlocked after the story.

11. **Rifts drop you on top of the station.** On arrival at Swole Station and the Crease, the hero stands 0.8 m from the rift, so the next E (for example to skip a line) reopens the Rift Express menu. **Where:** `c5r20_29_gain_arrive.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** spawn 2-3 m in front of the station.

12. **Crosspath's map is mostly unexplored after the whole story.** Large black areas remain around a town you've crossed dozens of times. This may be how the save was built. **Where:** `c5r1_03_map.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** reveal home-town maps fully once the story is done.

13. **The Crease looks unfinished.** It is a flat purple-black slab with doors standing in a row, floating over bright water in front of the Capital's walls. **Where:** `c5r21_30_capmap.png`. **Severity:** minor (look). **Fix:** close it in with walls and a ceiling (it's underground), and add light.

## What's great, don't touch

- **The quest log.** It names the giver, gives a "Where to go" line, shows rewards, and uses "???" for future steps to keep the mystery (`c5r1_02_quests.png`). Two clear postgame quests on the Main tab answer "what now?" at once.
- **The Rift Express screen.** Every station has a one-line joke ("The Rip on duty is slightly damp"), and the rule is clear: "you arrive at full HP... Rips are free" (`c5r3_10_rip_10.png`).
- **Rip's voice:** "Spot this whole dimension, brother! Lift with your LEGS!"
- **The Lab node map.** It is the best-looking screen I saw: tanks, roots, waterfalls, named rooms revealed as you go (`c5r11_20_lab_08.png`).
- **The Lab events.** Three meaningful options with the payoff in brackets, plus writing with real sadness under the jokes (`c5r17_26_node1_a.png`).
- **Dr. Siphon's intro.** "My daughter is resting in your old room. She is confused, poor thing. She thinks she rescued something." That is a genuinely unsettling villain line.
- **The "Before the fight" rules card, and "What beat you" on defeat.** It names the unit, the damage and a concrete tip (`c5r15_24_result.png`). Keep it, and make the hard fights fair enough that this screen feels like coaching rather than consolation.
- **The "End your turn?" guard** when you still have energy and a playable card.
