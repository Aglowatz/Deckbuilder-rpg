# Critic C2: the first zone and its main dungeon (Refusemancer)

## 1. Summary

I never got into the Verdant Dump, and I never saw the Rotheart. In about 100 minutes of real play time, the whole session went on Crosspath's two opening errand quests ("Meet the Merchants" and "Make It Yours") and the start of the Verdant Dump gatekeeper fight. Some of that was slow tooling on my side. Most of it was the game. The town's collision snags the hero on lamp posts, benches and the fountain. One save mode trapped me in the Deck Station twice. The pack opening takes about 15 seconds. And the zone exit is not where the town map's goal marker points: the map marks the next quest goal, never your Path's zone, unless you dig the zone out of a "Where to?" list. What I did see looks gorgeous and is often funny: Tilly, Sable and Maren are well-written characters, and the battle board reads well. But the first 20 minutes of a Refusemancer's town visit are errands, and none of them build toward the Path the player just picked. The Dump's gatekeeper was the first thing the game put in front of me that felt like my story, and it reads as an afterthought: one line of menace, a "wall of thorns" I couldn't see, and a boss on 4 HP against my 16.

How I played: 45 short scripted runs (most under a minute, because navigating took trial and error), chained with `dev save` / `dev load`, starting from `n6_ref_postcave` (level 3, 520 gold). Path: Refusemancer. Time: about 100 minutes. Shops:
- **Tilly Tonic's Supplies**, 520 gold: bought a Healing Salve (70) and a Firebrand Charm (100), leaving 350. Got Power Wraps free.
- **Sable's Card Stall**, 350 gold in the run that counted: bought 2x Alley Scrap (100 each), leaving 150. The stall offered only **two** Refusemancer singles (Alley Scrap, Rusty Can Opener). General Packs cost 150. I didn't buy one, but Maren gave me one.
- Quest reward: +90 gold, which took me to 240.
- Pip's hats (100-120) are cosmetic. Bertram (gear) is gated behind "Some Assembly Required".

Dev commands: only `dev save` / `dev load`. Note that `dev load` puts the hero back at the town arrival point, not where the snapshot was taken, so every run had to re-walk the town. I did not use `dev travel`. Fights: I reached exactly **one**, the gatekeeper Moss Mulligan. I made one manual card drag, then let `pilot` play; it stalled on round 5 at You 12 - Moss 1, stuck in a Curb Alert target prompt. I did **not** meet the brief's "four fights by hand", and I did not reach the zone, its quests, its roamers or the Rotheart. So my difficulty, dungeon and story scores rest on very little evidence, and I say so in each one.

## 3. Findings

1. **I spent 100 minutes and never got into the Path zone.** Two errand quests (5 + 2 steps) sit in front of the zone. Neither points the player at it, and town movement is slow and snaggy (see 2 and 4). A player who just chose Refusemancer never hears "your Path's zone is west, past the forest edge". I found the exit only by picking "The Verdant Dump (Exit / portal)" out of a 17-item "Where to?" dropdown. **Where:** `c2r1_03_map.png` (the goal marker points at Tilly; the Dump is unmarked) and `c2r43_01_dump.png` (the Dump sits far west, off the explored area). **Severity:** major. **Fix:** after the cave, give one short line ("Your Path's home, the Verdant Dump, lies west of the square") and always show your own Path's exit on the map and minimap with a Path-coloured icon. Make "Make It Yours" optional, or fold it into the first Dump visit.

2. **Save Deck enters a slot-pick mode that reads as a soft lock.** After editing a deck, clicking **Save Deck** gives no dialog. The only cue is a line at the bottom, "Save to which slot? Click a slot in the racks above or below (Esc cancels).", drawn *over* the locked-slot icons. In that mode Esc and the X close button both did nothing I could see. I was stuck in the Deck Station twice (once from B, once at the in-world station) until I noticed the line. The quest step "save your deck to a slot" only completes after you click the slot. **Where:** `c2r40_01_saved.png`. **Severity:** major (close to a blocker for a player who misses the line). **Fix:** Save Deck should save the slot that's in use right away, with "Save as new slot" as a separate button. If a slot pick has to stay, use a real modal with Cancel, and make Esc and X cancel it.

3. **Pressing E at the Dump gate picks the sealed portal, not the gatekeeper.** Standing next to Moss Mulligan, the prompt reads "[E] Sealed - the corrupted guardian must fall first", and pressing E just shows that again. I had to shuffle about half a metre to get "Talk to Moss Mulligan". **Where:** `c2r44_02.png`. The scene log said `E -> portal_refusemancer` with Moss 1.6 m away. **Severity:** major. The player is told to beat Moss and can't seem to start the fight. **Fix:** while a portal is sealed, prefer its guardian NPC over the portal, or make the sealed portal's E start the guardian talk.

4. **Town collision snags constantly.** The hero stops dead on lamp posts, benches, signposts and the fountain rim, with no sliding along edges. From one spot west of Tilly's I could move neither south nor west (a lamp and bench pinch). Holding W into the fountain or a signpost just stops you. **Where:** `c2r12_03_sable.png` (hero pinned beside the lamp post between Tilly and Sable). **Severity:** major (every errand turns into pathfinding). **Fix:** use capsule collision with wall sliding, shrink prop colliders to their footprints, and keep the square's main lanes clear of benches and lamps.

5. **General Packs are mostly dead cards for a one-Path deck.** The deck rule is "At most 1 Path". I opened Maren's free General Pack in five separate runs (the result is random each time). Across 15 cards I got about 4 Refusemancer cards and 2-3 usable colourless ones; the rest were Gourmand, Necrocrat or Beefcake cards I can't play. Sable's singles shelf had only 2 Refusemancer cards. Spending is the main reward loop, and at 150 gold a pack you mostly buy nothing. **Where:** `c2r29_01_deck.png` (Necrocrat, Necrocrat, Gourmand for a Refusemancer). **Severity:** major (the loop of gold, then cards, then a better deck feels empty). **Fix:** weight General Packs to the player's Path plus colourless, or sell Path packs from the start, and stock 5-6 Path singles per Path at Sable.

6. **The gatekeeper is underwhelming: 4 HP, one note, no portrait, no visible wall.** Moss starts the fight at **4 HP** against my 16. The world text promises "A wall of thorns has swallowed the forest path. It looks like it is breathing", but the path behind him looks open and green. His three lines are all "...the green is hungry..." ellipses, with no portrait card (every shopkeeper gets one). **Where:** `c2r45_01_moss.png`, `c2r44_02.png`, `c2r45_04_battle.png`. **Severity:** major (this is the first beat of the Path story). **Fix:** build the thorn wall, give Moss a portrait and one line that shows who he was before the corruption, and raise his HP or give him a rule that teaches Refusemancer play.

7. **The Curb Alert target prompt points at a tiny pile.** Casting Curb Alert says "Choose a target / Click a highlighted target", but the target is a card inside the 60-pixel Refuse Pile at bottom right, captioned only "choose a card" in tiny text. The auto-player got stuck there for minutes; a human will hesitate too. **Where:** `c2r45_07_end.png`. **Severity:** minor-major. **Fix:** when a spell targets the Refuse Pile, open the pile as a large picker overlay automatically.

8. **Sable's tutorial line contradicts the player's deck.** "Your deck's all plain neutral cards, darling: honest, dependable and dull. The power is in the Paths." I already had 34 Refusemancer cards. **Where:** quoted line, Sable's first conversation. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** branch on deck contents ("You've got a little Refuse in you already. Let's make it a lot.").

9. **Pack opening is slow and unclear.** You click the pack, the cards fly and flip, and the summary with **Done** appears about 15 seconds later. Clicking the table in between does nothing, and nothing says whether to click the cards. **Where:** pack screens, `c2r29_01_deck.png`. Card titles also hide under the "NEW!" ribbons ("…G APPROVAL", "…NOTICE"). **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** make any click skip to the summary, cut the animation to about 4 seconds, and move the NEW! ribbon off the name bar.

10. **The town map opens almost all dark.** You start knowing only Maren and the Notice Board, while the HUD tells you to "Say hello to Tilly Tonic… on the north side". Fog of war in a safe hub town only hides the shops. **Where:** `c2r1_03_map.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** reveal the hub town fully on arrival, and keep fog for zones.

11. **Stale toast and an odd counter.** "Received: Power Wraps (1 charge). Press C to equip it." stays on screen after you've equipped it. The Items panel says "0 / 2 unlocked" for equipped slots, which reads as "no slots unlocked". **Where:** `c2r8_02_char.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** clear the toast when the item is equipped, and change the label to "Equipped 0 of 2".

12. **Every NPC talk costs two presses per line.** The first E finishes the typewriter and the second advances. With Tilly's 6-line intro plus a freebie plus a how-to, that's about 14 presses before her shop opens. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** a faster typewriter, a hold-to-fast-forward, and trimmed intros for shopkeepers.

13. **Tilly's voice is one-note.** Every sentence ends in "!" ("Hi! Hello! You look like you need supplies! Tilly Tonic! I'm teal today…"). It's funny for two lines and grating by the shelf tip. **Where:** `c2r6_06_tilly.png`. **Severity:** minor. **Fix:** give her a few flat, deadpan lines for contrast.

14. **"Rules of this fight" is drawn twice, overlapping, on the battle HUD.** **Where:** `c2r45_07_end.png`, top left. **Severity:** minor (polish). **Fix:** merge the two rule badges under a single header.

Not a game bug: `stdout.log` for the gatekeeper run has 121 `SCRIPT ERROR`s, all from the test harness's `tools/ui_driver.gd` / `battle_pilot.gd`. No `SCRIPT ERROR` came from game code in any of my 45 runs.

## 4. What's great, don't touch

- **The art direction.** Crosspath's square at golden hour (`c2r1_01_start.png`), the painted half-timber houses and the junkyard battle board (`c2r45_07_end.png`) are cohesive and inviting. Tilly's portrait (`c2r6_06_tilly.png`) is a standout character design.
- **Sable and Maren's writing.** Dry, specific, warm: "Every card has a story, darling. Some of them even happened."; "Here, have a trophy. Everybody gets one… the handle comes off if you wave it about."; "a deck you never chose is a deck you'll lose with."
- **The quest text tells you exactly what to press** ("press C, pick them in Items, then an empty slot"), with a "Where to go" line in the log. Keep that precision; just point it at the Path zone sooner.
- **The Deck Station's rules panel**, with live green checks for deck size, copy caps and one Path plus the cost curve chart (`c2r1_04_deck.png`). It teaches deck-building rules at a glance.
- **The shop confirm dialog** ("100 gold for one copy. You own 0 and will have 420 gold left.") and the quest reward card ("240 gold in your purse now - pocket money"). Clear and friendly.
- **The battle layout**: phase ladder on the right, hand at the bottom, Refuse Piles beside each deck, item slots on the left. It reads at a glance, and the free mulligan prompt with its infrastructure/spell breakdown is good onboarding.
