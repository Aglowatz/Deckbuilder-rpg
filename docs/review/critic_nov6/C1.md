# Critic C1 - the first hour (Beefcake)

## Summary

The Wayward Path makes a strong first impression. The title, the painted wake-up still, the cave node map and the battle board are lovely, and the opening dialogue is clean and quietly funny. The first duel teaches well: one tip at a time, each pointing at the thing to click, a phase ladder that always names the current step, and a "What beat you" recap after a loss that tells you exactly what went wrong. Then come the snags. The Path choice selects only from a small band at the top of each column. One tip is hidden behind the dungeon's own title scroll. The reward screen takes a card for you if you are mashing E. The well offers healing when you are already at full HP. The cave forgets every bit of progress if you leave it. In my session the cave boss (Hollow Warden) beat the built-in pilot twice in a row; the duels before it were 3-HP warm-ups. That is a sharp spike for a tutorial. **I did not reach Crosspath with the real story state, so Tilly, Maren, the merchants and the gatekeeper are unreviewed.** Read this report as a review of the opening, the Path choice, the tutorial cave and the Character page only.

How I played:
- 21 runs, Beefcake Path, about 95 minutes of wall clock.
- I played the first cave duel (Cave Scavenger) by hand with clicks for three full rounds: infrastructure, Dropped Weights on a blocker, Iron on an attacker, attack selection. The pilot finished it.
- In the second duel (Hollow Stalker) I played the opening by hand, then handed it to the pilot. The rest of the cave was piloted.
- The pilot lost the Hollow Warden boss: "Hollow Warden wins after 6 rounds", with the Warden on 1 HP.
- Why I didn't finish: a run restarts at the title, and a snapshot taken inside the cave reloads in the forest with the cave reset. Each attempt therefore had to replay the whole cave. Fights are not deterministic (different draws, rewards and well results each run), so my scripted replays drifted ("button Take: NOT FOUND"), and a whole chained run fell apart before the boss.
- **`dev travel town` used once**, about 80 minutes in, to recover from the above and reach Crosspath. That jump skipped the story: the quest log was empty ("Nothing here right now.") and gold was back to 120. A later `dev load` of a snapshot taken in town put me back in the forest ("Map - The Awakening", "Goal: Cave gate"). So I could look at the town and the Character page, but **not Tilly's lesson, Maren's errand or the shops**.
- Gold: 120 at the start. +40 per cave win. 240 before the boss. No shop visit was reached.
- No `SCRIPT ERROR` in any `stdout.log` (19 runs checked).

## Findings

1. **Cave progress is wiped whenever you leave it; there is no mid-cave checkpoint.** A snapshot taken on the cave map reloads in the forest, and entering again shows every node as "?" (`c1_r6_cv_03_map.png` vs a later entry). Gold stayed at 160 but the cleared nodes did not. A real player who quits or crashes mid-cave replays every duel, and it also feeds an exploit (re-farm the 40-gold fights). **Severity: major.** **Fix:** save the dungeon's cleared nodes and current node when you save or quit, or at least once each fight is won, and resume there. If a reset is intended, say so on Retreat ("Leaving resets the cave").

2. **The first boss is a wall after two 3-HP warm-ups.** The Warden adds a Cave Echo mid-fight and swarms with four attackers. The pilot lost twice; the first loss was 6 rounds with the Warden on 1 HP. The recap blamed Village Militia, a card the player owns too, which reads oddly as "the enemy's" threat (`c1_r18_c8_11_post.png`). **Severity: major.** **Fix:** make the step between duel 2 and the boss smaller: give duel 2 6-8 HP and a blocker so the player practises blocking, or trim the Warden's swarm. Keep "you keep cards and gold" on defeat (good). Name the enemy's copy in the recap ("their Village Militia").

3. **Path choice: clicking the cards or the blurb in a column does nothing; only the header area selects.** I clicked a Beefcake card (415,400) and the blurb (415,510): no highlight, and Begin stayed grey with no hint why (`c1_r4_pc_10_click_desc.png`). Clicking the icon (415,300) worked (`c1_r4_pc_11_click_icon.png`). In another run even the icon click did not take on the first try, and Begin was "NOT FOUND" while greyed. Most players click the cards first. **Severity: major** (the first real choice in the game feels broken). **Fix:** make the whole column one click target, with hover feedback on the column. Show a one-line hint next to a disabled Begin ("Pick a Path first").

4. **The "dungeon map" tip is hidden behind the cave's title scroll.** On entry the tip text sits under the banner; only "...Lose a duel and you are carried back to town with your..." peeks out (`c1_r7_cv_10_tip.png`). **Severity: major** (it is the tip that explains the stakes of losing). **Fix:** show the tip after the title animation, or put it below the banner (y > 260).

5. **E auto-takes the highlighted reward card.** After the first win, pressing E (which the game has taught as "continue") picked "Dropped Weights joins your deck." with no look at the three options (`c1_r12_c2_02.png` shows the pre-selected Take button). **Severity: major** for a deckbuilder: deck choices are the core decision. **Fix:** no default focus on Take (or make E skip only the gold/XP part). Require a deliberate click or a second press on a card.

6. **The Hollow Well offers a heal when you are already at full HP.** "Pass: Heal 4 HP. Fail: Lose a card for the dungeon." at 15/15 HP (`c1_r12_c2_04.png`). The risk is all downside, so a new player who takes it is being punished for curiosity. The panel is also mostly empty space. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** grey out Reveal with "You're at full HP" when the reward is useless, or give a reward that is never wasted (gold or a card) at full HP.

7. **Iron-targeting mode swallows Space and E silently.** After clicking the Iron and missing the unit (the units shift sideways when a new one is played), the battle sat in "Choose a target" while Space and E did nothing (`c1_r10_b3_03_end.png`). The panel does say "Right-click to cancel". **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** let Esc or Space cancel targeting as well. Briefly flash the valid targets when the player clicks empty board.

8. **Mid-conversation prompts say "E / Click to close" on lines that are not the last.** Example: "Someone in a hood is kneeling beside you... Their face stays in shadow." shows "close" and is followed by more lines. Also, every line needed two presses in my runs (one to finish typing, one to advance), with the label unchanged. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** show "continue" until the real last line. Change the prompt (or show a typing indicator) while the text is still typing.

9. **The dungeon banner's subtitle names the wrong place.** "The Forgotten Cave" with "The Forest Clearing" under it (`c1_r6_cv_03_map.png`, banner in `c1_r7_cv_10_tip.png`). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** subtitle "Beneath the Forest Clearing", or drop it.

10. **The hero's look changes between the wake still and the dialogue portrait** (teal cloak and wild hair in `c1_r1_02_wake.png` vs a brown cloak in `c1_r1_16.png`). **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** recolour one so the cloak matches.

11. **"More" on the Path choice dumps the full 45-card list as a run-on line**, and the card count reads odd ("19 Beefcake Infrastructure, 17 units, 6 spells, 1 trap, 2 tools") right after the player is told about Iron (`c1_r3_pc_04_more.png`). It tells you nothing about how Beefcake *plays* turn to turn. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** replace the list with three highlighted signature cards and one "a typical turn" sentence. Put the full list behind a hover.

12. **The forest opening is very dark** (`c1_r5b_f_03.png`). The cave marker saves it, but props and paths blend together at 1600x900. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** lift the ambient light about 15 % or add a rim light on the walkable path.

13. **Hand cards' rules text is unreadable at 1600x900** (`c1_r8_b1_09.png`). You must hover every card. The hover panels are good, but scanning a hand is slow. **Severity: minor.** **Fix:** a short keyword line in larger type on each card (e.g. "Enters: +Iron").

14. **(Session finding, not proven to be a game bug.)** Each battle plays out differently even when the same moves are repeated (different draws, rewards and well results). Fine for players, but combined with Finding 1 it means a stuck player can't "just redo the bit before". **Fix:** see Finding 1.

**Not reviewed** (out of time after Findings 1-2 forced full cave replays): Crosspath's real arrival, Tilly's item lesson, Maren's "Meet the Merchants", shop prices and what 240 gold buys, the road out of town and the Beefcake gatekeeper. The Character page (`c1_r20_t_02_character.png`) is clear and well laid out. "Equipment unlocks after Some Assembly Required" and "No items yet - Tilly Tonic's Supplies sells some." point the player forward nicely.

## What's great, don't touch

- **The tip system in duel 1**: one instruction at a time, a bold verb ("Click the glowing Infrastructure."), an arrow to the exact card, and "Do it now; the duel waits for you." (`c1_r7_cv_e3.png`, `c1_r8_b1_03.png`). Best-in-class onboarding.
- **The phase ladder on the right**, with a live "Your main phase / Choose a target" explainer, and the context button that changes "To Combat -> Attack with 1 -> End Turn".
- **Cost tooltip**: "Needs 3 energy; you have 0 (each ready Infrastructure gives 1 a turn)." Exactly the right answer to "why can't I play this?".
- **The "End your turn? You can still play 1 card this turn." guard**, with a "Don't ask again" option.
- **The defeat recap "What beat you"** and "the cards and gold you won are kept". Turns a loss into a lesson.
- **"-2 HP if unblocked"** preview on the enemy portrait while you choose attackers.
- **The wake-up and the Rescuer's lines**: brief, human, a little wry. "No pressure. (Some pressure.)" is the house voice; keep it.
- **The Hollow Warden's intro** ("Only one may pass this door, and he is long dead." / "That light in your hands. I know it... You cannot be him.") is a great story hook.
- **Art**: the title screen, the painted wake still, the cave node map (`c1_r6_cv_03_map.png`) and the golden-hour Crosspath square (`c1_r20_t_00_arrive.png`).
- **The Well's odds line** ("Your deck: about 69 % to pass") is great design. It teaches deck building through a mini-game.
