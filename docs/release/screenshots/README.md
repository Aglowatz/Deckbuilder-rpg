# itch.io screenshots (Fable Nov 21 review, from the build of Nov 21)

Eight pictures of the current game, captured in the real game at **1920 x 1080** (fullscreen on the development PC, nothing
scaled), with no debug text. Play scripts: `docs/review/play_scripts/nov21r/p7_itch_shots.txt`, `p7b_itch_shots.txt`,
`p7c_battle.txt`, run as
`GODOT_ARGS="--fullscreen" SHOT_RES=1920x1080 bash tools/play.sh <script> <run name> 800`.
The state is a late-game bot save (the four zones free), so the world is at its brightest. Upload in this order; the
first one is the picture players see in listings.

Things to know before you choose:

- `04_shop_sable.jpg` and `extra/deck_station.jpg` have a once-ever tip panel on them ("Tip: Buying cards"); it is the game as a
  new player sees it. Say so if you want them shot again without it.
- `02_battle.jpg` is the gatekeeper duel against Chef Fennel Gravois, turn 5 (no boss, no spoiler).
- There is no Capital picture in the eight: the late-game save stands under the city (the Crease), which is dark, and the
  Capital above is a spoiler for the last third of the game in any case.
- Avoid the STORY-* stills other than the wake-up: spoilers.
- The Nov 9 and Nov 16 sets are still in `../itch_page/screenshots/` (the classroom quiz, the Wardrobe, Dex's table, the
  Shattered Gallery map); they show the older menu look.
