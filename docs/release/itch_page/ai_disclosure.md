# AI-generated content disclosure (draft for the itch.io page and the credits)

Short version (for the page, under the description):

> **About the art, music and writing.** The Wayward Path was made by one person with the help of AI tools. The paintings - cards,
> characters, places, story pictures and the interface art - were generated with an image model from my own descriptions, then picked,
> edited and fitted into the game by hand. The music was written as code with AI assistance and rendered with a free soundfont (FluidR3_GM).
> The story and dialogue were drafted with AI help and edited by me. The game design, the rules, the cards and the balance are my own work.
> The sound effects are from free CC0 libraries (Kenney, Freesound) and the 3D models from Kenney and KayKit (CC0).

On itch.io: tick the AI-generated content option in the upload/edit form ("Graphics" and "Sound/Music" and "Text") and add the tag
`ai-generated` if the form asks for it.

The same text is in the game: title menu > Credits ("About the art, music and writing"), file `data/credits/credits.txt`.
