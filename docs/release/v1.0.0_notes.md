# The Wayward Path 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v1.0.0-windows.zip` (see `docs/overnight_report.md` for whether it is built, and the exact
commands). Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or Documents, not Program Files) and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. If
Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway". `README_PLAYTEST.txt` in the folder explains the start
files. **Saves carry over** from every release candidate and earlier build.

1.0.0 is RC6 (the owner's fix list, the new dungeon pop-ups, the Crease as a cavern: `rc6_notes.md`, never built on its own), the Nov 13
dialogue rewrite, the last art batch and a final round of fixes. No difficulty setting was added, the battle board and the menus are
where they were, and Primm's fight is unchanged.

## New since RC6

**The writing**
- **1,245 lines rewritten** (Nov 13): Elder Maren, the Rescuer and the main story, the core cast of every zone, side content and the
  world's passing lines. Every character keeps one voice and one endearment; the deadpan ones (Bertram Beetsworth, Captain Spotless)
  keep short sentences.
- **Maren tells you her name at the cave mouth**, in her welcome.
- 66 loading tips rewritten: hints in plain words, lore in the world's voice.

**New art in the game (Batch 15, 39 pictures)**
- **The Capital:** painted windows on every townhouse (lit in the freed city), official portraits of Primm on the gold easels, a row of
  rubble and dead scrub along the bare edges of the Reek, the Transit Yards and the outskirts.
- **The freed Capital**, after Primm falls: the four lands' banners in a row down the avenue and over his emblem, paper lanterns rising
  in front of the castle and soft, occasional fireworks (still, or off, with Reduce motion and on the Low-end preset).
- **Elowen walks.** At the end of the opening she walks away into the trees instead of sliding, and she walks to the cave after each
  cave-mouth scene.
- **The Endless Buffet:** two painted market stalls; Madame Mirepoix now stands in front of hers.
- **The D.N.A.:** a ghostly now-serving orb at the head of the lobby queue.
- **The Verdant Dump:** the raccoon king's statue on the throne beside the Rotheart's gate, and patched cloth sails turning on the
  three mills.
- **The Gainlands:** worn canvas gym mats.
- **The Shattered Gallery** has a new map; **Dex Shufflewick's** matching game has its own table and card back; the battle item slots,
  the locked card packs at Fenwick's and the pop-up banners are painted; the Hall Pass Slippers and the Blade of Earned Reputation
  have their own icons; Nurse Hesper's emblem is a green leaf; Fig Sly has two arms.
- **The credits** scroll over a panorama of the whole kingdom at dusk.

**Fixes**
- **New Game on a fresh install goes straight to the story.** The three-slot screen appears only once a save exists.
- **Roaming things that hurt give a warning.** A Tidy-Bot, a meatball or any roamer whose touch costs HP stops, flashes and sounds
  an alert for most of a second before it comes. The few safe seconds after arriving somewhere now count only while you can move.
- **The Capital's map** names its districts, shows the Crease as its own small map beside the city, and no longer piles its labels up.
- **The minimap** is smoother (rounded edges, not blocks).
- **The Crease:** the camera no longer fills with rock when you walk along the side walls.
- **The Verdant Dump:** no pale wedge across the view in the south corners.
- **Crosspath's south edge** ends in a hedge and a shadowed ravine, not a flat violet band.
- **The D.N.A.:** filing towers, paper stacks, a roped queue and loose forms on the ground outside the offices.
- **The Wanderer's dialogue portrait** fades out at its right edge (it ended in a hard line).

## Known issues
See `post_1_0_backlog.md`. The ones a player may notice:
- The Rescuer's disguise art has no walk frames, so she walks away in Elowen's own frames (seen from behind).
- The pack is about 864 MB, the zip about 750 MB (music at full quality by choice).
- The world maps are still drawn from the walk grid (painted maps were not in this batch), and the Shattered Gallery's eight foes have
  no bestiary pictures yet.
