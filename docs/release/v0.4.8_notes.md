# The Wayward Path v0.4.8 (2026-10-30) - release candidate, not shared yet

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.8-windows.zip` (762 MB; the pack inside is 852 MB, over the 800 MB budget). Unzip the whole
folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or Documents, not Program Files) and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. If Windows says "Windows
protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway". Start with `README_PLAYTEST.txt` in the folder. Press F8 in game to report a
problem.

These notes cover everything since **v0.4.6** (the build testers have; v0.4.7 was never shared).

**Your saves carry over.** Saves from v0.4.6 and earlier load as before: your decks, cards, gold, gear, slots and progress stay exactly as
they were. Levelling is slower now (below); a hero who is already above the level their XP would give keeps that level, its rewards and
its XP, and simply levels up again a little later.

## A painted world

- **Painted enemies.** Every roaming enemy now walks the zones as a hand-painted character instead of a grey 3D figure, with a little
  pause and a glare when it spots you. Renamed foes keep their old Collection Book entries.
- **Elder Maren is a tortoise**, as she always said: new portraits (happy and worried too) and a new figure in Crosspath and at the cave.
- **Boss entrances**: bosses, lieutenants and gatekeepers slide in as full painted characters when a big fight starts; Primm changes as his
  fight goes on.
- **Story pictures**: fifteen new paintings behind the moments that matter - the four memory fragments, the castle gate, the Chef's dish,
  Agnes's office, the seed, the model room, the kept face, the throne, the facade falling, the funeral, Isla coming home and the old cell.
- **Zone landmarks**: a painted cave mouth and giant trees in the forest; a royal-crest fountain in Crosspath, with a Path banner on the
  square for every zone you free; the Colossal Hamster Wheel, floating islets and regime posters in the Gainlands; filing towers, ghost
  lamps and a queue of waiting ghosts in the D.N.A.; tasting stands and a cake-cathedral skyline in the Endless Buffet; the Rotheart on the
  Dump's horizon, a painted Junk Mountain and the raccoon king's dumpster; the great white wall, the castle on its hill, a gold Primm statue
  (and what happens to it), lost-property notices and painted rifts in the Capital.
- The three Dump farmhands and Primm after the ending are painted characters now.

## Cards, feats and battles

- **New card art** for the 15 cards that had none (the newest cards and the Hazmat Intern) and a new Push Broom of Undeath.
- **Feats**: forty achievements in Menu > Feats, each group with its own icon; a toast with the icon when you earn one. Feats you already
  earned on an old save are counted when it loads.
- **Battle feel**: snappier hits, damage numbers in the attacker's Path colour, resources that fly to their counters, a proper entrance
  for Legendaries, a "Turn N" ribbon, a new 4x speed, and **hold Space** to hurry the enemy's turn.

## Gold, gear and rewards

- **Prices on one curve**: cards cost 30 / 60 / 150 / 300 by rarity, gear by rarity and tier, items 30 / 45 / 60. Roaming enemies pay more
  in later zones, side-dungeon clears pay 100 the first time, and the level track gives more of its gold early, when you need it.
- **Reroll a card pick** once for gold (40 + 10 per level): a button under the three cards after a gatekeeper, elite or arena win, and
  beside Skip on a dungeon's rewards screen. A boss's special card stays in the new set.
- **Shops say more**: every tile shows its rarity colour, a readable effect and whether it is an Upgrade, Sidegrade or Downgrade for what
  you wear; reward boxes tell you what your gold buys.
- **Equipment rebalance**: the Wicked Dagger and the Solid Plate now boost your **Colorless** units; their old any-unit +1 attack is the
  new **Blade of Earned Reputation** (Epic, Bertram's advanced rack). The Spotter's Barbell and the Gainsmith's Lifting Belt work with
  Beefcake units. Twenty pieces in all got new numbers or rarities (the Glass Jaw Gauntlets, Sucker Punch Mitts, Crown of Mild Hubris,
  Four-Seal Signet and others); the Reckless Tonic heals 5. Gear you own takes its new stats.
- **Hats fit**: every hat sits on your head from the side and from behind too, in the world and in the wardrobe preview.

## Levelling and difficulty

- **A slower, steadier climb.** Levels used to arrive far ahead of the story (level 12 at the House of Gains, 25 or more by the Dump).
  Now you reach about level 5 at the Gainlands, 8 in the House of Gains, 14 at the Test Kitchen, 19 when the Dump is freed and 22 at
  Primm; the postgame takes you on toward 30, each level a little dearer than the last. The first few levels still come quickly.
- **Fights re-tuned for that pace**: the Iron Cave and the Forgotten Vault are fairer at the level you meet them; Chancellor Clench and
  his Honor Guard ease off a little; Captain Spotless, Compostella, the castle's elites, the Ancient Spotter and Colonel Crumb hit
  harder; Primm's three phases make a real final fight.

## Fixes

- A memory leak in the D.N.A. (each visit kept ~23 MB of video memory until you quit) is fixed: long sessions stay smooth.
- Old Hob no longer stands in the road to the D.N.A.
- The four lieutenant relics (Quota's Stopwatch, the Colonel's Ladle, Plume's Quill, Clipwell's Shears) have their own icons.
