# The Wayward Path v0.4.6 (2026-10-27)

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.6-windows.zip` (691 MB). Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or
Documents, not Program Files) and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
"Run anyway". Start with `README_PLAYTEST.txt` in the folder. Press F8 in game to report a problem.

**Your saves carry over.** An older save loads into save slot 1. Your decks stay exactly as they were, and you keep every card you own.

## Smarter enemies and bigger fights

- Enemies plan their turns now: they pump before they attack, clear the blocker that matters, hold back when a swing would lose, and
  only throw a unit in front of an attack when they have to. Early foes still make the odd human mistake; bosses do not.
- Bosses make an entrance, and many of them change the fight halfway through with a scripted event.
- Beating an elite, a boss or a gatekeeper lets you choose one of three cards themed to the fight.

## Cards

- **14 new cards**, at least one for every Path: a hammer thrower and a desk flip for Beefcake, a final notice and a service charge for
  Necrocrat, an amuse-bouche and pest control for Gourmand, a curb alert, a sewer choir and a landfill leviathan for Refusemancer, a
  hand-me-down shield for everyone, and more. Each starting deck carries one of its Path's new Commons.
- About thirty cards got clearer text, fairer costs or better flavour. Jello Weights is now **Wobble Weights**.
- **Cards you win now go to your collection, not straight into your deck.** Picks, chests, quest rewards, packs and shop cards all
  wait for you at the **Deck Station**, where new cards are marked **NEW**. Only the Forgotten Cave still adds its picks to your deck.
- Some cards are now earned: each dungeon has signature cards you can only get there at first. The Collection Book shows where to earn
  them. The bosses' own cards appear in a new "Seen in battle" section.
- Sable now sells two Commons of your own Path.

## Saves and controls

- **Three save slots.**
- **Controller support**: every screen, menu and battle works with a gamepad, with on-screen button prompts and rebinding in
  Settings > Controls.

## Performance and graphics

- Faster loading and smoother frame rates in every area, plus a Dynamic resolution option in Settings > Display.
- Height fog in the Verdant Dump, a shared wind for banners and foliage, better water, lantern shadows, and shadows that reach further.

## Fixes

- **D.N.A.**: walls in front of the hero fade cleanly instead of popping, and signs hang on the walls.
- **Verdant Dump**: bridges and trees fixed.
- **Gainlands**: the gym hall and Old Man Mountain fixed, and signs moved off the paths.
- **Endless Buffet**: fixes to its paths and props.
- **Volume**: the sliders now change the sound evenly. Your old settings convert automatically.
- Boons and curses in dungeons say what they do, and two that did nothing now work.
