# The Wayward Path v0.4.9 (2026-10-31) - for testers

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.9-windows.zip` (762 MB). Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or
Documents, not Program Files) and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
"Run anyway". Start with `README_PLAYTEST.txt` in the folder. Press **F8** in game to report a problem.

These notes cover everything since **v0.4.6**, the last build testers had (v0.4.7 and v0.4.8 were never shared).

**Your saves carry over.** Saves from v0.4.6 and earlier load as before: your decks, cards, gold, gear, slots and progress stay as they
were. Levelling is slower now (below); a hero who is already above the level their XP would give keeps that level, its rewards and its
XP, and levels up again a little later. A save that is past the first duels (level 5 or a zone freed) no longer replays the beginner tips.

## New tonight: less walking, clearer goals

- **A goal pointer everywhere.** The step of the quest you track has a golden marker over it, a soft ring on the ground, an arrow at the
  screen edge when it is off-screen and a dot on the minimap and the map - in town, in every zone and in the Capital. A side quest gets
  a second, **blue** marker at the same time. When the goal is somewhere else, the pointer leads to the road there. Entering a zone tracks
  that zone's own story line. Settings > Display > **Goal pointer** turns it off.
- **Sprint**: hold **Shift** (on a controller, click the left stick: you sprint until you stop). Settings > Controls > **Always sprint**
  makes running the default (Shift then walks). On a controller, the camera zoom moved from the left-stick click to the Share/View button.
- **Fast travel from the map**: press **M**, then **Fast travel...**: go straight to any Rift Express station you have reached
  (Crosspath and the zones' towns; never into a dungeon). A zone you have freed always counts as reached.
- **The map names places**: vendors, the Hall of Records, roads and dungeons are written under their icons, and your goals are listed in
  the legend.

## New tonight: battles, dialogue, decks

- **See what your card will do before you commit.** While you aim a damage card, a buff, an ability or a Resource at a target, the
  target shows the result: the damage and the HP it keeps, a skull if it dies, its new stats for a buff ("+2/+1 -> 4/3"), and "-N HP"
  or **LETHAL** on a hero. Only your own action is shown - the game never reveals what the enemy will do. Attack previews also show the
  HP each unit keeps.
- **Dialogue**: any key finishes the line, then moves on (Esc, the F keys and Shift keep their jobs). The little voice ticks with the
  letters as they appear and stops when the line is done. In a back-and-forth, the one who just spoke stays on screen, dimmed, while the
  other answers. A story scene or cutscene you have **seen before can be skipped with Esc** - no more clicking through a boss's whole
  intro after every loss.
- **A smarter Deck Station**: a cost curve beside the deck rules, a line with your Paths and card types, new **rarity** and **New**
  filters, "**Works well with**" in a card's preview (the cards in your collection that make what it uses, or use what it makes), and
  **Suggest a deck**: pick a Path (or two) and get a deck built from what you own - a sensible curve, units first, within the copy
  limits - to review before you save. Reset goes back.
- **Clearer HP**: the zone HP bar says when your max HP is different here ("Max 11 here (-5, hover)" in the Capital) and hovering it
  lists why.
- **Shops answer from where you hear them**: vendors open on E from as far away as their chatter carries.
- Names no longer give away twists before the story does.

## New tonight: sound and speed

- Every dungeon map has a quiet bed of its zone's sound under the music; sprinting, a new goal and map travel have their own sounds; all
  sound effects were measured and the outliers in each family brought into line (Settings sliders unchanged).
- Coming back to Crosspath from a zone loads about a second faster.

## Since v0.4.6 (already in v0.4.7 / v0.4.8, never shared)

- **A painted world**: painted roaming enemies, Elder Maren as a tortoise, boss entrances with painted characters, fifteen story
  paintings, painted zone landmarks (the forest's cave mouth, the Colossal Hamster Wheel, the D.N.A.'s filing towers, the Buffet's
  cake-cathedral skyline, the Rotheart, the Capital's great white wall and castle).
- **Feats**: forty achievements in Menu > Feats, with a toast when you earn one.
- **Battle feel**: snappier hits, Path-coloured damage numbers, resources that fly to their counters, a Legendary entrance, a "Turn N"
  ribbon, a 4x speed and **hold Space** to hurry the enemy's turn.
- **Gold, gear and rewards on one curve**: clear prices by rarity; reroll a card pick once for gold; shops say whether a piece is an
  upgrade; an equipment rebalance (twenty pieces), hats that fit from every side.
- **A slower, steadier climb**: about level 5 at the Gainlands, 8 in the House of Gains, 14 at the Test Kitchen, 22 at Primm; fights
  re-tuned for that pace.
- **First-hour fixes** (two outside reviews): a beacon to the cave, E / Enter / Space / controller A answer every popup and tip, the
  Path screen's "More" shows the whole starting deck, the hand drawn smaller so every card's text is readable, a "What beat you" recap
  after a defeat, Settings > Text > Tutorial tips on/off, clearer reasons when a card cannot be played, no black world after the Deck
  Station, a Menu button with Glossary, Feats and Settings.
- A memory leak in the D.N.A. is fixed (long sessions stay smooth).

## What we'd love feedback on

1. **The goal pointer**: does it help you find the next step without feeling like a leash? Is the blue side-quest marker useful or noise?
2. **Sprint and map travel**: do they make getting around feel right? Do you use Always sprint?
3. **The aim preview**: does seeing the result before you commit make targeting clearer? Did it ever show something that did not happen?
4. **Suggest a deck**: are the suggested decks good starting points? What would you change first?
5. **Dialogue**: is the typing speed right (Settings > Text)? Do the voice ticks get annoying? Is Esc-to-skip on replayed scenes enough?
6. **The mid-game**: the Buffet, the Dump and the Capital - where did you get stuck, and which boss felt unfair?
7. **Loading**: how long do zone loads feel on your PC?

Press **F8** at the moment something feels wrong: it saves a report with a screenshot that we can read.
