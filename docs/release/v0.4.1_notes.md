# The Wayward Path v0.4.1 - playtest build (2026-10-19)

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.1-windows.zip` (594 MB). Unzip anywhere and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. For your own playtest,
not shared yet. Saves from v0.4.0 load (the save format did not change). A save keeps the deck it has: the new starting decks below
apply to new games.

## What is new since v0.4.0

### Fights and balance
- Every fight was measured in the simulator and retuned where it was out of its band (normals, elites and bosses; details in
  `docs/design/balance_log.md`).
- **Each equipped item works once per battle** (its slot greys out after use and is back next fight).
- Equipment and items: Grandma's Rolling Pin is now Epic, the Trash Panda Tiara Uncommon; the Big Brain Beret costs 2 max HP; Cheater's
  Dice no longer cost a card in hand; the Thorned Loincloth costs 2 max HP (was 5); the Scroll of Insight draws 2; the Alchemist needs 6
  essence of two Paths (was 10).
- **New starting decks for the Necrocrat and Gourmand Paths**, and small changes to the Beefcake deck: the Forgotten Cave is now cleared
  about 90% of the time on every Path but the Refusemancer.
- **The Gainlands are kinder to Necrocrats**: Processing Time now makes only the *first* Necrocrat unit each turn enter exhausted
  (was every one). A one-time warning still tells a Necrocrat hero on arrival.
- **Boss rules you can see**: Shiro Swindle's and the arena's special rules now actually apply in your duel (before, they were missing),
  and every gatekeeper's rule shows as an icon. Shiro and the arena show a "Before the fight" card with the rules; the same icons stay in
  the battle HUD (hover for the text). New: Grand Bellows' **Heckling Crowd** (you take 1 damage at the start of each of his turns) and
  Primm's **Royal Guard** in phase 1 (he starts with two Militia on the field).

### Playtest support
- **Report a Problem**: press **F8** anywhere (or Pause > Report a Problem). Type a line and Send: a folder opens with your note, a
  screenshot, the log, your save and settings. Please send that folder (zipped) with your feedback.
- **Crash-safe saves**: every save is written to a temporary file, checked, and the previous save kept as a backup that loads if the main
  one is damaged. The game also saves when you change area, after battles and after quest steps.

### The opening
- **"Make It Yours"** (the first deck edit): the cave's cards are already in your deck, so the quest now says so, and Save Deck works on
  your first visit even if you change nothing (before, it stayed greyed out until you changed a card).

### Settings and screens
- Battle speed **1x / 1.5x / 2x**, an **Auto-end turn** option (off by default), and the end-turn warning names your unused energy.
- **Arrival cards** for every zone, Crosspath, the Capital, the forest and each dungeon: the painted banner with the Path icon and a
  subtitle (full the first time, brief later, click or Enter to skip).
- The quest tracker says "Go to <area>" when the next step is somewhere else.
- The Path choice, the equipment-slot choice and the Collection Book share one tile look (the chosen tile wears a ring).
- Bosses show their portraits in the Collection Book and on the battle plate.

### Looks, sound and speed
- The Endless Buffet's gravy moat and the Verdant Dump's sludge pool move like liquid; the D.N.A. has a tiled office floor instead of a
  grey void.
- New sounds for a sent report, an auto-ended turn and the zone warning.
- Menus open faster: the character screen and the town's card and item shops are prepared while the loading screen is up, so their first
  open is smooth (the slowest first open in a full tour went from 345 ms to 150 ms); the card vendor, Deck Station and Collection Book
  open in lighter frames.

## Known issues
- **Primm's three phases in a row** are probably harder than intended (the simulator cleared them 4-21% of the time before the freed
  leaders' boons were counted; the re-measure with the boons is still to do). Phase 1 on its own is easy.
- The Refusemancer's Forgotten Cave run (82%) is now the lowest of the four Paths.
- A level-5 Necrocrat still finds the Gainlands roamers hard in the simulator (25-42%); replays show the simulated player stalling at
  1 enemy HP, so people should do better - tell us how it feels.
- The Rotheart's normal fights are hard (54-63% in the simulator) under its two rules (kept as they are for now).
- The Deck Station's first open can still take ~150 ms.
- Open design questions are in `docs/overnight_inbox.md` (the Path choice's amount of text, the audio listen pass, the dialogue plan).

## Checks run on this build
- Full GUT: 1906 tests, all passing (walkability tests for Crosspath and the zones included).
- **Release smoke on the exported pack** (`tools/run_release_smoke_exported.sh`: the stock Godot 4.7.2 binary runs the exported
  `TheWaywardPath.pck`, since release templates ignore `--script`): 15 of 15 - title shows v0.4.1, New Game into the forest, F8 writes
  report.json + screenshot + log, the pause menu shows the version, a save loads back.
- `tools/run_release_smoke.sh` in the project: passed. `tools/run_playtest_smoke.sh` (the opening hour): passed.
- The exported `TheWaywardPath.exe` ran 30 s to the title with no script errors.
- Full tour bot: 7 areas, 0 errors, every first menu open at or under 150 ms. Onboarding bot: New Game -> first saved deck edit in
  ~10.6 minutes for a person (97 clicks/keys, 31 dialogue lines; `docs/playtest/onboarding_timing.md`).
