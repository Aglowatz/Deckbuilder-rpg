# The Wayward Path v0.4.2 - hotfix (2026-10-20)

**Fixed: freeze when first entering Crosspath after the cave.**

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.2-windows.zip` (628 MB). Unzip anywhere and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. Saves from v0.4.0 and
v0.4.1 load.

## The fix

Walking out of the Forgotten Cave, the game built all of Crosspath in a single frame with a black screen and no loading screen. That
took 8-25 seconds depending on the PC and whether it was the first launch. The window stopped answering, so Windows marked it
"Not Responding", and it looked frozen.

- **A loading screen again** on the way out of the cave. The town is built a piece at a time behind it, so the picture keeps moving
  and the window keeps responding.
- **No hitches after it fades in.** The loading screen stays up until the town is fully ready: the character sprites, footstep
  and menu sounds, the town's ambience, Maren's portrait and the first shop screens are all loaded first. On the test PC, no frame
  took longer than 80 ms during the arrival, even on a first launch.
- **One thing at a time on arrival.** Anything waiting for you as you arrive (a reward box, level-ups, the "too many Paths" warning) now
  opens one after another, before Elder Maren's welcome, and you can always move once the last one closes. As a safety net, if the
  Wanderer is ever held in place with nothing on screen to close, the game frees them after 4 seconds and notes it in the log.
- **F8 (Report a Problem) and Esc** work while any of these arrival screens is open.
- The game log now records how long each area took to load and the slowest frame afterwards. If something still feels stuck, please
  press F8 and send the folder.

## Also in this build (since v0.4.1)

- **Elder Maren's early errands** now guide the first hour, one at a time: meet the merchants and buy a card, make the deck your own at
  the Deck Station, then deal with the ghosts in Tilly's store room, then visit Bertram. The story's main quests open after these.
  Saves already past the cave keep their current quests.
- Quest steps are revealed one at a time, and the quest text no longer gives away what comes next.
- The Rescuer's opening scene has been rewritten. Every NPC's name now shows within 6 m.
- New art: Gainlands gym cards, trees, banners, roads and cliffs; the Verdant Dump's cliffs, vines, wisteria, water wheels and falls;
  10 more NPC sprite sets, 11 portraits, 61 icons, bestiary pictures and the Iron Cave map.
- Many dialogue and quest-text passes, the Old Well hidden quest, a Gear tab in the Collection Book and smaller fixes from the
  overnight runs of Oct 19-20.
