# The Wayward Path v0.4.0 - playtest build (2026-10-18)

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.0-windows.zip` (size: see the table at the end). Unzip anywhere and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`.
Saves from v0.3.1 load (the new roamer respawn clock lives in the quest counters and simply starts empty).

## What is new since v0.3.1

### The rebalance (Parts A-E)
- Iron, Red Tape and Eat Garbage follow the new rules; lingering stat changes expire on their owner's next turn.
- Every encounter has a proper deck and, where it matters, one-sided rules shown as icons with tooltips (never the enemy's intent).
- Unique 45-card starting decks per Path (`docs/design/starting_decks.md`); the progression model and the balance simulator (`core/sim/`) are in.
- Card numbers and texts changed as listed in `docs/design/sheet_changes.md`; every change has a line in `docs/design/balance_log.md`.
- Equipment/items (Part F) and the last iteration passes (Part G) are queued for a later run; the unmeasured late-game groups are listed in `docs/design/balance_state.md`.

### Names, glows and people
- Vendors, quest givers and quest objectives always show a floating name over their heads ("Sable - Cards", "Bertram Beetsworth - Gear"); everyone else shows their name when you walk up. Vendors glow gold at the feet, quest givers green; quest givers with a quest also keep their marker.
- The white-suit CITIZEN only walks the Capital; Crosspath's strollers are villagers.

### The opening
- The wake-up ends with the Wanderer noticing the hooded rescuer ("...Who are you?"); she asks which Path resonates the strongest within you.
- Out of the Forgotten Cave you stand beside Elder Maren at the north end of Crosspath, the town in view, and her welcome opens at once.

### Crosspath
- Three easy enemies roam the lanes round the edge of town (a Stray Gym Hound, a Lost Junior Clerk, a Bin Raccoon). Small gold and XP, they come back after a few minutes, and three wins after the cave and the two starter quests reach level 5.
- The armory quest asks for one win of any kind: roamers, gatekeepers, dungeon battles and the arena all count.
- The level-5 notice says a new quest is available from Bertram Beetsworth.

### Cards and the board
- Yeet: the thrown unit is destroyed after it lands. The Raccoon: "You may eat garbage for free during your turn."
- Infrastructure cards print their own names (Powerhouse, Ghost Town, Feastforge, Wasteworks and the specials).
- The small boxes beside the board are labelled: the trap slots ("TRAPS - 0 of 3 set") and the token tray ("TOKENS").

### Rules you can read
- Zone effects, dungeon rules, boons, the Capital's broken services and a fight's one-sided rules are one row of icons: on the node map, in a brief when a dungeon starts and before each battle, in the zone HUD and in the battle HUD. Hover an icon for its text, click for the full list.
- Chest sparkles are bigger, brighter and visible from farther, with a soft light pulse.
- The Deck Station's ten deck slots are the racks painted at the top and the bottom of the station: click a slot to open that deck, Save Deck asks which slot by lighting the racks up (overwrite asks first). 4 slots open at the start, two more at levels 7, 14 and 21.

The growth is the four zone kits painted since v0.3.1 (Gainlands, Endless Buffet, Verdant Dump, Capital: about 300 textures, 150 MB packed as BC7 at a 1024 px cap) and the sound pass (500 SFX, +13 MB). Dev-only content is excluded (docs, tools, tests, sim reports, screenshots, paintovers, the tmp tools folder, source PSD/Blender files, unreferenced 3D models: `tools/update_export_excludes.js`). Audio stays at the delivered 101-196 kbps (average 126): re-encoding to 96 kbps would save about 50 MB at an audible cost. Still well under the 1 GB target; the next easy cut is a 512 px cap on zone-kit prop textures (not landmarks or grounds), about 60 MB, left for a visual check.
