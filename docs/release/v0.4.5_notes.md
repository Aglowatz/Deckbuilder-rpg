# The Wayward Path v0.4.5 - release candidate (2026-10-23)

**Not shared yet - for a first look before it goes to testers.** A bigger build than the last few: a graphics pass over every area,
hats and cloaks, the rest of the main story (Primm's lieutenants, Elowen's arc, the royal funeral), new art, a reworked shop economy and
fresh starting decks, a balance pass, and dialogue fixes.

Windows build: `builds/TheWaywardPath-v0.4.5-windows.zip` (689 MB). Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or
Documents, not Program Files) and run `TheWaywardPath.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
"Run anyway". Start with `README_PLAYTEST.txt` in the folder.

**Your saves carry over** from v0.4.0 - v0.4.4. Cards and decks you already own are never taken away; only what the shops sell changed.

## A new look

- Every area has its own light now: a moonlit opening forest with rays through the canopy, a midday Gainlands with crisp shadows, a
  low dinner-time sun over the Endless Buffet, an overcast D.N.A., golden-hour shadows in the Verdant Dump and an ash-hazed Capital.
- Crosspath's ground is shaded under buildings and trees, and the characters catch the light from the side.
- Battles glow: effects light up the board and the cards, each area tints its battleboard and map, chests flash when they open.
- Graphics settings have a new upscaler and sharpness option. Medium runs about 10 frames per second faster than before on laptop
  graphics. Low and Safe Mode are as light as they were.

## Hats and cloaks

- Pip Threadwell's Hats & Hems sells four new cloaks (Red Cape, Patchwork, Tattered, Royal Mantle) and five hats (Wide Brim, Chef,
  Bowler, Tricorn, Wizard). They unlock as the story goes on; some are found in chests instead.
- The wardrobe shows your hat on the preview, and every hat follows your head as you walk. The dye colours the plain hooded cloak.

## The rest of the story

- **Primm's four lieutenants** are now fights and scenes: Quota, Colonel Crumb (the boss of the new **Paste Works** side dungeon in the
  Capital's Hungry Quarter), Percival Plume and Clipwell. Each pays a relic the first time.
- **Elowen's arc**: the stranger who woke you in the forest waits at the cave mouth after each zone you free. At the castle gate she is
  taken - and in the new 16-room Path-ology Lab she fights at your side against Dr. Siphon.
- After Primm falls: a royal funeral in Grave Row, Isla Brannoch comes home, and Old Hob turns up in places you would not expect.
- The Old Well has its own portrait.

## New art

- About seventy new pictures: the five hats, the stranger's portrait and figure, item and gear icons, the last token pictures (every card
  and token has art now), painted maps and battleboards for the Paste Works and the Lab, Bestiary pictures, and new ground, plants, sky
  and walls for the Gainlands and the Verdant Dump.
- Rebuilt Oct 23 with the rest of the batch: thirteen repainted portraits that now match the characters you meet in the world (Elder
  Maren, the Gatekeeper, Rip, Pump, Thunderabs, the Mountain, Ectomund, Pate, Flambe, Basil, Wren, Kestrel and Spotless), and in the
  Verdant Dump glass-and-bottle-cap gravel in the junk yards, bottle thatch on the reed roofs, little patched windmills on the farm
  terraces, and new rust, petal and moss patches on the ground.
- The Crown of Leaves is held back until its picture is redone (it stood on the head like a hoop). If you find it in the Dump chest it
  stays in your wardrobe and appears once the new picture is in.

## Shops, starting decks and the Deck Station

- **Sable** in Crosspath now sells a small sample that changes as you level up: Colorless cards and a taste of each Path.
- **Each Path's zone sells that Path**: Commons and two Uncommons on your first visit, the rest of the Uncommons and the Epics once you
  free the zone, a Legendary after the ending. Path Packs and the Black Market are as before.
- **Starting decks** have about 15 Colorless Commons alongside the Path's own cards, so the first hour plays more smoothly.
- The **Deck Station** fits every screen size and UI scale, each deck rack shows its cover card, and card pictures no longer cut off heads.

## Balance

- The whole game was re-measured with the decks a player can really own under the new shops. Six fights were off and were adjusted
  (the fights, not the cards): Prudence Pallor and Moss Mulligan, the first two gatekeepers in Crosspath, are a little easier (they
  were the hardest early fights for the new starting decks; Prudence no longer starts with a Red Tape); two Iron Cave fighters are
  easier; Baron Quadzilla, the Iron Cave's boss, is harder (his units get +1/+1); the Lab's Drain Warden is easier (+1/+1, was +2/+2).
- The Forgotten Cave is cleared about 9 times in 10 with every Path's starting deck.

## Dialogue

- People with a long introduction now say hello first and ask their favour the next time you talk to them (the ! stays up). Minigame
  hosts explain their game first and open it on your next talk.
- Elder Maren keeps her greetings short once she has said her piece, and they change as you free zones.
- Agnes no longer greets you like a stranger in the Hall once you have met her at the front desk.
- In the final fight, the prince only quotes the leaders whose zones you actually freed.
- The Capital's people say new things once Primm is gone.
- Two characters were renamed so no two share a name: the Verdant Dump's animal handler is **Billie Muckfoot**, and the Gainlands'
  Calf Cove portal ripper is **Shredna**.
- The Path choice at the start is quicker to read: each Path shows a one-line plan; point at it (or press More) for the full description.

## Fixes

- Five props were missing from the Endless Buffet and the Verdant Dump in the exported game (a cup, a pie, an egg, a van and a box).
- A stream of errors in the background after the stranger slips away at the cave mouth (no visible effect, but it filled the log).
- Checked on DirectX 12 and Vulkan, on Low, Medium and Safe Mode: the opening hour and the whole story play through.
