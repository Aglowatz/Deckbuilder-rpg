# The owner's playtest list, Nov 25 (for the next Fable run)

Played on the Nov 24 build. The owner's items, made specific. The art is Batch 21 (sheet tab "Batch 21 Nov 25"; `docs/art/batch21.csv` also lists Fable's own requests from the Nov 24 run).

## Cards
1. **First Pick:** it peeks at 1 card (not more).
2. **Haunted Buffet:** costs 1 less and needs only one ingredient.
3. **Final Meal Request:** costs 1 less.
4. **"Ratatouille, Rodent De Cuisine"** is renamed **"Rodent De Cuisine"** (card, glossary, quests, dialogue, saves).
5. **Vigor Mortis:** costs 1 more.
6. **Circle of Life Insurance:** costs 1 less.
7. **Global Gains Platinum Membership:** the unit it's attached to always has the keywords (Hustle, Overtime, Bulldoze) from the moment it's attached. Only the buff happens at the start of each turn.
8. **Table Captain:** new art with two arms (Batch 21 DR-06).
9. **Iron Filing Clerk:** new art with no filing cabinet; a barbell wrapped in red tape and chains (Batch 21 NB-17).
10. **Card art sits too high:** card names cover the heads. Shift card art down in the card frame (per card where needed, or a general offset), on every screen that shows cards. Check every card for heads under the name.

## Equipment
11. **Hover Boots:** only opponents at the High Rollers table may get them (not zone drafts, Classic, or any other opponent).

## Dungeons
12. **Test Kitchen:** the corrupted pantry effect is -5 HP.

Make it a real castle interior (new art: Batch 21 MAP-PC, portrait, scrolls; 22 rooms, the same links). Update the node logic: node positions, path curves and scrolling to match the new art, and fix the duplicate nodes.

## The Shuffle House

16. **The menu:**

    - Mystery Mini's reward says "Path Packs";
    - every box shows "Entry Fee:" and "Rewards:" on separate lines.
17. **A "Random" option next to the Path toggles.**
