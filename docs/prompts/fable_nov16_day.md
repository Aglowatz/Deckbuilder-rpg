## Part 2 — Four new card minigames, one per Path zone (replace the current non-card minigames)
**First, inventory every minigame in the game.** Keep Dex's card matching and any other card-matching game exactly as they are. Replace each zone's other minigame (e.g. the D.N.A.'s "Pneumatic Soul Routing", the classroom trivia, the gym game) with the design below, at the same spot in the world.

Every new minigame:
- **Uses the game's real cards** (art, name, cost, power/toughness, Path icon), drawn from the zone's Path plus colourless cards.
- Keeps **the same rewards, unlock and replay rules** as the game it replaces.
- Opens with a **one-screen "How to play"**: three short lines and a small animated example, which can be reopened with a "?" button.
- Is **winnable on a first try by a new player** and has a clear win/lose screen with the reward.
- Has a full-screen layout on the zone's existing minigame background (MG-BG-*), or a blurred zone backdrop with a wood/brass frame like Dex's table.
- Has card flips and slides, a satisfying success effect and existing SFX.
- Works with mouse and controller, at 1280x720 and 1920x1080, with Large text.

**The designs** (the owner can adjust them; build these unless the inbox says otherwise):
1. **Gainlands (Beefcake): "Reps"**, higher or lower.
   - One card is face up. Guess whether the next card's **power** is higher or lower. Ties count as correct.
   - **8 correct in a row wins**; a miss resets the streak, with up to 3 misses allowed.
   - Visuals: a weight bench; each correct guess adds a plate to a barbell, and at 8 the hero lifts it.
2. **Endless Buffet (Gourmand): "Order Up"**, exact-cost orders.
   - A ticket shows a target total, e.g. 7. A buffet row of 6 face-up cards is on the pass.
   - Pick cards whose **costs add up exactly** to the target: 3 tickets, 2 swaps of the row allowed.
   - Visuals: a kitchen pass with tickets on a rail; chosen cards slide onto a plate; the bell rings on a correct order.
3. **Verdant Dump (Refusemancer): "Sort It Out"**, conveyor sorting.
   - Cards ride a slow conveyor. Send each into the right bin before it falls off the end: four bins, **sorted by card type** (Unit, Spell, Infrastructure, Other), with the type icons on the bins.
   - **20 cards; 16 correct wins.** It speeds up gently.
   - Visuals: a scrap-wood conveyor with four painted bins and a little squeak per sort.
4. **D.N.A. (Necrocrat): "File in Order"**, filing.
   - 7 face-up cards, scattered. Put them in a filing cabinet in **ascending cost** (ties broken by power) by swapping two at a time: **at most 9 swaps**.
   - A "stamp" button checks the order and shows which ones are out of place (one free check).
   - Visuals: a grey filing cabinet whose drawers fill, and an APPROVED stamp thump on a win.

Update the quests, feats, the glossary and any dialogue that names the old games, with the same NPC hosts. Add tests for each game's win/lose rules. Screenshot each "How to play", mid-game and win screen.
