# The owner's decisions, Nov 23 evening (they go with owner_list_nov23.md)

## The characters and the arena
- **Trey Pickett is a card shark:** a grinning shark in a loud red-and-cream striped suit and a straw boater, with a squeaky card cart and a bell. He talks like a carnival auctioneer ("Pick it, pick it, PICK IT!"). Zany, over the top, family friendly. He replaces the badger-baker portrait and the citizen sprite stand-ins.
- **Octavia Eightfold** runs the draft arena: a flamboyant octopus showwoman with a **casino vibe**, dealing with all eight arms. She's Trey's flamboyant aunt and mentor. She has big personality and showbiz patter, written in the game's voice.
- **The Shuffle House:** the draft arena in Crosspath, a **carnival-casino hybrid** building (Batch 20 art). Fable picks the spot while it rearranges Crosspath. It must be easy to see from the town square and easy to walk to.
