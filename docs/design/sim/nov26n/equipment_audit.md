# The main-game equipment and item audit (Nov 26 night, Part C) - proposals only

Brief: `docs/prompts/opus_nov26_night_v2.md`, Part C. **Only text was changed** (five pieces reworded, below); no number moved.

## 1. Every piece and item: where it is found, rarity, effect, text

The full list is `equipment_inventory.md` (generated by `tools/equipment_inventory.gd` from the game's own data: `GearCatalog.all_pieces`,
the Collection Book's source hints, the draft pool, and the text exactly as the game shows it). 81 pieces of equipment and 18 items.

**Text check.** Every description was read, and a script flagged missing full stops, empty effects, over-long lines and numbers in a
piece's rules that its words do not mention. The five script flags were false positives (a resource-kind number; "two" spelled out). Six
texts were unclear or inconsistent and were **reworded** (the one display order - name, slot and rarity, effect, flavour - is kept by
`GearText`; test `tests/test_nov26n_gear_text.gd`):

| Piece | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter Blade | "...each battle gets +1 attack." | "...each duel gets +1 attack." | every other piece says "duel" |
| Overdue Library Book | "...Then discard 1 card at random." | "...Then toss 1 card at random." | "toss" is the game's word (Shuffle Slippers, the rules) |
| Thorned Loincloth | "Max HP -2; whenever an enemy unit attacks you..." | "Max HP -2. Whenever an enemy unit attacks you..." | sentences, as on the other pieces |
| Big Brain Beret | "...one non-infrastructure card per turn, and max HP -2." | "...one non-infrastructure card per turn. Max HP -2." | the cost as its own sentence, as on every other piece |
| Extra Pocket | "...twice per battle. The first use spends one copy; the second is free..." | "...twice per duel. The first use spends one copy, and the second is free..." | "duel", and a sentence (found by the new test) |
| X-Ray Goggles | "The opponent's hand is revealed to you - it doesn't see through a face-down trap, though." | "The opponent's hand is revealed to you (its face-down traps stay hidden)." | the "it" was ambiguous |

Two pieces do the same thing under different names and wordings: **Thinking Cap** ("You draw an extra card on your first turn") and
**Traveler's Boots** ("Draw an extra card at the start of your first turn"). Not wrong; noted for the owner (decision in the report).

## 2. The pieces in fights

Three measurements, each with its limits:

- **Against the zone bosses and Primm** (`equipment_lift_a.md`, `tools/equipment_lift.gd`): each piece swapped into its own slot of the
  model gear, two reference Paths per boss (Clench, Mortimer, the Doppelganger, Compostella, Primm's first phase). Only one game per
  Path and boss fitted the night (10 games a piece, about +/-30 points), and only 33 of 81 pieces were reached: **a rough screen**.
  No piece moved the bosses by more than two wins in ten except the Draft Champion's Laurels (+3, 10/10). The model gear already wins 7
  in 10 here: the zone bosses are not where gear decides fights.
- **In the drafts** (Part B, `draft_tables.md`): 216 drafts with the gear they took. This is where pieces separate (+/-20 points over
  10-40 drafts): see the list below.
- **In the postgame** (Part D, `docs/design/postgame_strategies.md`): the kits that win the hard fights.

### Must-takes

- **Glass Jaw Gauntlets** (+2 attack, -6 max HP): +16.7 in drafts; the core of the aggro kit that beats the Warden, Siphon, Plume and the
  Perfect Copy. Its -6 HP is a real cost, and the right one.
- **Curve-Topper Boots, Elastic Waistband, Earned Blade:** the rest of the aggro kit (+13.1 and +14.6 in drafts).
- **Quota's Stopwatch** (first turn, extra card, +3 max HP): +22.2 in drafts, no drawback at all.
- **Four-Seal Signet, Flamethrower, Colonel's Ladle, Booster-Wrapper Cape:** the sturdy kit that carries the Gallery's middle (with burn).
- The Path bonuses when the deck is that Path: **Protein Shaker Bandolier** (+18.4), **Mystery-Stain Bib** (+17.3), **Pantry Apron** (+20.7).

### Dead or near-dead pieces

- **Crowd-Pleaser's Cape** (-23.1 in drafts, 17), **Red Tape Dispenser** helm (-24.1, 11), **Bin Lid Helm** (-25.7, 4), **Steady Sandals**
  (-15.2), **Pathwork Waistcoat** (-14.3, 19).
- **The Commons that only add 1-2 HP** (Cardboard Cuirass, Lucky Sock, Steady Sandals, Saucepan Helmet, Lucky Dice): never chosen over
  anything with an effect once a player has five slots of Uncommons; fine as starter gear, dead after level 10.
- **Thinking Cap / Traveler's Boots** (an extra first-turn card): fine early, outclassed by Quota's Stopwatch and the Courier's Lanyard.

### Pieces that don't work or barely do (bugs)

None found. The text-against-rules check found no mismatch, and no piece's effect showed as missing in the sims. Not covered tonight: a
piece-by-piece in-engine test of every effect (the existing gear tests cover the pieces changed since Oct 29).

### Combos that break a fight

- **The Extra Pocket with burn items.** Part D found burn (Firebrand Charms, a Sharpening Stone) to be the key to the hardest postgame
  fights; the Pocket uses each item twice, so two Firebrand Charms become four. Two paired measurements of the Pocket (draft decks with and
  without it) ran over their 15- and 20-minute limits and wrote nothing, so this is **a strong suspicion, not a measurement**. The sim's
  item policy also does not read the Pocket (it would need "each item twice" passed in, as `tools/draft_sim.gd --what=pocket` now does).
- **Hover Boots + Paper-Plane Cape** on a Necrocrat deck: both make units fly (redundant, not broken).
- Nothing else stood out.
