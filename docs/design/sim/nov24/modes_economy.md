## The first win of each (once per save)

A first full win adds 300 gold, 200 XP and a Gilded Pack at a table (Classic and High Rollers each once); the Halls' first clear adds 300 gold, 300 XP, a Gilded Pack and a piece of draft equipment. Weighted by the chance of a full win, a first Classic run is worth about 496, a first High Rollers run about 377 (after its fee), a first Halls run about 511.

## Reading it

- **High Rollers against Classic:** the fee is paid back in gold alone from about 42 % a fight; under that it is a gold sink that pays in Prismatic Packs. At the same win rate it is worth more than Classic at every result; the opponents' better equipment is what the fee buys.
- **No mode out-earns the main game's own replays by much** per hour once the draft's seven minutes are counted, so drafting is a different way to play for packs, not a faster farm. The side dungeon replay stays the gold farm of the game.
- **Prismatic Packs** come from three places only (the Dungeon Draft, High Rollers, the Halls) and only after Primm; before Primm those rewards are Gilded Packs, which the shop prices higher (400 against 350): nothing is lost by the lock.
- **Cashing out** of the Halls is worth less than half a clear and has no pack beyond a General Pack: a consolation for a run that would not survive Octavia, not a strategy.
