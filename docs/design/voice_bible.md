# Voice bible (Oct 16)

How every character in Patchwork sounds. Source for personalities: `data/source/npc_list.csv` (Personality and Voice columns); story facts:
`docs/design/story_bible.md`. Use this when writing or rewriting any player-facing line. The dialogue rewrite (old and new lines side by side)
is in `docs/design/dialogue_rewrite.md`.

## Global rules
1. **Funny and fun first.** Every box should earn a smile or move the story; most should do both.
2. **Puns and dad jokes are welcome, often.** The groan is the point. One pun per box is plenty; never stack three.
3. **Millennial references are sprinkled, never explained**: away messages, dial-up, top-8 friends, 90s infomercials, flip phones, "this meeting
   could have been an email", participation trophies, avocado toast, "per my last email", "new phone who dis", "it's giving", lo-fi beats.
   At most one per conversation, and it must make sense even if the player misses it.
4. **Short lines.** 1 to 3 sentences per box, never a wall of text. A long thought becomes two boxes. Punchlines go last in the box.
5. **Sincere beats stay sincere.** The prince's captivity, grief for the king and queen, the leader reveals (Grandmaster Flex, the Grand Chef,
   Agnes, Compostella), the memory fragments, Isla coming home and Primm's fall are played straight: no joke on top, no wink after. A gentle
   warm line is fine; a punchline is not.
6. **Names and facts never change**: card names, mechanics, numbers, places and who-did-what stay exactly as the game uses them.
7. **No readable text in the world.** Signs are pictures; their "E to read" text is the place for the words.
8. **Tokens**: `{villain}` (Primm), `{prince}`, `{town}` (Crosspath), `{capital}`, `{pathordia}`, `{wanderer}` are filled in by the game.

## Path humour
- **Beefcake** (the Gainlands): gym bro sincerity. Reps, gains, leg day, protein, spotting, "bro". Everything is a workout, and the jokes are
  big-hearted, never mean. Puns: *raise the bar, no pain no gain, pumped, swole, core values, whey too much*.
- **Necrocrat** (the D.N.A.): office comedy for the afterlife. Forms in triplicate, take a number, per my last email, death and taxes, the
  meeting that could have been a séance. Dry, deadpan, procedural. Puns: *dead tired, grave concern, final notice, ghosted, red tape*.
- **Gourmand** (the Endless Buffet): cooking-show drama and food puns. Chefs are passionate, kitchens are battlefields, everything is
  "yes, chef". Puns: *whisk it, lettuce, thyme, seasoned, half-baked, the plot thickens*.
- **Refusemancer** (the Verdant Dump): cheerful, folksy recycling and compost humour; raccoons are royalty. Gentle, earthy, a little gross.
  Puns: *rot-ten luck, trash talk, compost-ure, one man's trash, waste not*.
- **The Capital**: Primm's perfection is the joke - relentlessly upbeat PR language over obvious misery; the citizens' cracks are where the
  sadness shows.

## Characters (2-3 sample lines each)

### The town (Crosspath)
- **The Wanderer** (short, wry inner monologue; earnest when it counts): "My name is... no. Still nothing. Great start." / "Somebody has
  to fix this. Apparently it's the damp young man."
- **Elder Maren** (grumbly, slow, calls everyone "dearie"): "Sit down, dearie. You're dripping on my pies." / "That's no prince. That's a
  damp young man with good posture." / (sincere) "I knew. I think I always knew. Forgive an old woman for being afraid."
- **Gatekeeper Brannoch** (short grunts, then a tender sentence): "Hm. Road's open. Mind the potholes." / "Gate has no lock. People need
  to be able to come home."
- **Sable** (smooth, theatrical card dealer): "Every card has a story, darling. Some of them even happened." / "Misprints are just
  cards with personality."
- **Tilly Tonic** (fast, bubbly, exclamation points): "Hi! Hello! Everything's tested! On me! My hair is purple today!" / "That one's
  for healing! Mostly!"
- **Bertram Beetsworth** (clipped, dead-serious, beets and bears): "Question. Do you have adequate armour? Wrong. You do not." / "Fact:
  a bear can't wear a helmet. You can. Buy the helmet."
- **Pip Threadwell** (grand, dramatic fashion): "Gasp. That cloak. Who did this to you?" / "Every hat tells a story. Yours is a
  tragedy. Let's write a sequel."
- **Foil Fenwick** (breathless, nerdy, whispers "foil"): "Smell that? That's fresh pack. That's... foil." / "If you pull a shiny I need
  you to tell me. I need it."
- **Auntie Alembic** (soft, mystical, unexpectedly blunt): "The Paths were happiest mixed, dear. Like soup. Or a family." / "That
  flask is listening. Be nice."
- **Announcer Grand Bellows** (LOUD, everything is an announcement): "IN THIS CORNER... YOU! WITH A DECK!" / "THE CROWD GOES MILD! No,
  wait, WILD! Sorry. Low ceiling. Echoes."
- **Old Hob the Gravedigger** (creepy-cheerful, ends on a chuckle): "Hm. Not on my list. Off you pop. Heh." / "Just measuring. Don't mind
  me. Heh heh."
- **Brannoch's daughter Isla** (tired, warm, embarrassed): "Hi, Dad. I know. I should have written." (played straight)

### The gatekeepers (corrupted, then freed)
- **Brick Bronson, the Muscley Gatekeeper** (corrupted: barking slogans; freed: gentle, apologetic, loves stretching): "NOBODY CROSSES
  WITHOUT A SPOTTER. I AM THE SPOTTER." / Freed: "Sorry about the yelling. And the bridge. Want to stretch first? Hamstrings are friends."
- **Prudence Pallor, Checkpoint Supervisor** (corrupted: monotone policy; freed: dry humour): "Permit required. Permits are not issued.
  Please take a number. It is zero." / Freed: "Here. Your permit. I filed the original in triplicate. Old habits die hard. Unlike me."
- **Chef Fennel Gravois, Keeper of the Pantry Key** (corrupted: manic, pushy; freed: dramatic, warm): "TASTE IT. For the war effort.
  TASTE IT." / Freed: "The key. Take it. And take a biscuit. You look underseasoned."
- **Moss Mulligan, the Corrupted Druid** (corrupted: whispery, overlapping; freed: soft, nervous, loves raccoons): "nothing goes to
  waste... NOTHING goes to waste... leave..." / Freed: "Oh no. Did I thorn at you? I'm so sorry. Please say hi to the raccoons for me."

## Line length check
Dialogue boxes fit about 3 lines of 70 characters at the default text size and 2-3 lines at the large size; every line is checked by
`tests/test_dialogue_fit.gd` (Block 4e) against both sizes.
