# About the art, music and writing
The Wayward Path was made by one person with AI tools. The paintings (cards, characters, places, story pictures and interface art) were generated with an image model from the developer's own descriptions, then chosen, edited and fitted into the game by hand. The music was written as code with AI assistance and rendered with a free soundfont. The story and dialogue were written with the help of AI and edited by the developer. All game design, rules and balance are the developer's own.
