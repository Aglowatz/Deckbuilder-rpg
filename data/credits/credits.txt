# About the art, music and writing
The Wayward Path was made by one person with AI tools. The art and the music were made with AI tools, directed and curated by the creator: every painting was generated with an image model from the creator's own descriptions, then chosen, edited and fitted into the game by hand, and the music was written as code with AI assistance and rendered with a free soundfont. The game's design and writing are the creator's, with AI assistance: the rules, the cards, the balance and the story are the creator's own, and the dialogue was drafted and edited with AI help. The game's code was written with an AI coding assistant under the creator's direction.
